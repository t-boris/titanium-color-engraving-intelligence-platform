## Decision

The region's pixels (the split rasterised from its outline, made one piece with the holes of its outline only) are clustered into the chosen number of colours at the finest superpixel size, sampled a pixel in from the border; a border pixel takes the colour class of the nearest inner pixel, so a neighbour's colour caught by the raster never makes a strip along the outline, and colours closer than ΔE 2 are one. A part is at least half an even share of the region (never more than the work's minimum area, never less than four finest superpixels), so a small region can be split too; smaller parts merge into the neighbouring part of the nearest colour. The new borders inside are traced from the pixels as the split's arcs are and kept inside the outline. The region's own arcs are only cut where an inner border meets them (at the nearest point of the traced arc), so every neighbour keeps its traced border point for point (to the 5 decimals points are kept at) and the picture stays watertight; with smoothing on, a cut border is smoothed per piece like any border between junctions, so near a new junction it moves by a fraction of the smoothing (measured on «Luna 1» at 0.5 mm: at most 0.24 mm). A part whose outline would cross itself is refused rather than saved (a loop touching itself at a corner, as the split's own loops do, is fine); on «Luna 1» 1 of 200 splits was refused so (a spike thinner than 0.02 mm, at 8 colours; fewer colours split it). The largest part keeps the region's id, place and titanium colour chosen by hand; the parts of its colour keep the region's palette colour, each other colour is one new palette colour (indexes appended) that takes the nearest titanium colour (closest), everything else in the colouring untouched. A split needing more palette colours than the layers allow is refused with how many fit; new colours can still need more LightBurn layers than exist (recipes, zones and inscriptions count), and the export then says so as after any colouring step. An excluded region's parts are excluded; a region in a photo zone splits into parts in that zone. The step is a new version of the split that undo brings back; variants of the earlier split stay on it.

## Reason

Splitting the whole picture again with the region marked finer renumbers every region, loses merges and starts the colouring over, and with the colour centres kept it mostly splits nothing (the region is one class by definition). Re-tracing the whole picture from the rasterised outline would move every border a little and erode thin features on each round. Cutting only the region's arcs keeps the operator's work and the shared borders exact.
