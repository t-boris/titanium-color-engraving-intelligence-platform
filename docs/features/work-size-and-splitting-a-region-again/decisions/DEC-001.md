## Decision

«Вписать» writes a new version of the split document in which every arc point, the size, the millimetres per pixel, the minimum area and the tolerance are scaled by the same factor; the regions, their ids, the palette, the image pixels and the important areas are untouched. The plan's size and markers follow, inscriptions are scaled with lettering.scaled, and each photo zone is settled at its new width. The step is undoable: undo and redo set the plan's size and markers from the restored document.

## Reason

A re-split at a new width with the same settings gives the same raster (the minimum area is a share of the picture, superpixels work in pixels), but it renumbers the regions, loses merges and starts the colouring over. Scaling the arcs is exact, instant and keeps every hand choice, which is what the operator asked for.
