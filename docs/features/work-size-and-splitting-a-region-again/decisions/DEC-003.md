## Decision

A size change leaves the work's smoothing (a strength in mm along each shared border, DEC-083 of vector-detail-and-colour-control) as it is; the slider shows what it says and the laser's lines keep their rounding in mm.

## Reason

Smoothing is a property of the engraved lines, not of the picture; scaling it would move the operator's slider silently and would have to be clamped at its end.
