## Statement

The operator can give a saved photo work a new width or a new height in millimetres; the other side follows the picture's proportions. The work stays the same work: the same regions with the same ids, merges, colours chosen by hand or automatically, exclusions, variants, inscriptions (scaled with the picture, their gap in mm kept) and photo zones (their dots re-taken at the new width). The change is a step of the work's history that undo brings back, markers included.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Given a photo work of 150 × 200 mm, setting its height to 150 mm makes it 112.5 × 150 mm; setting its width to 100 mm makes it 100 × 133.3 mm.
- [x] After the change every region id, palette colour and chosen titanium colour is as before; every arc point is scaled by the same factor; the file's layers cover the new size.
- [x] Inscriptions keep their place and size on the picture; a photo zone keeps its regions and its dots are re-taken at the zone's new width.
- [x] Undo brings the former size, geometry and markers back; redo brings the new one; a colouring variant made before the change can still be restored after it.
- [x] Both sides given, no side, a size that is not a positive number, or the size the work already has are refused with a message; a work that is not a photo work is refused.
