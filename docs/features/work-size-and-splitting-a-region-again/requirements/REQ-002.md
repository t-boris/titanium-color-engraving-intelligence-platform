## Statement

With one region selected in the editor, the operator can split it again: the region's picture is divided into a chosen number of colours (2 to 8) at the finest detail, and each connected part becomes a region of its own. Every other region keeps its traced geometry exactly, shared borders included; the parts of the region's own colour keep that colour, each other colour of the parts is one new colour of the palette that takes the nearest titanium colour, the rest of the colouring untouched; the region's exclusion and its photo zone carry to all its parts, a titanium colour chosen by hand for the region stays on its largest part. A split that would need more colours than LightBurn's layers allow is refused with how many fit. The step can be undone.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Splitting a region of a gradient picture into 3 colours gives at least 2 regions in its place; every other region keeps its traced arcs, pixels and colour, and the palette grows by at most 2 colours.
- [x] Rasterised uncovered and doubly covered areas stay below 0.5 % after the split; the union of the parts is the former region (traced). With smoothing on, a cut border is smoothed per piece like every border between junctions: it moves only near the new junctions, by a fraction of the smoothing strength.
- [x] A region in a photo zone splits into parts that are all in that zone; an excluded region's parts are excluded; a hand-chosen titanium colour stays on the largest part.
- [x] A region that is one colour at that detail is refused with a message saying so; a number of colours outside 2..8, or more new colours than the layers left, is refused with a message.
- [x] Undo brings the former region back with its colouring.
