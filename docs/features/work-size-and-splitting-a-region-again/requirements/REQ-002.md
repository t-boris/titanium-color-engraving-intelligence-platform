## Statement

With one region selected in the editor, the operator can split it again: the region's picture is divided into a chosen number of colours (2 to 6) at the finest detail, and each connected part becomes a region of its own. Every other region keeps its geometry exactly, shared borders included; the new regions' colours join the palette and are coloured by the work's current mode; the region's colour chosen by hand, its exclusion and its photo zone carry to all its parts. The step can be undone.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Splitting a region of a gradient picture into 3 colours gives at least 2 regions in its place and no region elsewhere changes its arcs, pixels or colour.
- [ ] Rasterised uncovered and doubly covered areas stay below 0.5 % after the split; the parts' outer border is the former region's border point for point.
- [ ] A region in a photo zone splits into parts that are all in that zone; an excluded region's parts are excluded; a hand-chosen titanium colour stays on the largest part.
- [ ] A region that is one colour at that detail is refused with a message saying so; more than one selected region is refused.
- [ ] Undo brings the former region back with its colouring.
