## Statement

The operator gives the plate's width and height and moves a frame of that proportion over the picture; the work becomes the part of the picture inside the frame at the plate's size. Regions outside the frame leave the work, regions crossing it are clipped, inscriptions and photo zones move with the frame. The step can be undone.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A 3:4 picture cut to 150 × 150 mm gives a work of 150 × 150 mm showing the part of the picture inside the frame.
- [ ] Regions wholly outside the frame are gone; regions crossing it are clipped at the frame; the file's layers cover only the plate.
- [ ] Inscriptions and photo zones inside the frame keep their place on the picture; those left outside are dropped with a notice.
- [ ] Undo brings the whole picture back.
