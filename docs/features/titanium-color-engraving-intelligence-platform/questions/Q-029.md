## Question

Should fill layers (Scan shapes, text, the cleaning rectangle) also find the work at the scale of the ruler, as pictures do?

## Why it matters

The operator said the ruler and the angle can say where to look for any sector of fills, and that finding any one layer is enough. REQ-055 searches pictures only; a vector-only work whose outlines are ambiguous would still need four corners. Rasterising the largest fill layers into templates would reuse the same search, at a cost of about 8 s per layer, and needs a real vector work that fails today to be validated against: none of the 19 other real pairs does.

## Options

- **A**: Not now: wait for a real vector work that fails to align.
- **B**: Yes: add the four largest fill layers as templates.

## Recommended

A
