## Question

What are the focal length of the F-theta lens, the real spot diameter on the titanium and the pulse width you want as the profile constant for colour marking?

## Why it matters

With the spot diameter the system can report fluence (J/cm2) and pulse overlap, the two quantities that govern the oxide layer thickness and so the colour. Without it only pulse energy and areal dose are available. The existing profile uses 200 ns, which is a valid step of this laser, but the choice has not been confirmed.
