## Question

Should flat-fill points stay in the curve of a photo-engraving mode, given that they disagree with the photo engravings' tonal bins by up to 39 ΔE?

## Why it matters

The curve of Girl on a bike and Motorcycle mixes three engravings: two photo engravings and the Power × Speed plate. At the same power a flat fill (10.56 %) reads L 98.3 b 34.8, the two photo bins (10.98 %) read L 72.6 b 24.2. The preview now says that 52.5 % of the Girl picture lies at powers where engravings differ by more than 5 ΔE; light tones (5-14 %) disagree by 14-40 ΔE, dark tones (23-29 %) by 2-6 ΔE.

## Options

- **Keep both**: Keep flat fills and tonal bins in one curve and show the disagreement (today's behaviour)
- **Photo only**: Build a photo-mode curve from tonal bins only, and use flat fills only for fills
- **Offer both**: Show two predictions side by side, one from each kind of evidence

## Recommended

Photo only: the data show the two kinds of evidence give different colours at the same power, and the cause is not known; changing the curve construction needs the operator's yes
