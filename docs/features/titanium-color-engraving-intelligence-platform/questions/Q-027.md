## Question

In grayscale mode, does the laser fire at Min power on pure white, and does a path shape sitting on an Image layer (Lana Portrait C17, 12 outlines; girl art deco, 8) engrave anything?

## Why it matters

DEC-095 assumes white is struck at Min (documentation and forum reports, not a measurement) and that plain shapes on an Image layer strike nothing. Both change which fills count as engraved again. The real photographs show no step at pure white.

## Options

- **Ramp plate**: Engrave a test plate: a grey ramp in grayscale with Min above 0, and a transparent hole, so the photograph settles both
- **Keep assumptions**: Keep the assumptions and name them on the screens

## Recommended

Ramp plate: it needs an Image-layer writer for the LightBurn file, so it needs the operator's yes before it is built
