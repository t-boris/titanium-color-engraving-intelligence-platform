## Question

May the predicted look of a photo engraving be built on camera-relative measurements (photographs without a ColorChecker, as every pair so far), flagged as such, or must the screen refuse until a referenced measurement exists at that mode?

## Why it matters

Today the only photo-engraving evidence (Inna) is camera-relative and the photograph was dark (L* 1.4..7); a referenced-only rule means no prediction at all until a ColorChecker is obtained, while a flagged prediction already shows the tonal relation (which greys come out lighter, browner, darker) and improves as plates are photographed.

## Options

- **Flagged camera-relative**: Predict from camera-relative colours when nothing referenced exists; the preview states that the colours are as that camera saw them under that light
- **Referenced only**: Refuse until a referenced measurement (photograph with a chart) exists at the mode; the screen says which plate to photograph

## Recommended

Flagged camera-relative
