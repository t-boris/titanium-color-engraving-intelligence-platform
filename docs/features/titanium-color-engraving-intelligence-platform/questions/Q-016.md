## Question

What counts as an independent repetition toward the 'repeated' and 'validated' stages? REQ-001, REQ-005 and DEC-016 require one shared repetition-independence criterion but do not define it. The prototype counts distinct `pieceId` values.

## Why it matters

Automatic promotion up to 'validated' (DEC-006, plan I-6) cannot be implemented without a definition of an independent repetition.

## Options

- **A**: Each separate physical engraving counts, on a new piece or a new area of the same sheet; a re-photograph never counts.
- **B**: Only an engraving on a new physical piece counts (prototype `pieceId` rule).
- **C**: Each separate engraving counts, and the repetitions must also span at least two known sheets or batches.

## Recommended

A — Counts real physical repeats while keeping the link to the sheet; a re-photograph is rig repeatability, not a repetition of the engraving.

## Answer

A. Each separate physical engraving counts, whether on a new piece or a new area of the same sheet. The sheet link is kept for context. A re-photograph of the same engraving never counts.
