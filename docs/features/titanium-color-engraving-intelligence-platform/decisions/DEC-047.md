## Context

An operator needs to remove a mistaken or unwanted LightBurn/photo pair. Accepted measurements are otherwise immutable and may have influenced learned knowledge, so physical row deletion would erase provenance and violate the observation invariant.

## Decision

Deletion marks the pair `Deleted`, removes its uploaded file copies, and hides it from normal pair lists and direct retrieval. Its immutable observation rows remain for audit but are excluded from learning, color maps, reports, and device experiment results. Rebuild derived knowledge after deletion.

A plan with only deleted pairs can also be removed from active experiment lists. In that case the plan and plate are marked `Deleted` and retained as the historical context for those pair records. Active pairs still prevent plan removal; a plan with no linked pairs can be physically deleted with its plate.

## Consequences

Accepted pairs can be removed from active use in the experiments list or pair review. Confirmation explains that their measurements leave active recommendations while the immutable history remains in audit storage. Plans linked only to deleted pairs disappear from the active list while retaining their pair and measurement history. Existing original files outside the application's upload directory are not touched.
