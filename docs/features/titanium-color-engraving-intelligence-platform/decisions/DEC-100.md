## Context

Q-026 asked whether fills can predict grayscale photo engraving. On the operator's Power x Speed plate (pair 95) the fill row has exactly the settings of grayscale Mode A (150 mm/s, 60 kHz, 300 ns, 0.0169 mm). Against the grayscale pixels of Motorcycle and Girl on a bike at the same power: dark (P 24-29) dE 1.4-6.2, middle (P 16-22) 15-21, light and gold (P 5-15) 6-36; no power factor explains it (best x1.1 still 12-17). The operator: the grayscale dot is much smaller than a fill's, the line interval matters.

## Alternatives

1. Use fills as points of the curve (refuted by pair 95)
2. Correct fills by a power factor (best factor still leaves 12-17 dE)

## Decision

The power -> colour curve of a grayscale scale is built only from grayscale Image engravings: the tonal profiles of photographs and the steps of grey ramps (each step measured by its own geometry). Fills with the same settings are listed beside the scale with their dE to the curve, never as points. A grayscale Image layer's median is not a colour-map point when its tonal steps exist.

## Reason

Fills disagree with grayscale pictures by up to 36 dE at the same settings and power; mixing them would put wrong colours into the prediction the operator checks against his own engravings.

## Consequences

A new scale needs grayscale engravings: the grey-ramp plate (REQ-058) makes them 16 steps per tile. Answers Q-026.
