## Context

The operator asked (2026-10-18) to understand colour physically: there is no pigment, the colour comes from a thin titanium-oxide film whose thickness sets the interference, and to predict thickness from the engraving settings and colour from thickness, and to propose settings for a wanted colour. A direct settings-to-colour regression learns a spiral badly and cannot extrapolate.

## Alternatives

1. Learn settings -> colour directly from observations (black box): needs many more points, cannot extrapolate, no physical check
2. Pure interference film: explains the coloured regimes but not the dark neutral 'black' regimes (21-23 dE off)
3. Interference film plus a share of absorbing dark oxide, fitted per observation, with global optical parameters per setup (chosen)

## Decision

`oxide.py` computes the reflectance of air / TiO2 film / titanium by the Fresnel equations at the capture angle, converts it to Lab (D65, CIE 1931 2 deg) and mixes it by area with a flat dark-oxide reflectance of 4 %. Global parameters (film index scale, film absorption, gloss, angle) are fitted on every chart-corrected observation by minimising the median residual of the coloured regimes (chroma >= 8); per observation the film thickness and dark share are read off a grid (0-400 nm, 2 nm; dark share 0-0.9), with any other interference order that fits within 3 dE reported as an alternative; a colour with 60 % dark oxide or more does not determine its thickness. `GET /oxide/model` gives the parameters, the spiral and every observation's reading; `GET /oxide/kinetics` the thickness against the dose of the settings with a power law and its r2, usable only above r2 0.5; `GET /colors/settings` the thickness candidates for a wanted colour, the measured settings that produced such films, and the kinetics law when usable. The colour map draws the spiral under the points, in any of the three Lab planes with a lightness band, shows the film reading of a picked point and the film card for a wanted colour.

## Reason

On the 43 calibrated observations the fitted model (index scale 1.0, absorption 0.05, gloss 1.0, angle 0) reaches a median residual of 3.6 dE on the 32 coloured regimes (goal 5), 70 % of all points within 5 dE; the 11 dark regimes are dark oxide, not film. 25 points admit a second interference order within 3 dE: the spiral loops, and finished works give no dose ordering. Kinetics on this set: r2 0.07, no usable law; the works mix pulse regimes and areal energy alone does not order the thicknesses.

## Consequences

The model's optical constants are published values, not measured on this metal; the capture angle is one value per photograph and the stand should fix it. The next evidence is plates with a monotone dose ramp at one pulse width and frequency, photographed with the chart: they order the points along the spiral, settle the interference order and give the kinetics its law; until then the settings card offers measured films only, never a computed dose. The research questions of REQ-030 (predicted look of a photo engraving) can be answered through thickness once the kinetics holds.
