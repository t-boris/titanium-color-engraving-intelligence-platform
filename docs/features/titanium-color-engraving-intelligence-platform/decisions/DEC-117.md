## Context

The operator, 2026-10-23, on «Inna Portrait-P1-30 150»: the look shown before engraving was much darker than the result and missed the yellows; after accepting results the platform must rework its colours and settings so that the next prediction is closer. The operator chose four parts: a review of each acceptance, aligning photographs, the work's own plate, trust in a prediction. The mode 150 mm/s, 60 kHz, 350 ns, 0.0212 mm was measured three times: grey ramps with 38.4 mm rows on Big Plate 150 (3 mm), cleaned (pair 108); a portrait with 100 mm rows on Small Plate (2 mm), not cleaned (109); a portrait with 150 mm rows on Big Plate 150, cleaned (111). Before 111 the prediction at 150 mm took the nearest size measured (109): median 18.7 dE, max 59.5, 2 of 16 tones within 5 dE, lighter by 17 L*. The clean titanium of the same plate and cleaning reads L* 79.4 on the ramps' photograph and 91.2 on the portrait's. Nothing showed this to the operator: the scale's confirmation compares only engravings at a measured size and against all others, not what was predicted then.

## Alternatives

1. Store the prediction with each photo work's export (as DEC-090 does for vector works): faithful, but nothing for the photographs already accepted, and a better model could not be measured on them
2. Compare against every other engraving (the scale's confirmation): measures the data now, not what the operator was told, and refuses sizes outside the measured ones, which is exactly the case that went wrong
3. Attribute the error to causes by a fitted model: too few engravings to fit; a wrong attribution would steer the next changes

## Decision

prediction_review.review(store, pair_id) sets each tonal layer of an accepted photograph beside the curve of its mode made from the engravings known BEFORE it, at its own row length, as the photo work showed it: prediction.curve_at, the nearest size included when it lies outside the measured ones, never extrapolated. An engraving is known from the first acceptance of any photograph of it, so a remeasurement or a deleted and re-accepted photograph keeps its place. The curve uses one kind of colour, as curve_for chooses it (chart-corrected first, then against clean titanium); a photograph of the other kind is not compared. At each power inside the curve it gives the colour predicted and measured, dE (CIE76), the median and maximum, the share within 5 dE, and the median shifts of L* and b*. It names what the prediction rested on, as facts with their numbers and nothing weighed: size (measured, between, outside, unknown, with the sizes); plate (the plates and their thickness); cleaning (whether each file cleans, from the check's composition or from the file); photo. The photo fact compares the clean titanium of this photograph with that of the prediction's photographs and of the other photographs of the same plate and cleaning. The reading is the cleaning witness, else the lightest tone when it looks like clean titanium by ingest's rule. Readings 3 L* apart or more differ; on the same plate and cleaning that difference is the photograph's light. summary(store) gathers every review, cached per data state. It gives the median dE of every tone compared, and the same for layers whose size was measured beforehand. It is the measure that any later change of the model (photo alignment, own plate) has to lower. The experiment's «Результат» tab shows the card «Прогноз и результат» for an accepted photo engraving, loaded after the rest. API: GET /api/pairs/{id}/prediction-review and GET /api/prediction-reviews.

## Reason

The operator cannot correct what is not shown. Recomputing from the engravings known then is what the photo work showed at the time. It works for every photograph already accepted without storing a promise, and with a later model it says what that model would have predicted from the same data. Facts with numbers, not attributed shares, keep a guess from convicting the wrong cause. One measure across every review lets a model change be kept only when it makes predictions better.

## Consequences

The first engraving of a mode says there was no prediction. The 1-to-3 L* readings use ingest's clean titanium rule (L* at least 50, chroma at most 15) on the lightest tone, which is the picture's white at Min. Baseline on the operator's data, 2026-10-23: 3 layers reviewed (pairs 88, 109, 111), median 13.8 dE over 47 tones; 10.2 dE where the size was measured beforehand (88 against 93, same plate and cleaning, its photograph 9.2 L* lighter). The cold first call reads the colour references, about 1.6 s once per process; warm calls take about 0.03 s.
