## Context

F-001 identifies a contradiction: REQ-005 and REQ-006 retain a legacy/uncontrolled tier and exclude its observations from validation repetitions, while accepted DEC-015 establishes a universal observation input and removes that separate tier.

## Alternatives

1. Apply DEC-015 by removing the legacy/uncontrolled tier and its special restrictions without adding shared observation-quality criteria.
2. Restore a separate legacy/uncontrolled tier and revise DEC-015 and dependent requirements accordingly.
3. Apply DEC-015 and make repetition eligibility depend on common observation-quality and repetition-independence criteria for all sources.

## Decision

Apply DEC-015 and replace source-specific restrictions with common eligibility criteria. Observations from system-generated calibration plates, finished engravings and imported works shall be assessed under the same observation-quality and repetition-independence criteria. Whether the system created the work shall not determine eligibility for validation repetitions.

## Reason

The team selected a unified quality-based approach. This preserves the accepted universal ingestion workflow while allowing validation eligibility to depend on the quality of evidence rather than the origin of the work.

## Consequences

Remove the legacy/uncontrolled tier and its validation-repetition exclusions from REQ-005 and REQ-006. Mark DEC-012 explicitly as superseded by DEC-015. Preserve the universal LightBurn-file-and-rig-photograph ingestion workflow. Define shared observation-quality and repetition-independence criteria separately before implementing automatic validation; this decision does not establish specific thresholds.
