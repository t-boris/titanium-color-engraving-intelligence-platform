## Context

REQ-002 requires every predicted parameter set to carry a confidence, an expected color error and a stability indicator. REQ-005 requires the stages measured, candidate, repeated, validated and production with a configurable repetition threshold, and says the quality and independence criteria must be defined before automatic validation. DEC-021 defines an independent repetition. Nothing defines the three estimates or the thresholds between the lower stages.

## Alternatives

1. Reuse the prototype's demonstration formulas (confidence = min(0.96, 0.45 + 0.08 n), expected error 6 without a variance estimate).
2. Refuse every prediction until the user defines the formulas.

## Decision

Knowledge is the set of eligible observations that share one parameter vector within one laser profile version. Observations of the same engraving, for example re-photographs, are first averaged into one value per engraving.

- Stability: with two or more engravings, the total variance of Lab across engravings (the sum of the sample variances of L, a and b, in delta E squared units); otherwise "not assessed".
- Expected color error: the square root of that total variance plus the mean delta E of repeated photographs from their engraving's mean (zero when no engraving was re-photographed). Not available when stability is not assessed.
- Confidence: n / (n + 2), with n the number of independent engravings; between 0 and 1.
- A prediction is emitted only when all three are available. Otherwise the system answers "not enough data for a prediction".
- Coverage: a requested color is outside the measured space when any Lab component lies beyond the minimum or maximum of the available samples.
- Automatic stages, only when the evaluation criteria have been agreed and the repetition threshold T, the delta E tolerance and the variance threshold are saved: measured for any eligible observation; candidate for more than one observation; repeated for at least 2 independent engravings; validated for at least max(2, T) independent engravings with total variance not above the variance threshold and every engraving within the delta E tolerance of the mean. Without agreed criteria nothing is promoted beyond measured.
- An automatic action never lowers a stage. A production entry whose later observations break the variance threshold or the tolerance is flagged as questioned and keeps its stage.

## Reason

These are the simplest estimates that use only measured data, are defined for any number of repeats and never claim certainty from a single observation. The stage thresholds follow the prototype's rule shape without its demonstration numbers: T, the tolerance and the variance threshold stay operator settings with no default.

## Consequences

Proposed only. Confidence n / (n + 2) is an arbitrary shrinkage curve, the variance estimate needs at least two engravings, and the lower-stage rules (candidate, repeated) are not backed by any requirement. The user may replace any of these; the formulas live in one module.
