## Context



## Alternatives

1. Draw every layer that is off in every check: in a file whose off layers were never engraved the outlines would claim marks that are not there; the evidence of pictures found is what allows it.
2. Draw only the layers whose pictures were found: the text fills, which carry the best evidence of the alignment, would stay invisible.
3. Measure the layers that are off once they are found: their file settings may differ from what was engraved (open question, not decided).

## Decision

The overlay skips a layer flagged left_out, except a layer flagged output_off when the stored alignment carries `located` (the work was found by pictures, REQ-055): that layer is outlined where the shared transform puts it, in the colour (255, 200, 40) (BGR, a light blue), thin like the rest, and it is not measured. A layer left behind is never drawn. After a work is found by pictures the check replaces the note \"this layer was not engraved\" of the layers that are off: a layer whose picture was found says that its picture is on the plate (engraved earlier), another says that it is not measured and where it is drawn, and the warning \"they were not engraved\" is removed.

## Reason

The operator said that finding one layer places all the others and, after 0.17.1, saw one frame where three pictures had been found. The photograph of pair 105 shows every one of those layers engraved, so the sentence \"not engraved\" was false for it, and drawing only the measured layer hid the proof that the alignment is right. The outlines of two text layers (97,649 and 25,975 outline pixels) have the highest edge strength at no shift, 1 to 2 pixels (0.05 to 0.1 mm) at most. Not measuring them keeps REQ-050: the settings written in the file for a layer that is off may not be the ones it was engraved with.

## Consequences

This narrows REQ-050's \"a layer left out is not drawn on the overlay\" to layers left behind and to layers that are off in a check that was not found by pictures. The stored check of pair 105 is not touched: the overlay is redrawn from it, and the notes of its layers are corrected by the next check.
