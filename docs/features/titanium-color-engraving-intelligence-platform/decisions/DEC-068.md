## Context

Implementing REQ-030 (with DEC-062) and the honest search of REQ-029/REQ-020 on 2026-10-18 required choices the specification does not make: what exactly a "mode" is, which camera-relative colours feed a curve, how picture adjustments meet the measured bins, how the share outside the span is counted and how a search entry counts its evidence.

## Alternatives

1. Treat global passes as job context outside the mode
2. Mix colours expressed against clean titanium with colours as photographed in one curve
3. Count the share outside the span on the preview resolution

## Decision

A mode of the prediction is laser, lens, titanium stock, speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval, passes and global passes; its curve takes the grey-to-power-to-colour bins of accepted, clear tonal profiles of grayscale photo engravings and accepted flat fills (Scan layers) with exactly those settings; service layers, image-layer medians, weak profiles, checks awaiting acceptance and deleted pairs never feed it. Among camera-relative points, colours expressed against the clean titanium of their photograph are preferred to colours as photographed, and the two are not mixed. A bin's power goes through the file's gamma, contrast and brightness exactly as the rendered picture does. The share outside the measured span is counted on the whole picture, not on the preview. The preview is drawn upright as LightBurn shows it (its y axis points up); mirrors and quarter turns of the file are applied, other rotations are named and not applied. Overrides apply to every image layer of the file. The colour-map search keeps one entry per recipe of one laser, lens, titanium stock, way of measuring (chart, against clean titanium, as photographed) and provisional state; it reports independent engravings (a re-photograph counts once, DEC-021) separately from photographs. Checked but unaccepted pairs are shown as provisional and are searched only on request.

## Reason

Each rule keeps a predicted or searched colour tied to measurements of the same physical job and never mixes evidence of different reference.

## Consequences

Proposed for the operator's review. The accepted Inna pair rendered through its own curve reproduces its 13 bins within 0.17 dE; 5.49 % of the picture lies outside the measured power span.
