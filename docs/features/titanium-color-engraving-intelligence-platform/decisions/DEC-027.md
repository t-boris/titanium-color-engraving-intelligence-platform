## Context

REQ-003 and REQ-013 require registration markers and a machine-readable identifier, engraved with the grid for plates and optional outside the image area for other plans. No requirement fixes the marker format, physical size or the laser settings used to engrave them. The markers must be engraved, so they need a power, frequency and speed of their own.

## Alternatives

1. A custom marker design instead of standard ArUco/QR.
2. Default marker-layer laser settings supplied by the system.

## Decision

Markers are four 4x4 ArUco markers (ids 0 to 3) placed outside the grid at its four corners, and a QR code carrying `TCE1:<experiment or plan identifier>`. Both are written as filled rectangles on one dedicated marker layer that is excluded from observations. The marker layer's power, frequency and speed are an explicit operator setting with no default, because they decide how the marker burns into the titanium and the specification gives no value. Plate generation and marker export are blocked until they are set. Marker and QR sizes are configurable with documented starting values.

## Reason

ArUco and QR are standard, OpenCV detects both, and the detection can be tested by rasterising the exported geometry. Not guessing marker laser settings avoids a plate that cannot be engraved legibly or that damages the workpiece.

## Consequences

Proposed only. Whether the engraved markers have enough contrast on titanium to be detected in a real photograph is a physical question that has not been verified. The marker layer is not a calibration observation and never enters the color-space model.
