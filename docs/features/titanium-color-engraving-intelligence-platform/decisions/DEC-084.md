## Context

Operator, 2026-10-20, on the 0.12.0 colour map: the circles all looked the same size (most recipes were engraved once; a linear scale of the confidence made two agreeing engravings 7 px against 9 px). Operator's rule: on agreement enlarge by a factor 1.5 up to a maximum size, on a difference reduce by a factor 1.5, within limits. On the operator's data 23 of 555 settings were engraved more than once (46 of 512 under the loosest key), so most circles stay at the base size. DEC-083 item 7 described the first scale (radius 3 + 12 × confidence).

## Alternatives

1. Radius linear in the confidence (0.12.0): two agreeing engravings differ from one by 2 px, not visible (rejected by the operator's look at the screen)
2. Radius by the square of the confidence (0.12.1): visible, but not the rule the operator stated

## Decision

The radius of a circle is 4 px for a setting engraved once. Every further engraving that agrees with the agreed colour multiplies it by 1.5, every engraving that differs divides it by 1.5: radius = 4 × 1.5^(agreeing − 1 − differing), limited to 2 px at the least and 14 px at the most (agreeing and differing as counted by DEC-083). The tooltip and the legend say it. Larger circles are drawn under smaller ones. This replaces item 7 of DEC-083; the confidence number itself (k²/((k+2)·n)) is unchanged and still shown.

## Reason

It is the operator's own rule, and it is easy to read off the map: a circle half as large again means one more agreeing engraving. The squared-confidence scale of 0.12.1 was my own guess and is replaced.

## Consequences

The base size 4, the factor 1.5 and the limits 2 and 14 are constants of the colour map screen; there is no setting for them. Four agreeing engravings reach 13.5 px and a fifth is held at 14.
