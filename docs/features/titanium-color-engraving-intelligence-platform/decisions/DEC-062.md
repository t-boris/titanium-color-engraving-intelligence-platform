## Context

Q-025 asked whether REQ-030 may predict from camera-relative measurements (photographs without a ColorChecker), as every pair so far is, or must refuse until a referenced measurement exists at that mode. The operator answered on 2026-10-18: "Flagged camera-relative".

## Alternatives

1. Referenced only: refuse until a photograph with a chart exists at that mode and name the plate to photograph

## Decision

When no referenced (chart-corrected) measurement exists at a mode, the prediction of REQ-030 is built from camera-relative colours of that mode and the preview states, on the picture itself, that the colours are as that camera saw them under that light. Referenced points are always preferred over camera-relative ones at the same mode; the two are never mixed in one curve. Where a clean-titanium witness exists the camera-relative colour is expressed against it (DEC-056) before it is used.

## Reason

The operator chose it: the tonal relation (which greys come out lighter, browner, darker) is already visible in camera-relative photographs and the prediction improves as charted plates are photographed.

## Consequences

REQ-030 is unblocked. Every curve point carries referenced or camera-relative; a camera-relative prediction is never used as a standard colour recommendation (DEC-053) and never enters the colour model.
