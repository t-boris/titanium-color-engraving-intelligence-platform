## Context

Closing the loop of REQ-013, the deletion rule of DEC-047/DEC-066, the capture setup of REQ-009/DEC-025, restored plans of REQ-005/006 and the target check of REQ-006 on 2026-10-18 required choices the specification does not make.

## Alternatives

1. Refuse a file whose identifier names another plan than the one chosen at upload
2. Restore plans at check time instead of at pair creation

## Decision

A photograph counts as linked to a plan only when it was uploaded for that plan or is a re-photograph of an accepted pair; if the file's identifier names another plan, the file wins, as it did for plates. A marked plan whose markers cannot be read falls back to matching its own drawing automatically; a plate still refuses (REQ-003). A file whose identifier belongs to a deleted plan or plate is reported as belonging to a deleted experiment. When a deletion leaves knowledge whose evaluation criteria were never agreed, only the counts decide whether it is flagged (two independent engravings keep 'validated' unflagged). The marker-layer setting may also hold a pulse width, line interval and passes; nothing is filled in when left out. A rig's chart values default to the published ColorChecker Classic sRGB values converted to Lab (D65, 2 degree observer); D50 values are adapted by the Bradford transform; only the 2 degree observer is accepted; a re-photograph defaults to its source's rig and an alignment revision keeps its source's rig record. Restored plans are made when the pair is created, take the planned colour of any knowledge entry that has all three estimates, simplify outlines within 10 micrometres, are archived with their last pair, and are not created for pairs that existed before. A target check of a photograph without a chart is recorded as not comparable; the stored check keeps the tolerance in force at acceptance while the analysis judges by the current tolerance.

## Reason

Each rule follows the closest accepted decision (file authoritative, DEC-017; never guess a mapping, REQ-003; never lower a stage, DEC-029) and keeps the operator's own data intact.

Proposed for the operator's review.
