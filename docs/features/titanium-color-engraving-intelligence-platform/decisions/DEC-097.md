## Context



## Alternatives

1. Keep the shapes of output-off layers in every alignment route: the experiment with the outline route still failed on pair 105 (orientation ambiguous) and would let layers that were never engraved distort the alignment, which REQ-050 removed.
2. Estimate the scale from the plate edges or the ColorChecker: the plate is cut by the photograph's frame and the chart's size differs between charts.
3. Search the scale in a grid without a ruler: slower (about 40 scales) and a single picture can match at an impossible scale.
4. Read the tilt of the rulers as a rotation prior: tried on the real photograph, the strip edges were not stable enough; the search tolerates about 1.5 degrees instead.

## Decision

A new automatic alignment route runs after the drawing's outlines and the largest picture have failed: the scale is read from the ruler in the photograph (tick spectrum of 1 mm with its 5 and 10 mm ticks, chains of windows along a narrow strip, one scale for the largest set of windows), and every picture of every layer of the file, switched off or not, is template-matched over the eight orientations at that scale (±5 %) on an edge structure blurred to tolerate about 1.5 degrees; the orientation and place on which the pictures agree win, ECC refines each picture, a second ECC pass starts from the transform they share, and one homography is fitted to their corners. Shapes of layers whose output is off are kept in `Layer.regions_off` (`Layer.placed`) for this route only; they are still not measured, and the corner route keeps fitting the corners to the measured layers.

## Reason

All layers of a file lie in one coordinate system, so one found layer places every other; the photograph of a finished plate shows what was engraved at any time, including layers that are switched off in this run of the file. The previous routes used the largest picture of the layers that are on, which in pair 105 is a symmetric seal that is ambiguous alone, and the manual corners were fitted to its bounding box. A known scale removes the search over scale that made single pictures ambiguous (a 75 px/mm false match), and several pictures agreeing on one origin verify the orientation without a threshold on one picture.

## Consequences

The route needs a ruler in the plane of the plate (a plate raised in a deep frame reads 1.5 times off and is refused), line-art-like pictures (coloured portraits correlate weakly and are left to the older route), and a camera square to the plate within about 1.5 degrees. It adds about 2 s for the ruler and 7 to 11 s for the search to a check that has failed every other route. Fill layers are not searched yet. The stored check of pair 105 keeps the old wrong alignment until the operator checks it again with the automatic alignment.
