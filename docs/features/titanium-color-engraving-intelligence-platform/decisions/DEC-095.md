## Context

Real mixed works in /Volumes/Storage0/Art/Laser: a cleaning rectangle, 7-20 colour fills (Scan) and one image layer burned last (grayscale 1500 dpi, jarvis 2540 or 1100 dpi, sketch 1100 dpi). Checked on a copy of the data with the finished-work photographs.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the bitmap rectangle as the covered area and ask the operator to mark the vector layers by hand
2. Decide from the photograph, region by region, whether the picture shows (texture analysis), without reading the picture's alpha and mode
3. Model the dither algorithms pixel by pixel to know exactly where dots fall

## Decision

An image layer engraves the pixels of its picture that are opaque and, by mode, struck: grayscale scans every opaque pixel at a power between Min (lightest) and Max (darkest), so white is engraved at Min and only Min 0 leaves white blank; threshold, ordered, atkinson, dither, stucki, jarvis, newsprint, halftone, sketch and 3dslice fire full-power dots whose density follows the darkness (threshold and pass-through by a cut at mid grey), so white is never struck; a transparent pixel (alpha under one half) is never struck in any mode; Negative image swaps light and dark; the picture adjustments (gamma, contrast, brightness) apply first. A layer burned earlier is engraved again only where a later image layer strikes at least 5 % of the pixel's area. The picture lies in the file as the tonal profile and the registration read it (row 0 first), turned over only when the photograph says so.

## Reason

The rectangle rule made every vector layer of the operator's two real mixed works unmeasurable (Joker 0 of 7, girl art deco 0 of 6 measured). The operator builds a mixed work by cutting the vector areas out of the picture with transparency: in the Joker the fills lie under 1.5 % of the marked area covered by the picture, and with the strike rule all 7 are measured. In girl art deco the picture is a fully opaque jarvis halftone (dot density 48 %) over all the fills (89.7 % of the marked area is under both), so its fills correctly stay unmeasured, now with the reason shown. The mode rules follow LightBurn's documentation (Min power for white in grayscale, binary dots elsewhere) and forum reports that transparent pixels are empty; the 5 % limit and the half-grey cut of threshold are assumptions, named as such on the screens.

## Consequences

The firing rule for grayscale white at Min power and for partly transparent pixels is documentation and forum evidence, not a measurement on the operator's laser; a ramp plate with a transparent hole decides it. Checks of mixed works measure the fills where the picture is transparent or white; the dots of a dithered layer over a fill are excluded at the 5 % limit of coverage.
