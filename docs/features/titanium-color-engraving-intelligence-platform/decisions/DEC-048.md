## Context

Unmarked finished works are aligned by register_geometry: Hu-moment matching of the 16 largest closed shapes, at least three agreeing, then an affine fit. On a real framed artwork (19 layers, 235 vectors, photographed in a frame that hides 0.6-2.4 mm of the plate border) it returns nothing, because only 43 of 235 paths were recognised as closed (Bezier edges were ignored) and large shapes are the ones the frame clips or merges with the black frame. The operator asked for the opposite logic: find a few elements that really match, trust them, and compute the positions of all others from the file distances, including those under the frame.

## Alternatives

1. Keep Hu-moment matching of whole shapes and only fix closure detection
2. Always ask for four manual corners
3. One global chamfer optimisation without per-element gating
4. Homography plus a polynomial or thin-plate correction of the anchor residuals

## Decision

For an unmarked work the photograph is registered in four steps. (1) The visible window of a frame is detected from its inner edges. (2) The 8 orientations (4 rotations, with and without mirror; a LightBurn file may be a vertical mirror of the photograph) are scored by the distance of all file outlines to photo edges around the window; the best must beat the second by a margin or the registration is refused. (3) Every file element is searched independently in a small window around its predicted place; only elements with a close, distinctive match become anchors. (4) A homography is fitted to the anchors only (RANSAC), every element is predicted from it, and steps 3-4 repeat with a shrinking window. Elements are then classed as anchor, confirmed (matched but not used), computed (not visible or low contrast), partly hidden and hidden. The result is accepted only with enough spread-out anchors, a small anchor residual and an orientation margin; otherwise the operator places four corners as before (REQ-013).

## Reason

Measured on the pilot pair: the coarse orientation cost was 1.73 px against at least 4.08 px for the other seven orientations; 77 anchors and 123 independently confirmed elements out of 235; median outline residual 0.08 mm at 10.2 px/mm. A blind test that hides a strip of the window and predicts it from the rest gave 0.2-1.0 mm for strips of 7-30 mm, while a real frame hides 0.6-2.4 mm. A polynomial correction on top of the homography was tried and gave no stable gain, so it is not adopted.

## Consequences

New module for the algorithm; lightburn.py must supply exact outlines; ingest reports anchors, statuses, orientation margin, window and hidden margins; register_geometry stays as a later fallback; manual corners stay mandatory as the last path. Expected error under the frame is an extrapolation and is reported as an estimate, never as a measurement.
