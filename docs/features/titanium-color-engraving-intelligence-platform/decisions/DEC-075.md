## Context

The operator asked that the colour map and the colour choice prefer what engraves faster, taking into account speed, line interval, passes and every other setting that turns into time. On a galvo laser, Q-Pulse and frequency change the energy of a pulse, not the time of a scan. The operator chose a filter plus the fastest recipe within tolerance.

## Alternatives

1. A slider weighing colour accuracy against time
2. A filter only, colour chosen by distance

## Decision

The engraving time of a filled recipe per square centimetre is 100 / (speed x line interval) seconds times passes, times global repeats, times 2 with cross-hatch, plus a turnaround allowance per scan line; a Line-mode recipe has a time per centimetre of path (passes and repeats included); pulse width and frequency do not enter it. It is shown as an estimate. The colour map and the vector work show the time of every recipe and filter by a maximum time per square centimetre. When colours are assigned automatically, among the measured colours within the delta E tolerance of the wanted colour the fastest recipe is chosen; the estimated time of the whole work is shown before export.

## Reason

The operator chose it on 2026-10-19: equal colours should cost as little machine time as possible.

## Consequences

The turnaround allowance is an engineering default until timed against a real job; the estimate is labelled so.
