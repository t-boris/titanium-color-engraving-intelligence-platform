## Context

BUG-004, pair 115: the export writes every LightBurn tag of the file a recipe was measured on, so the layers of work 39 whose recipes were measured on hidden layers came out hidden (C11, C18). LightBurn's select-all, move and resize then left them behind, and the check measured them beside the plate. The recipe key also counted the Show switch, so the same settings measured on a hidden and on a shown layer were two recipes with two colour map entries. On the operator's data, 58 observations (14 settings) carry hide=1. Their 11 knowledge rows are all measured, none approved. In the palette 12 entry ids would change, and only works 22 and 39 keep them (colouring, variants, undo, layers, exports). Rows 6 to 14 of the knowledge had lost their evidence earlier, when the parser started to keep the hide tag. The operator chose on 2026-10-23: «Не часть рецепта».

## Alternatives

1. Keep Show in the recipe and only stop the export from writing it: the same settings stay two recipes, and the hidden variant never repeats its shown twin
2. Make Show part of the recipe on purpose and let the export keep hiding the layer: LightBurn's tools then skip the layer, and every move of the work leaves it behind
3. Change the key and leave the works as they are: works 22 and 39 lose 7 and 8 regions' colours («no longer measured») and cannot be exported until the operator chooses them again

## Decision

lightburn.LAYER_SWITCH_TAGS = (hide, doOutput) are the job's, as the run order is (DEC-051). New observations do not store them (recipe_settings). model.parameters_key leaves them out, so stored observations key as the same settings measured on a shown layer. The export never writes them (plans.JOB_TAGS), so a job's layers are shown and engraved. The check of a photograph still reads both switches from the engraved file (REQ-050). tools/forget_layer_switches.py computes every palette id as 0.25.1 did and as now, for the laser, lens and titanium of each work. It renames each old id that changed wherever a work keeps it (exact, or as layer key e<id>), writes an audit entry, and refreshes the knowledge. It first makes a backup copy, and a dry run is the default. It refuses, changing nothing, when an old id would become two ids or would remain in a form it does not rename. It runs once at the deployment of 0.25.2, with the application stopped.

## Reason

Whether LightBurn shows a layer says nothing about how it engraves, and a hidden layer in a job is skipped by the tools the operator places the work with. Moving the stored ids keeps every colour the operator chose. On a copy of the operator's data, works 22 and 39 show the same colours, regions, layers and times, with nothing missing, and their exports have no hidden layer (two before).

## Consequences

Knowledge rows 31 to 39, 357 and 358 (the hidden variants) keep their stage and history but have no evidence any more, like the rows of a deleted pair. Rows 6 to 14 get their evidence back, and the evidence of 358 gets a new row. One hidden variant pools with its shown twin in the palette (602 entries become 601). A database restored from a backup made before 0.25.2 needs the tool run again. DEC-051's open point (old observations that still carry priority) is unchanged.
