## Context

The focused spot and the usable field depend on the lens, not on the laser source, and the operator can swap lenses. A lens held as a field of the laser passport would be overwritten on every swap and old plates would silently change meaning; DEC-005 and REQ-010 already require that data taken under different conditions are not merged silently.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the focal length and the spot diameter as passport fields (lost on a swap, no history).
2. Make the lens part of the profile constants and version the profile on every swap (heavy, and Q-022 questions the profile).

## Decision

Lenses are a separate table. A laser has at most one installed lens. Creating a plate stores a snapshot of the installed lens on the plate and on its plan. The characterization of a laser takes the installed lens and derives the spot and the work field from it. Checking a pair warns, without blocking, when the installed lens differs from the plate's lens. The spot diameter priority is the operator's measurement, then the calculation, and only a lower bound when M2 is unknown.

## Reason

A swap becomes a visible event, and a stored plate keeps the optics it was made for. A lower bound is not presented as a fact, in line with the principle that unconfirmed numbers do not drive results.

## Consequences

The measurement model still groups by profile version and does not yet split by lens: whether a lens change must start a new data group is open and should be settled together with Q-022. A plate made before this change has no lens snapshot and shows none.
