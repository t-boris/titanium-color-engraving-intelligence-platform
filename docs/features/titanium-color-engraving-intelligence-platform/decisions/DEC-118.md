## Context

The operator, 2026-10-23: in a dot kind (Jarvis and the others) the dots' colour could only be a power of the work's grey scale, while a dot kind's layer fires every dot at one set of settings (Min = Max), so any measured colour could be the dots. Asked which colours of the map may be dots, the operator chose only recipes with a line step of 0.0085 mm or more: finer steps overlap the dots many times, and the colour of such a fill says little about one dot. A dotted work's view already took about 0.49 s, almost all of it in the error diffusion of its look; gathering the colour map and the palette costs 0.2 to 0.3 s more.

## Alternatives

1. Only powers of the work's own scale (as before): the operator cannot reach the violets, blues and greens the scale does not make
2. Every recipe of the map, finer steps included, with a warning: the operator chose not to; such a fill's colour is no estimate of a dot
3. Keep only the picked recipe's id in the work and resolve it from the palette at every view: the view would gather the map each time and pass 0.5 s
4. A separate request for what each colour gives the dots beside the map's own: both gather the map in parallel and the screen opened in 0.82 s

## Decision

A point of the colour map is picked by its group (POST /api/photo-works/{id}/settings {dot_colour}). dot_colours.picked turns it into a change. A tone of a grey scale (a step of a grayscale image layer, measured on the work's titanium, at a power inside its scale's span at the work's width) sets the work's scale and dot_power. A fill of the palette of the laser, lens and titanium (pooled over the plates of one grade, DEC-088) becomes dot_recipe: a copy of its entry id, its settings (power, speed, frequency, Q-pulse, step, passes, global passes) and its colour at the work's width (palette.at_size, DEC-107). The copy is taken again when the width changes, and marked missing when the entry is no longer measured. The settings the dots fire at (photo_state.fired_at) replace the scale's in the look, the close-up, the time, the DPI and the layer of the LightBurn file. The kind's own measured tones and its ramp («Проба этого вида») are looked up at those settings (photo_state.kind_mode). A power on the scale, another scale or undo puts the dots back on the scale; the scale's power is kept for that. Refused: another titanium, a check not accepted, a line, a picture's tone, a tone of a dot engraving (its layer's image mode, now carried by every tonal point of the map as image_mode), a cross-hatched fill, a step under 0.0085 mm. The map picks in its own request (GET /api/colors?dots={id}): the map of the work's laser and lens with dots, what each shown colour gives the dots or why not. The dots of a work's look are kept in memory beside its stored preview, so an open work is dithered once.

## Reason

A dot kind's layer has one set of settings, so the recipe of any measured fill is exactly what LightBurn can fire, and its step is the dots' pitch. A copy in the work keeps a view from gathering the map, and the file always says what the screen showed. Classifying the points the map already gathered costs 0.05 s instead of a second gathering.

## Consequences

A fill's colour is the colour of the fill: one dot is the shortest row and may come out lighter or of another hue. The work says so until its ramp at those settings is photographed. Photo zones of vector works keep their dots on the scale until 0.25.1. On the operator's data (work 37, 2026-10-23 copy): the map opens to pick in 0.63 s against 0.57 s for the map alone (the map's model of the film is requested after its colours: a defect of its own). Picking a colour saves in about 1 s, the cost of any change of a dotted work's look. Opening the work again after a save waits 1.4 s for the list of scales, which is read anew after every write.
