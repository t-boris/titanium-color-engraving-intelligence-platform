## Context

Implementing REQ-021 (DEC-065) and the complete recipe export of REQ-012 on 2026-10-18 required choices the specification does not make: where a vector job lives, which recipes a region may take, how text and line regions are judged, how many output layers a job may use and whether non-plate jobs clean first.

## Alternatives

1. A separate table for vector jobs
2. Allow recipes of an unknown or another titanium stock with a warning
3. Apply the DE threshold to text and line regions too

## Decision

A vector job is a plan with mode 'Vector job'; its vector document and every earlier version are JSON files beside the stored source image, listed in the plan's history with every exported file. Only recipes measured on the job's titanium stock are used; recipes of an unknown stock are not. Colour, fill and tonal regions follow the DEC-030 threshold (tonal compares lightness only); text and line regions take the nearest recipe of a fitting LightBurn mode (Line for strokes, Scan for filled text) without a colour threshold, because legibility is not measured (REQ-020). One settings group takes one recipe and groups with the same recipe share one output layer (DEC-044); the marker colour is always kept free, so a job holds at most 29 recipes, 28 when it cleans first. A group's LightBurn mode is set by its largest region; a region needing another mode is weak until moved to its own group. An operator-chosen recipe overrides the threshold, as a discrete replacement does; a weak region is never exported with an invented recipe (it is excluded or sent to the designer as a target). Area conversions (discrete and photo) use only fill recipes, never Line-mode or image-layer recipes, which changes the palette. Any plan may clean first: cleaning on C00 with the established clean-first recipe adapted to the laser's frequency range and pulse steps, markers on C01. Exported jobs carry TCE1:<plan identifier> so their photographs link back at ingest.

## Reason

Each choice keeps an exported layer tied to a measured recipe of the same laser, lens, stock and LightBurn mode, and keeps the LightBurn palette limit.

## Consequences

Proposed for the operator's review. An old discrete draft matched to a Line-mode recipe must be re-matched before it can be finalised.
