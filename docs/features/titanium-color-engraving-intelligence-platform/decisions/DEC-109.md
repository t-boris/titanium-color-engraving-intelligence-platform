## Context

After 0.20.1 the operator saw white dashed lines on the plain picture and, in the engravings of «Inna Portrait» (pairs 109, 111), skin in one tone without a gradient and nearly black square patches in its shadows; the 0.20.1 choice (DEC-108) took each pixel's grey from anywhere on the scale, so neighbouring skin tones landed on distant powers (grey-blue beside tan). The operator: lighten the skin in an even gradient, keep the lips and the main features; contours off or switchable, never on the plain picture.

## Alternatives

1. Keep per-pixel nearest colour with lightness weighted (DEC-108, rejected: blotches on the skin)
2. Map lightness over the whole scale in grey order (rejected: the scale's lightness rises and falls with power, so a gradient would swing through blue and back)
3. Keep the picture's hues where they are strong (rejected for now: patches at the borders; the operator chose the smooth path)
4. Remove contours entirely (the operator allowed it; kept as an opt-in switch for picked greys)

## Decision

«Сохранить рисунок» lays the picture's tones along a path of the scale: from its lightest grey to its darkest in the order of power, lightness never rising by more than 0.5 L*, and of such paths the one whose squared colour steps add up least (dynamic programming), so a hue swing at one lightness is skipped. The picture's lightness is smoothed edge-preserving over 0.25 mm (bilateral, 6 L*), its contrast over 1.5 mm raised by 0.5, stretched over the path from its 1st to 99th percentile with mid-tones lifted (t^0.8), and each pixel takes the first path grey at that lightness; a picture of one lightness keeps it. The picture's hues are not matched ("Ближайший цвет" does that). The order measure ranks ties together (Spearman). Contours (DEC-108) are off by default and drawn only when the greys are picked by colour; for the plain picture the switch is disabled and nothing is added. This replaces the choice part of DEC-108; its contour method stands.

## Reason

Neighbouring tones get neighbouring powers, so a gradient stays a gradient and grain does not become spots; on the live portrait the order of light and dark is 0.99 on every scale of the operator (0.98 before), in 0.07 s at preview size and 0.5 s for the file. The plain picture is the operator's own and is engraved as uploaded.

## Consequences

The whole picture takes the colours of one stretch of the scale (gold, sepia, grey or copper depending on the scale); the median distance to the picture's own colours rises (24 dE on the portrait). RENDER_VERSION 6 redraws cached pictures. Works saved with contours on keep the setting but it only acts with picked greys.
