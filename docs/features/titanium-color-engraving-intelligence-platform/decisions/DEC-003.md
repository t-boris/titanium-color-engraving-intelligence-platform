## Context

Source of color data and its calibration determine the CV pipeline and the meaning of color error.

## Alternatives

1. Free-hand smartphone photos with a chart
2. Colorimeter/spectrophotometer per cell

## Decision

Plates are photographed on a fixed rig (camera on stand, controlled light and angle, RAW) with a color reference chart in every frame.

## Reason

Reproducibility is the project goal and requires stable capture conditions; a rig scales to hundreds of cells and is cheaper than a colorimeter.

## Consequences

Rig must be built (hardware out of scope); the spec must define capture protocol and chart-based correction; angle-dependent (iridescent) measurements become possible later.
