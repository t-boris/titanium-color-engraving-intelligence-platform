## Context

Photo zones (REQ-073, DEC-114, DEC-115) only had a kind, a scale and powers, chosen on a card in the editor's «Фото» tab. The operator (2026-10-22): a zone must do what a photo engraving work does — keep the drawing, choose the greys, colour, and so on; without it the result is poor. The photo work had all of it, written for a whole picture in photo_work.py and photo-engraving.js.

## Alternatives

1. Grow the zone card in the editor's «Фото» tab with every control of the photo work: no room for the picture beside its look, the scales and the tabs; the same controls written twice
2. Make each zone a photo work of its own, linked to the vector work: two plans to keep in step, the zone's file layer and undo split between them
3. Copy photo_work.py and photo-engraving.js for zones: two copies of the same rules and messages that drift apart

## Decision

The state of a photo engraving (the kind and its powers, how the greys are chosen — keep the drawing, nearest colour, colouring by objects — contours, the rules of parts, the colouring, the AI's answers and their checks and messages) moves into one module, photo_state, used by a photo work and by a photo zone alike; a zone keeps it in colouring["photo"], a zone made before reads the missing keys as the drawing kept (photo_zones.full). The panels of the photo work screen move into photo-panels.js, each taking the API address of what it belongs to. «Открыть» on a zone card opens the zone on its own screen (#/vector-zone?work=&zone=): the scales of the work's laser, lens and titanium on the left, the zone's picture beside its predicted look in the middle, the tabs on the right; the zone's width is its regions'. Every change of the zone is a step of the vector work: its undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) are the work's, and a step that takes the zone away returns to the work. 0.23.0 gives the picture, the scale, the kinds and the dots; 0.23.1 adds «Раскраска» and «AI» with objects and parts found once on the whole picture and cut per zone. The AI may see the picture under the vector work's permission (DEC-084), not a zone's own.

## Reason

One core and one set of panels mean a zone and a photo work choose, predict and write their greys the same way, with the same messages, and a fix in one is a fix in both; the zone stays part of the vector work's colouring, so its file layer, its undo and its plan row stay where they were.

## Consequences

photo_work.py delegates to photo_state; photo_zones.SETTINGS grows by colour_choice, contours, rules and colouring (GREYS) and ANSWERS (ai, ai_theme); a new zone copies the last zone's settings. New routes: GET /vector/{id}/photo/{n}, POST …/settings, …/undo, …/redo, GET …/preview.png, …/closeup.png. The plan page and the zone card name how the zone's greys are chosen.
