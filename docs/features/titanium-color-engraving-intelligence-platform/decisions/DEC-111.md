## Context

Operator, 2026-10-22: "когда генеришь LightBurn file - не забывай про cleaning layer". plans.export_picture wrote the picture alone, for a photo work and for «Картинка на шкале», while plates and vector works clean first on C00 (plans.clean_layer); the grey scales were measured on cleaned plates. The upload of a work's photograph already knew a CLEAN-CROSSHATCH layer as the pre-test cleaning witness and finds the picture layer by its tonal profile. The operator chose: always.

## Alternatives

1. A switch per work (the operator chose always)
2. Clean the whole plate (the plate's size is not known to a work; the picture's rectangle is what is engraved)

## Decision

Every picture file (a photo work's file, «Картинка на шкале») has C00 the laser's cleaning recipe (plans.clean_layer of its passport) as a rectangle over the picture, run first, and the picture as the image layer on C01. The work's time includes the cleaning and says how much of it is cleaning; the file's text names the cleaning layer. A work's file opens in the preview from the work («Файл в предпросмотре») with its laser, lens and titanium.

## Reason

The colours of a scale were measured on cleaned titanium; a picture engraved on an uncleaned surface would not give them. It is the same layer plates and vector works write, and the upload already reads it.

## Consequences

A picture takes longer (the cleaning is a cross-hatch over its rectangle). Files made before 0.20.3 have no cleaning layer; their photographs are measured as before.
