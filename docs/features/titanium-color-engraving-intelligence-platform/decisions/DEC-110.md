## Context

Operator, 2026-10-22, with a screenshot of the grey-scale plate TCE-A93B49E6 on «Big Plate 150»: "должно работать для титана любого размера"; the preview had refused the photo work's file on «Small Plate» ("в этом режиме цвет ещё не измерялся") because measurements were partitioned by the stock record (material_id). Answers: the whole grade (Grade 5) is one titanium; thickness does not matter; everywhere (scales, photo works, preview, vector palette, colour map). The vector palette already pooled plates of one family and grade (palette._same_material). Stored keys carry the plate record: knowledge entries (observation_key |ti:id:N, 549 entries: 500 on Big Plate 150, 49 on Small Plate, one recipe on both), colour-map recipe groups (|ti:N|, whose hash is the id of a palette entry kept in vector works' assignments), findings and designer proposals.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the plate record as the titanium and ask the operator to engrave a grey-scale plate on every plate (refused by the operator)
2. Re-key stored data (knowledge entries, recipe groups, findings) by titanium: a migration that changes the ids vector works keep
3. A canonical record per grade (the smallest id) written into modes and works: editing that record's grade would move every work silently to another titanium

## Decision

The titanium of a stock record is its family and grade (case and spacing aside, as the record says now, not as an engraving's upload copied it); a record with no grade is only itself; no record is none (setup.titanium_key, setup.same_titanium; the palette's rule moves there). Grey scales and their curves take the points of every plate of the titanium; one scale per titanium, named by the titanium asked for or by the plate it was first measured on, saying the titanium and the plates it was measured on. The preview predicts a file on any plate of a measured titanium; a refused layer lists where its mode is measured, one plate per titanium. A photo work keeps its plate; a scale chosen on another plate of its titanium is the same scale, and an engraving on any plate of it counts as the work's scale. In the colour map one recipe on several plates of one titanium is one entry (its confidence over all their engravings) and the «Материал» filter offers titanium; each point still names its plate. Recipe groups keep the plate so palette entry ids do not change: the pooling is a second key (recipe). Knowledge entries, findings, designer proposals, the application guide and research sessions stay per plate (stored keys; not in the operator's scope).

## Reason

It is the operator's rule and the palette's existing rule, applied through one helper; nothing stored is rewritten, so no id that a work keeps changes, and a work's plate stays what the operator chose.

## Consequences

A scale measured on several plates of one grade has more engravings, so its confirmation and size information grow; a scale of a grade measured on one plate serves every plate of that grade, also where thickness or surface could change the colour (the operator's call: "толщина не важна"). Renaming a record's grade moves its measurements to another titanium at once. A typo in a grade ("Garde 5") makes a separate titanium. Knowledge-based palettes (validated or production entries; none exist yet) still take one plate.
