## Context

On «Inna Portrait» at 100 mm (live scale 350 ns, Min 1, Max 30) the nearest colour of the scale gave the light skin of the forehead a grey whose colour is a dark tan of the same hue, so the forehead engraved almost black; and where the scale gives skin and lips nearly the same colour, the lips vanished. The operator: the forehead must be grey, not black; contours must always show somehow.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the nearest colour only and warn (rejected: the picture is unrecognisable while every colour is 'nearest')
2. Map the picture's lightness alone to grey (rejected: loses the hues the scale can give)
3. Dither between greys (rejected: on titanium a dithered pattern engraves as its own colour, not an average)
4. Always draw every edge of the picture (rejected: lines on edges the engraving keeps add noise; speckles of skin and reflections become dots)

## Decision

A photo work chosen by colour has a colour choice, «Сохранить рисунок» ("drawing", the default) or «Ближайший цвет» ("nearest"), and a contours switch (on by default), both saved in the work and undone like any change; older works open with the defaults. Drawing: the picture's L* (2-98 percentiles) is stretched in order over the L* the scale reaches at the work's width (3-97 percentiles of the usable greys; a picture of one lightness is not stretched), local contrast over 1.5 mm on the titanium is raised by 0.8, and the grey nearest in Lab with L* weighted four times is taken; in flat areas (local L* std under 2) a grey whose colour changes more slowly with power is taken among the 8 nearest within 3 dE. Contours: an edge of at least 8 dE across 0.5 % of the picture's width (at least 0.3 mm), found at 10 px/mm, that the prediction keeps under 6 dE, reaching at least 1 mm, is drawn 0.3 mm wide in the darkest usable grey, or the lightest on a light ridge or where the dark one would not contrast by 15 L*; not in parts with an "even" rule nor where transparent. Order: colour choice, part rules, contours, the same for the preview and the LightBurn file, each at its own resolution in millimetres.

## Reason

Tone order carries a portrait: on «Inna Portrait» the drawing choice keeps the order of light and dark at 98 % (nearest colour 91 %), the forehead becomes light grey and the lips show by tone; the contours draw only what the engraving would lose (0.04 % of the portrait with the drawing choice, 1.2 % with nearest colour at 100 mm). Measuring in millimetres keeps the preview and the file the same picture.

## Consequences

The median colour distance to the picture grows (11.2 to 16.2 dE on the portrait): the hue is given up for lightness. RENDER_VERSION 5 redraws cached pictures. The thresholds are fixed constants in drawing.py, to be tuned on engravings.
