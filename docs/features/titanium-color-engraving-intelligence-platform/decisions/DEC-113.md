## Context

The operator wants to colour a photo work «как в разукрашке»: the lips, the glasses, the hair, parts of the background each in a colour of its own, the shades around it chosen too, maybe at random. On a grayscale scale power drives the oxide through straw, gold, violet, blue and back, so one colour has neighbours of other families.

## Alternatives

1. One target colour per object with the nearest scale colour per pixel: neighbouring tones jump between families
2. Recolour the photograph and then choose greys by nearest colour: the scale's colour families are not respected

## Decision

The objects come from objects.find (people model parts, lips and eyes from the face detector, the background split into up to four pieces by graph segmentation). An object with a main colour (a power of the scale, and lighter/darker -1..1) is drawn on the path through that colour: from it towards light and towards dark, steps of at most 5 dE, lightness moving one way on each side, the smoothest such path (dynamic programming). The object's own lightness, smoothed and with its detail raised as «Сохранить рисунок» does, is stretched over the path with its middle tone on the main colour (a power curve), shifted by a quarter of the path for lighter/darker. Objects without a colour keep «Сохранить рисунок» for the whole picture. Themes choose main colours among the scale's colours at least 8 dE apart: warm, cool, contrast, random by seed; the AI chooses among the same colours from the operator's wish, its answer checked and sent back once. The preview and the file use the same greys (the objects' map resized to the file).

## Reason

A path of small steps keeps the object in its colour family (no jump from gold to blue) while its own light and dark stay in order; the prototype on «Inna Portrait» showed the lips, glasses and background each recognisable in their own colours.

## Consequences

Borders between objects are where the models put them; the background pieces follow the segmentation at 400 px, which can make a stepped border. Colouring by objects is grayscale only: a dot kind fires every dot at one power.
