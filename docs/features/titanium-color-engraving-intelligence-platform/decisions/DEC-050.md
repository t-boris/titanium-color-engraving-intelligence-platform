## Context

A layer's measurement mask was the union of its shapes. In the pilot work 8% of the plate is covered by two or more layers, and LightBurn runs layers in priority order, so what is seen where outlines overlap belongs to the layer run last. Only the cleaning witness of a plate already subtracted later geometry. On a synthetic test with a layer 86% covered by a later one, the old rule measured the covering layer's colour (delta E 100) as that layer's colour.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the union mask and rely on the median
2. Drop every layer that has any overlap
3. Model the combination of two passes as a separate recipe

## Decision

For every test-feature layer the mask excludes the filled areas (closed shapes of Scan and Image layers, holes respected) of every layer run after it, ordered by priority and then layer index, with a two-pixel guard. The share excluded is stored per layer and noted when it reaches 1%. Lines, the plate's service layers and Cut layers cover nothing. The same exclusion applies to the narrow-stroke fallback.

## Reason

The colour of an observation must belong to the layer whose parameters are recorded with it. A median over a mask that is mostly another layer's colour silently assigns that colour to the wrong recipe.

## Consequences

Measured colours of overlapped layers change (they become the layer's own colour); plates with non-overlapping cells are unaffected. A layer that is completely covered gets no measurement and a note. The colour of overlap areas as a two-pass result is not modelled.
