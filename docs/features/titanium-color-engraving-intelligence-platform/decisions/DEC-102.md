## Context

LightBurn writes a path's VertList and PrimList once, on the first path that has them, and later paths with the same list carry only VertID / PrimID. In the engraved Inna + Boris 9_backup 222 of 574 paths had no PrimList of their own and were read as open lines with no area; 8 of the workshop's stored files have such paths (Sergey Kalinin 186 of 371).

## Alternatives



## Decision

Before reading shapes, the reader collects every VertList and PrimList of the file by its id (first definition wins; it always precedes its uses), and a path, a text's BackupPath included, without its own list takes the one its id names.

## Reason

These shapes are closed fills in LightBurn; read as open lines they were missing from areas, times, overlays and measurements.

## Consequences

Overlays are redrawn (render version 3). Checks made before 0.18.0 keep their measurements; re-measuring a work (Перемерить заново) measures those shapes too.
