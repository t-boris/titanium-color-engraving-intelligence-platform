## Context

Implementing REQ-022 on 2026-10-18 required rules the specification does not state: what counts as an anomaly or an improvement, how gaps are found, which gaps can become proposals, and how a proposal's status moves.

## Alternatives

1. Judge anomalies against a spread that includes the observation itself
2. Let every gap become a proposal

## Decision

An anomaly is an observation farther than the saved delta E tolerance from its recipe's mean or, without a saved tolerance, beyond 3 standard deviations of the recipe's other engravings (at least two of them). An improvement is a recipe that became validated, a target colour now reached, or an expected error lower than in the previous run; validated and reached repeat in every run while true and are marked new in the first run that sees them. Coverage gaps are checked per laser over its installed lens and the lenses and titanium stocks that have evidence; recipes resting on a single engraving give one gap per stock and task. Only target-colour, fill-evidence and single-engraving gaps can become designer proposals (the designer makes fill plates); job settings of such a proposal come from the target's earlier proposal or the most-measured settings group of that laser and lens, never invented. A proposal moves proposed, accepted (plates made, cleaning first), done (all plates photographed and accepted; it can drop back when a photograph is deleted), or rejected (only from proposed); proposals that had plates before statuses existed count as accepted. The schedule counts from the last run of any trigger; a failing analysis after an acceptance is written to the audit and never undoes the acceptance. AI insights are always hypotheses; insights of deleted research sessions are skipped. Findings name laser, lens and stock by id without foreign keys.

## Reason

Each rule keeps a finding reproducible from accepted measurements and keeps hypotheses visibly apart from measured conclusions (REQ-022 AC3).

## Consequences

Proposed for the operator's review. On a migrated copy of the live database a run takes 0.04 s and finds 4 gaps (333 recipes rest on a single engraving) and 3 hypotheses; no unstable or anomaly findings until evaluation thresholds are saved.
