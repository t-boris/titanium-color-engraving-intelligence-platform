## Context

The operator's consolidation of 2026-10-19 put into REQ-041 that colours without a clean-titanium reference are counted and omitted from the map by default, which contradicts accepted DEC-057 (shown by default, pale and dotted, hideable; kept out of search and automatic colouring unless asked). The operator was asked and chose DEC-057. Separately, DEC-074 named Apache-2.0 as the licence of OpenCV's YuNet face model; the model's own folder in OpenCV Zoo states MIT (Copyright (c) 2020 Shiqi Yu), which the zoo's Apache-2.0 defers to.

## Alternatives

1. Follow REQ-041 as written: hide such colours by default

## Decision

REQ-041's criterion on colours without a clean reference is read through DEC-057: they are on the colour map by default, pale with a dotted outline and a checkbox to hide them, counted, and left out of the nearest-colour search and of automatic colouring of a vector work unless the operator asks to include them; choosing one by hand stays possible and is labelled. The YuNet model file ships under its MIT licence, with the licence text and the source and sha256 beside it; this corrects the licence named in DEC-074, everything else in DEC-074 stands.

## Reason

The operator chose DEC-057 on 2026-10-19; the licence is a fact of the model's source.

## Consequences

No change to the current colour map; the vector-work palette's automatic modes skip colours measured as photographed unless included.
