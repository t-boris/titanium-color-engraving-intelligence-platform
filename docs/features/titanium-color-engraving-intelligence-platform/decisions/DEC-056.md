## Context

The colour screen was organised by profile version and, inside it, by experiment. The operator wants one complete colour map: a wanted colour is chosen and the map says where it occurred and with which settings. Colours measured without a reference chart are relative to their camera and differ between photographs only by exposure and white balance.

## Alternatives

1. Show the measured Lab as it is with a photograph label
2. Keep one map per profile version
3. Difference of Lab values instead of the ratio

## Decision

One screen shows every measured colour of every kept experiment, all laser and lens versions together with a filter (also material and how measured). Colours measured against a chart are absolute. A camera-relative colour is divided, per channel in linear light, by the cleaned titanium of the same photograph (the cleaning witness) and multiplied with the nominal clean titanium (the median of charted witnesses, otherwise a default); the ratio does not depend on exposure. Such colours are marked approximate. The witness is the observation with the cleaning role; for finished works it is the first layer when it is one filled rectangle enclosing everything else, also found again from the stored file for works accepted earlier. A colour with no witness is counted and left off the map unless asked for. Search returns the recipes nearest to a wanted colour, one entry per recipe, with how often it came out and from which experiments. The earlier model and prediction page stays reachable from the map.

## Reason

A ratio in linear light removes the exposure that makes camera-relative Lab values incomparable (tested: the same appearance photographed at 1.0 and 0.35 exposure lands on the same place within 1 delta E). The cleaning rectangle is already measured as the clean-only part of its layer.

## Consequences

The map is exact for charted photographs and approximate for the others; white balance differences between photographs are not removed. A work without a cleaning layer and without a chart has no place on the map. This supersedes the earlier rule that data of different profile versions are never combined for the display (the predictions keep that rule).
