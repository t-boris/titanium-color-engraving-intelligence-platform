## Context

The operator asked that a new photo work ask which kind of engraving and show the picture in each kind. Only grayscale scales are measured; no dot kind (Jarvis, Stucki, halftone ...) has been engraved on the operator's settings. A dot layer fires every dot at one power (LightBurn ignores Min for dots), and between the dots the titanium stays as cleaned.

## Alternatives

1. Show only kinds already measured: none would be shown
2. Simulate LightBurn's own dot code: it is not published; the published algorithms are used and the UI says so

## Decision

Every kind that colours titanium is offered (3D slice is left out). A dot kind is shown as an estimate: each dot has the colour the grayscale scale gives at the dot power, the titanium between the dots has the cleaned titanium's nominal colour, and the two are mixed in linear light by the share of dots at the layer's DPI (dots.coverage, published kernels; Bayer, newsprint and sketch inferred). The default dot power is the one whose colour stands out most from the cleaned titanium. «Проба этого вида» makes a one-strip ramp in that kind at that power; once its photograph is measured, the tones it measured replace the estimate at that power. The file writes the image layer with ditherMode of the kind, Min = Max = the dot power, C00 cleaning first.

## Reason

The operator chose «Оценка по шкале» (2026-10-22): the picture in every kind is shown now, before any dot kind is engraved, and is replaced by measurement as soon as the operator engraves the kind's ramp.

## Consequences

A single dot is the shortest row there is and the colour depends on row length (DEC-107), so real dots may come out another shade; the UI says «оценка: этот вид ещё не гравировался». Dot width correction (dotWidth, useDotCorrection) is not written yet.
