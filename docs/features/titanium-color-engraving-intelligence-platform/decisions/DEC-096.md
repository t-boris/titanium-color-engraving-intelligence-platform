## Context

REQ-030 and DEC-052 refused every mode except grayscale, because in a dithered mode the grey sets a pattern of full-power dots, not the power. Photo engravings in jarvis (2540 dpi) and other dot modes are in the operator's real works, and the operator asked to understand photo engraving in all its kinds and to predict how an engraving will look at given settings (goal 2026-10-21).

## Alternatives

1. Keep refusing every dot mode
2. Simulate each dither algorithm pixel by pixel and derive the colour from a dot model
3. Predict dot modes from the power curve of grayscale mode

## Decision

The modes ordered, atkinson, dither, stucki, jarvis, newsprint and halftone are predicted: the colour of a grey level is read from the tonal profiles of accepted checks in the same mode, at the same layer power, dot width, speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval and passes. The curve runs over the darkness of the grey (0-100 %), not over a power, and Min and Max do not apply. Dithering is not modelled. A profile photographed under a picture that lies over vector fills (at least 5 % of the marked area) is not used, because its colour is the fill mixed with the dots. Flat fills are not points of a dot-mode curve. Threshold (a cut at mid grey), sketch (edges) and 3D slice (depth) are refused with the reason: they have no tones to measure. A dot mode with no measurement at its settings is refused with the existing reason. Grayscale prediction is unchanged.

## Reason

A tone in a dot mode is the share of the area under dots, so what was photographed at that tone in that mode is the direct evidence; a dither model would be assumed physics for a laser whose oxide colour is a thin-film effect that is very sensitive to fluence. The same-power condition keeps a profile from another dot energy out of the curve.

## Consequences

Supersedes the dithered-mode refusal of REQ-030 and DEC-052 for these seven modes. No photograph pair in a dot mode exists in the database yet, so the prediction is tested on synthetic works only and refuses until the first accepted check in the mode exists; the girl art deco pair is excluded because its picture lies over the fills. The interval of two independent engravings is shown in ΔE as for grayscale.
