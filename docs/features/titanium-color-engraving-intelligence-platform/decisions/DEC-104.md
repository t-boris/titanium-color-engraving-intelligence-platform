## Context

The operator wants photo engravings kept as works, like vector works (REQ-061). Vector works are rows of the plan table (mode "Vector job", data_json) listed, copied, renamed and deleted through it; an exported plan carries a TCE1 identifier in the LightBurn notes and the photograph of its engraving finds it by that identifier (REQ-013, DEC-090). Ingest then aligns the photograph to the identified plan's layers and markers, which a photo work does not have.

## Alternatives

1. A table of its own for photo works: duplicates the plan table's listing, deletion, audit and experiment rules for no gain.
2. Writing the identifier into the file now: ingest would align the photograph to the plan's layers and markers; to be done with the engraving check.

## Decision

A photo work is a plan row of the new mode "Photo engraving work" (plans.MODE_PHOTO_WORK), handled by photo_work.py: the uploaded picture stored once in the data folder, the scale (laser and lens in the plan's columns, titanium and fixed settings in data_json), Min and Max, width, colour choice and a TCE-P identifier. Everything shown (prediction, time, file) is computed from these and the scale's current measurements; the pictures go through the render cache keyed by all inputs including the scale's measured points. Deleting goes through DELETE /plans/{id} as for vector works; photo works without photographs are not experiments. Until the engraving check of a photo work is built, its LightBurn file carries no identifier: its engraving is measured as the operator's own photo engravings are and counts towards the scale.

## Reason

The plan table already gives works their listing, deletion, audit and the rule that unphotographed works are not experiments; a photo work is one more kind of plan. An identifier in the file would make ingest treat the photograph as an exported system plan and align it to layers and markers a photo work does not have.

## Consequences

The prediction of a work changes when its scale is measured again; the list and the open work always show the current one. The engraving of a work is not yet tied to the work: that is the next step (the engraving check).
