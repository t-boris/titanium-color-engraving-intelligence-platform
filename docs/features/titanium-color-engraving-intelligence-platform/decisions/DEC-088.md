## Context

Operator, 2026-10-20: "Why can't an experiment be restarted? Add it after the colours are fixed" and, after DEC-086 and DEC-087, "Переделай все эксперименты с новыми подсчётами". Observations are immutable (DB trigger), so a better measurement method (the look-up of DEC-087, the laser's default Q-pulse of DEC-086) reaches an experiment only through a new measurement. The alignment-revision flow already creates a new pair from an accepted one (same RAW, same LightBurn file, same alignment, same supplied settings and tasks), and accepting it removes the old pair from active use.

## Alternatives

1. Update the old observations in place (refused: immutable by design, the audit must show what was measured when)
2. Leave the operator to press revise, check, review, accept on every check by hand (what exists today)
3. Replace unconditionally (a failed or worse re-measurement would destroy a good one)

## Decision

"Measure again" is a revision of the accepted pair (same photograph, alignment, supplied settings, tasks) that is checked with today's measurement, its masks reviewed as before and accepted in one step. The new measurement replaces the old one only when it is whole: the colour basis is the same as before (a pair measured against the chart is never silently downgraded to camera-relative), the check has no problems, and no layer is read less than before. Otherwise the draft is dropped, the old measurement stays untouched and the reason is shown. The old pair leaves active use as in any revision and its observations stay in the history. The answer lists every layer with the colour and Q-pulse before and after. Offered per check (experiment and analysis screens: "Перемерить заново") and run over every accepted pair when the measurement changes.

## Reason

It is the existing, tested revision path with a guard against making a measurement worse, so no observation is edited and the history keeps both.

## Consequences

Pair ids of re-measured checks change (a link to an old check id leads to a deleted pair; experiments are addressed by plan or engraving and keep their address). Re-measuring does not look at the photograph again: the alignment of the old acceptance is reused, so a wrong alignment is also kept. It needs the RAW and the LightBurn file still on disk; a pair whose files were purged is kept as it is. Each check takes about 10-30 s.
