## Context

The operator wants to ask the AI to change parts of a photo engraving ("платье сделать фиолетовым или красным ... кожу сделать равномерно светлой") and chose a people segmentation model for the parts (2026-10-22, REQ-062). The project runs models through OpenCV only (faces.py: YuNet ONNX). OpenCV 5's TFLite reader refuses the MediaPipe model (TRANSPOSE_CONV).

## Alternatives

1. The mediapipe library: a large new dependency with its own numpy and OpenCV pins.
2. Colour regions only (segment.py): cannot tell a dress from skin of the same tone.

## Decision

Use MediaPipe's selfie_multiclass_256x256 (Apache 2.0; background, hair, body skin, face skin, clothes, other), converted once from TFLite to ONNX with tf2onnx 1.17 (opset 13) and shipped as models/selfie_multiclass_256x256.onnx; people.py runs it with cv2.dnn.readNetFromONNX behind a lock (NHWC RGB 0..1, 256 x 256 input). The six scores are kept and stretched to any size (preview, file) and the largest wins.

## Reason

On the spike the ONNX model matches the TFLite one (max difference 6e-5, argmax agreement 100 %), runs in 0.06-0.09 s per picture through OpenCV, and needs no new runtime library.

## Consequences

A 16 MB model file ships in the package (models/*); pictures without people come out as all background.
