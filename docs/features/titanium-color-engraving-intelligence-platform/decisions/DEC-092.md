## Context

In the file the operator engraved from work 22 the picture was resized to 100 x 100 mm and moved, but layers C18 and C28, hidden in LightBurn, stayed at their exported places. The check measured them where the photograph shows other things (C18 L 66.7 against 44 promised, C28 L 75.7 against 32), their outlines were drawn outside the plate, and they pulled the alignment window wider than the plate.

## Alternatives

1. Leave out every hidden layer (wrongly drops hidden layers that moved with the rest and says nothing about a visible layer that did not)
2. Use the photograph: leave out layers whose elements are not found (the alignment finds some elements of a misplaced layer by chance: 18 of 29 of C18 were reported found at wrong places)
3. Re-align each such layer on its own (would measure the old place of a layer that was engraved at the new one or not at all, and the operator cannot tell which)

## Decision

The engraved file is compared with the newest readable export of the work. The move the rest of the file made is the median over the layers the two files share of the scale, the offset and the mirror of each axis; a layer is left behind when at least half of its shapes sit where the export had them (within 0.25 mm on x, y, width and height) and fewer than half sit where that move puts them. A move is believed only when at least three layers, and at least half of the shared layers, follow it. The layers whose output is switched off are left out for that reason alone, with or without an export.

## Reason

It names a layer only when the files prove it: the layer demonstrably sits at its old place while others demonstrably moved. A hidden flag alone is not enough (a hidden layer that moved with the rest is fine) and a layer that was edited matches neither place and is left alone.

## Consequences

Only vector works can be compared: a plate or a capability plan has no exported file of this kind and is not checked. A work engraved from a file edited beyond a move and a resize (layers split, moved one by one) says nothing. The operator has to check the pair again to see the effect.
