## Context

REQ-073: regions of a vector work are to be engraved as a photo engraving. The vector file is laid out top-down with MirrorY=True; the photo work's writer stores pictures bottom row first for MirrorY=False and writes transparent pixels white, which LightBurn's grayscale engraves at Min power. The operator's own mixed works cut the vector areas out of the picture with transparency (DEC-095).

## Alternatives

1. White outside the zone (as the photo work's file): grayscale Min > 0 would engrave the whole box over the vector colours
2. One zone per work: the operator may want the face in grayscale and the hair in Jarvis
3. Zones as separate photo works placed on the vector work: no shared undo, two files to align

## Decision

A zone is a list of regions with its own kind, scale (titanium and fixed settings of a measured grey scale), Min/Max, dots' power and «цвета по картинке», kept in the work's colouring so that undo, redo, copy and the history carry it; restoring a colour variant keeps the zones as they are. Each zone is one Image layer after the colour layers: the work's original picture cut to the zone's box (at most the layer's DPI), alpha 0 outside the zone's regions rasterised from the same smoothed borders the paths are written from, Bitmap stored top row first because the file is MirrorY=True. Its colours are predicted at rows as long as the zone is wide (DEC-107). A new zone takes the best measured scale of the work's laser, lens and titanium (chart-referenced first, then most engravings), its span at that width, grayscale and colours chosen by the picture keeping the drawing (DEC-108/109), so its colours follow the picture as the vector colours do. A lasso selects regions at least half inside it; a new split keeps a zone on the new regions at least half inside the old zone.

## Reason

The colouring is what undo, copy and variants already carry, so a zone behaves like any other choice of the operator; transparency is how the operator builds mixed works and white would engrave over the vector areas; colours by the picture make the zone sit in the picture like its vector neighbours.

## Consequences

That LightBurn leaves pixels with alpha below 0.5 unengraved rests on forum reports and the operator's own files (DEC-095), not on a measurement on this laser; the first engraving of a zone is its test. The zone's Image layer is measured on ingest as an image layer; the work's review compares colour layers only.
