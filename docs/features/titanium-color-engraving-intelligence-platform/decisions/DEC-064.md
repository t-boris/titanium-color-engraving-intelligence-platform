## Context

DEC-013 says an external LLM never chooses parameters or modifies data. The AI researcher (README, research.py) chooses a test type and settings and creates, replaces or cancels a pending test plate, runs the photo mapping and saves research insights when the operator asks for it in the chat. The operator was asked on 2026-10-18 which of the two wins and chose to record an exception.

## Alternatives

1. Comply with DEC-013: remove plate creation, replacement and insight writing from the AI researcher, keep it to explanations

## Decision

The AI researcher may create, replace or cancel one pending test plate only when the operator asks for it explicitly in the research chat, and only through the same validated plate generator a person uses (device limits, lens field, 30-layer limit, cleaning first, markers); it never edits or deletes observations, pairs or knowledge; its saved insights are hypotheses or candidates linked to accepted photographs and never measured conclusions. The offline Experiment Designer stays fully algorithmic and works without any LLM key.

## Reason

The operator chose it: the researcher's plates go through the same checks as manual ones and measurements stay the source of truth.

## Consequences

DEC-013 and REQ-006 hold for the Experiment Designer; the research chat is the stated exception. A plate made by the researcher records that it came from a research session.
