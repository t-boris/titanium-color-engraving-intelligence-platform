## Context

REQ-001, REQ-002, REQ-004 and REQ-009 require device-independent colors and a color error (delta E), but no requirement fixes the color space, the white point, the observing conditions or the delta E formula. The prototype used Lab without defining them.

## Alternatives

1. CIEDE2000 instead of CIE76 for perceptual accuracy.
2. A different white point or illuminant, for example D50.

## Decision

Measured colors are stored in CIE Lab with a D65 white point, converted from sRGB-encoded, chart-corrected values. Delta E is the CIE76 distance (delta E*ab). Both live in one module, so changing the formula later touches one place and leaves stored Lab values valid.

## Reason

It is the simplest well-defined choice and it keeps the Lab values stored in measurements independent of the formula used to compare them.

## Consequences

Proposed only. The delta E thresholds (REQ-004, REQ-005) are in CIE76 units until the user confirms a different formula. CIE76 over-weights saturated colors, so CIEDE2000 may be preferable later.
