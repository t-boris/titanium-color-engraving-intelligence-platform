## Context

The operator asked for a filter "diameter < 1" on the colour map. The diameter of a colour's circle (DEC-084) is its confidence: radius 4 for an engraving never repeated, x1.5 for each repeat with the same colour, /1.5 for each engraving with another colour, between 2 and 14. It has no unit of its own.

## Alternatives

1. A fixed radius in pixels (not meaningful: the circle keeps its size on the screen at every zoom)
2. A confidence threshold such as 0.3 (a new number to choose; a colour engraved once has no confidence at all)
3. Hide only contradicted recipes (2k <= n): leaves out less, and a recipe with one of three agreeing would stay although its circle is smaller than a single engraving's

## Decision

One diameter is the circle of one engraving never repeated. A colour is doubtful, and the filter leaves it out, when its circle is smaller: agreeing - 1 - differing < 0 (agreement.doubtful). Colours engraved once, and colours with at least as many agreeing engravings as differing ones plus one, stay.

## Reason

It is the only reading in which the number is relative to something the screen already shows, it needs no new threshold, and it removes exactly the colours that changed under the same settings more often than they were confirmed.

## Consequences

The filter is a checkbox, off by default, remembered with the other filters. The 20 of 610 colours of the real map that are doubtful today are all colours of two engravings that disagree.
