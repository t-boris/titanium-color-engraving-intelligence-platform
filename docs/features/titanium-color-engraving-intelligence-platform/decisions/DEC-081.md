## Context

Until 0.9.0 the vector work folded the menu away, 0.10.0 first copied this to four more pages, and the operator found the vanishing menu confusing: it differs from page to page.

## Alternatives

1. Only a 'Меню' button at the left edge with no rail (rejected by the operator's choice)
2. The old 238 px menu on every page except the one-screen pages (rejected: the menu vanished from the pages the operator opens most)
3. Keep folding the menu away on the one-screen pages (rejected: that was the complaint)

## Decision

On every screen of the application the sections hang at the left edge as a 56 px rail of icons (fixed, always visible, the current section marked). The button at the top of the rail, and 'Меню' in the header below 1000 px, opens the section names as a popup over the page; the page does not move. The popup closes by a click outside it, by Esc and by choosing a section. The rule that folded the menu away on 'workspace' pages (body.is-workspace) is removed; 'workspace' now only removes the width limit of the page. This replaces the side-menu sentences of DEC-080 (it said the menu folds away on the colour map, the experiment page, the colour model and the knowledge page and stays on the experiments list).

## Reason

Operator, 2026-10-19, when the colour map opened: 'Когда открываю карту цветов - меню исчезает'; then 'Пусть вообще оно будет висеть слева и открывается в попапе' and 'короче пусть везде будет так'. Chosen from two options: a strip with icons plus the popup, or only a 'Меню' button; applied to the whole application, including the vector work screen.

## Consequences

The page content starts 56 px from the left edge everywhere (the old menu took 238 px, so every page gains 182 px of width). Below 1000 px there is no rail: the menu is the 'Меню' button in the header and the same popup. The sidebar note moved into the popup. Tests that pinned 'workspace' on the colour map, experiment and model pages and the is-workspace menu rules were changed to the new rule.
