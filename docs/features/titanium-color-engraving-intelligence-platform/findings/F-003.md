## Finding

DEC-011 excludes questioned production colors from new engraving plans by default, but REQ-005 implements that exclusion only for Discrete and Color-path plans. Tonal plans can therefore silently use questioned parameter sets despite the accepted protection rule. Apply the exclusion and explicit per-plan override to Tonal plans as well.

> A questioned color is excluded from new Discrete Color Engraving and Color-path Photo Engraving plans by default; the user may explicitly include it in a specific plan, confirm it (clear the flag) or demote it manually.

## Resolution

Разрешить Tonal использовать параметры под вопросом с предупреждением. Это сохраняет доступный тональный диапазон, но допускает использование нестабильных параметров и ослабляет ранее принятое правило защиты. — see DEC-018.
