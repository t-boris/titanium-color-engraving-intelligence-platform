## Statement

The operator keeps photo engravings as works, as vector works are kept ("мне нужны работы, так же как и в векторной гравировке", 2026-10-22). The section «Фотогравировка» opens on the list of the operator's photo works. A work is a picture engraved in LightBurn's grayscale mode on one measured grayscale scale, saved with the scale (laser, lens, titanium and the fixed settings), Min and Max power, the width on the titanium and whether the greys are chosen for the picture's colours. A work opens on one screen: the scales to choose from, the picture beside its predicted look on the titanium, Min and Max, the width, the time and the LightBurn file, and tabs with the scale's details. Every change is saved in the work and the prediction follows it. The measured scales stay available as their own page («Шкалы серого»).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] «Фотогравировка» opens on «Мои работы»: each work shows its predicted look on the titanium, its name with a rename pencil, its size, its scale settings, Min and Max, its engraving time and when it last changed, with «Открыть», «Сделать копию» and «Удалить» (with a confirmation).
- [ ] «Новая работа» asks for the picture, a name (the file name by default) and the width; the work starts on the best measured scale at that scale's measured Min and Max, and opens.
- [ ] Without any measured scale a new work is refused with the reason and the way to measure one (the plate «Шкалы серого»).
- [ ] In an open work, choosing another scale keeps the picture, sets Min and Max to that scale's measured span, saves the work and shows the new prediction; changing Min, Max, the width or the colour choice saves the work and re-predicts; a late answer never replaces a newer one.
- [ ] Reopening the work, or reloading the page, shows the same scale, Min, Max, width and colour choice.
- [ ] «Файл LightBurn» downloads one grayscale Image layer with the work's scale settings, Min and Max and width, named after the work.
- [ ] «Сделать копию» makes a new work with its own copy of the picture and the same settings and opens it; the original is unchanged. Rename changes only the name. Delete removes the work from the list.
- [ ] A work whose scale is no longer measured still opens and says so, and another scale can be chosen.
- [ ] Photo works never appear in the experiments list unless photographed (they are works, not plates).
- [ ] The open work and the list use the full window width, and no text is cut at 1372 px or wider.
