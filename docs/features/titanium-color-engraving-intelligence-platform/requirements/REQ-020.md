## Statement

The system shall learn and retrieve evidence-backed relationships between a requested engraving task, source/geometry features, the complete LightBurn operation and hardware/material context, and the measured outcome. Supported task kinds shall include at least text, vector lines, filled vector regions, tonal images and color images. The task vocabulary may expand as experiments reveal other useful kinds.

Each measured record shall retain the physical laser, historical lens, titanium identity and stock/batch, source artifact, LightBurn file and every parsed or preserved setting. A recommendation shall identify its task, applicable material and hardware conditions, evidence records, sample count, confidence and known limits. Measurements remain immutable and distinct from predictions and recommendations. Results from unlike tasks, LightBurn operation modes, lenses or titanium stocks shall not be silently averaged together.

Metrics shall match what was actually measured. A calibrated photograph can support color and image-region measurements; line width, edge quality, text legibility and dimensional accuracy require suitable image calibration or an independent measurement. The system shall label unavailable metrics rather than infer them from laser settings alone.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] An experiment or engraving plan records the intended task kind and can preserve task-specific goals such as target color, tonal behavior, line width or text legibility where applicable.
- [x] A measured outcome is linked to its complete LightBurn layer settings, original job file, source image, laser, historical lens, titanium stock and experiment identity.
- [x] Search/recommendation can filter by task and relevant operating context and returns observed evidence separately from predicted outcomes.
- [x] Recommendations include links to supporting observations and disclose sample counts, repeatability/confidence and missing context.
- [x] Data from unlike tasks, operation modes, lenses or titanium stocks cannot silently contribute to one result summary.
- [x] Every reported quality metric has a measurement method and calibration basis; unsupported metrics are shown as unavailable.
- [x] A successfully aligned RAW pair appears on the color map as provisional, same-photo evidence before acceptance; it remains excluded from standardized predictions until accepted with its layer masks reviewed.
- [x] New task kinds and measured result features can be added without changing the meaning of existing records.
