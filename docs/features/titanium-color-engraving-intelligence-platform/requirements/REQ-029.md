## Statement

The system shall show every measured colour of every kept experiment on one map with the settings that gave it and where it came from, and shall find, for a wanted colour, the recipes that gave the nearest colours.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] One screen shows all measured colours, with filters for laser and lens, material and how the colour was measured
- [x] Each colour shows its settings (power, frequency, speed, pulse width, line interval, passes), its experiment, layer, photograph and date, and links to them
- [x] A wanted colour, chosen on a palette or entered as Lab or taken from the map, returns the nearest recipes, one entry per recipe, with the distance, how often it came out and from which experiments
- [x] A colour measured without a chart is shown against the clean titanium of its own photograph through the ratio in linear light and is marked approximate; the same appearance at another exposure lands on the same place
- [x] A colour with no clean reference is counted and left off the map unless asked for; service layers and deleted experiments are not colours
- [x] The finished-work cleaning layer is recognised (first layer, one filled rectangle enclosing everything else), also for works accepted earlier
