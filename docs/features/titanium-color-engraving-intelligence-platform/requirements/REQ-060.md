## Statement

The system shall estimate how long every work engraves ("3 hours or 15 minutes") from its LightBurn file: each plate and plan page from the file it exports, each finished work from the file that was engraved, and each picture on a grayscale scale for the chosen width, with the time of every layer and what the estimate leaves out.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An experiment page shows "Время гравировки ≈ …" for a plate (from its exported file) and for a finished work (from its stored LightBurn file), with the time of every layer.
- [ ] A plan page shows the time of its exported file; a vector job's time is the one of its own screen, and its photographed works show the time of the file that was engraved.
- [ ] The photo-engraving screen shows the time of a picture for the chosen width and scale, and the scale cards show seconds per square centimetre.
- [ ] The time counts each layer whose output is on, its area (or, in Line mode, the length of its paths), speed, interval, passes, global repeats, direction, cross-hatch and the pieces of scan line its shapes make (DEC-101); a layer whose time is not known is listed with the reason and the total says it is incomplete.
- [ ] Paths that take their vertex or primitive list from an earlier path by VertID / PrimID are read whole (DEC-102).
- [ ] Timed against the workshop's jobs: Inna + Boris at 150 mm (about 3 h) estimates 2.9-3.3 h; at 100 mm (1.5-2 h) 1.5-1.65 h.
