## Statement

The system shall decide from the picture of an image layer which part of the work that layer actually engraves, measure the vector layers of a mixed work wherever the image layer does not engrave over them, report the composition of the work (which area is photo engraving, which is fill, which is both, which is neither), and describe each layer's visible area by its median colour and its spread.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The area an image layer engraves is computed from its picture and mode (transparent pixels never engrave; in a binary mode white never engraves; in grayscale mode white engraves at Min power), not taken as the rectangle of its bitmap
- [ ] A layer burned before an image layer is reported as engraved again only where the image layer engraves; the rest of its area is measured
- [ ] On the operator's real works Joker and girl art deco, checked on a copy of the data with their finished-work photographs, the vector layers are measured (before: 0 of 7 and 0 of 6)
- [ ] Every check reports the composition of the file: for each layer its kind (cleaning, fill, line, photo tonal, photo depth), area and visible share; and the share of the picture area that is photo engraving only, fill only, photo over fill, or untouched
- [ ] The tonal profile of an image layer is built from the part of each bitmap object that is opaque, aligned and not engraved over later; one profile per bitmap object
- [ ] The median colour of every measured layer comes with the spread of its visible area (robust, L*); a photo layer that is flat or a fill that varies like a picture is named, with a threshold taken from the real works' numbers
- [ ] The review and experiment screens show the composition and the spread, with every new message translated
