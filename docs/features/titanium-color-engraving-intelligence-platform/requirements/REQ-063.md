## Statement

The system shall let the operator undo and redo every change of what a photo work engraves (scale, Min and Max, width, colour choice, the rules of the parts, an AI answer), as the vector editor does, and ask the AI either to refine the present rules or to start again from the plain picture; every part can be put back as it is at once.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] «Отменить» and «Повторить» in the head of a photo work undo and redo one change at a time (up to 50), also with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z outside text fields; they are off when there is nothing to undo or redo.
- [ ] After an undo or redo the scale, Min and Max sliders, width, colour choice, rules and the AI's note on screen are those of the work.
- [ ] The permission to send the picture to the AI is not a step: undo never turns it back on.
- [ ] A new change after an undo drops what could be redone; a change to nothing is no step; a copy starts without history.
- [ ] «Попросить AI» sends the present rules and the AI changes what the wish asks; «Заново с картинки» sends no rules and the answer replaces them all.
- [ ] «Все части как есть» removes every rule of the parts.
