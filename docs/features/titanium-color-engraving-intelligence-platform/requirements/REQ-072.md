## Statement

The system shall find the objects of a photo work's picture (background pieces, hair, face and body skin, clothes, other things worn such as glasses, lips, eyes), let the operator give each one a main colour of the work's scale with lighter/darker by a click, by a theme (warm, cool, contrast), at random or by asking the AI, engrave each coloured object in its own stretch of the scale around that colour in the preview and the file alike, and keep the colours in the work with undo.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The «Раскраска» tab lists the objects with their share and colour in the photograph, why lips or eyes are missing, and the picture with the objects tinted
- [ ] Choosing an object and clicking a colour of the scale colours it; the work switches to «Раскраска по объектам»; the other objects stay as «Сохранить рисунок»
- [ ] A coloured object's greys are only those of the path through its main colour (steps of at most 5 dE); its own light and dark keep their order; «светлее/темнее» moves its tones
- [ ] «Тёплая», «Холодная», «Контрастная» and «Случайно» colour every object in one step; «Случайно» gives other colours each time; «Всё — авто» clears them
- [ ] «Раскрасить по просьбе» sends the wish, the objects and the scale's colours (the picture only when allowed); an answer with a colour that is not among them is sent back once, a second bad answer changes nothing; the AI's note is shown
- [ ] Every change is undone and redone; a copy of the work keeps the colours; the LightBurn file engraves the same greys as the preview
- [ ] A dot kind refuses colouring by objects and says why; a work made before 0.21.0 opens with no object coloured
