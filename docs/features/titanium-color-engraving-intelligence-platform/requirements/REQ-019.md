## Statement

The system shall retain replaceable lens records and snapshot historical lens configuration on plates and plans. Focal length and real spot diameter shall remain unknown until supported by documentation or measurement. The system may calculate a labelled spot estimate from sufficient inputs, with an operator measurement taking priority; unknown spot inputs shall not prevent parameter-based experimentation but shall leave dependent physical estimates unavailable. Lens changes shall remain visible and shall not silently merge calibration knowledge.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A laser has at most one installed lens; a lens record may remain incomplete with unknown focal length or spot diameter rather than fabricated values.
- [ ] A measured spot diameter takes priority over a calculated estimate; estimates and measurements are labelled separately.
- [ ] A spot estimate is available only with the required lens and beam inputs. If M2 is unknown, a displayed bound is reference-only.
- [ ] Unknown focal length or effective spot diameter leaves dependent spot, fluence and overlap figures unavailable while independent operating-point figures remain available.
- [ ] Known lens and controller fields constrain the work field; a conflict is flagged.
- [ ] Every new plate and plan snapshots the installed lens configuration and available spot value with its basis; later edits or replacement do not rewrite that snapshot.
- [ ] Ingestion warns if the currently installed lens differs from the linked plate's lens; comparable knowledge remains separated by the historical configuration.
- [ ] An installed lens cannot be deleted; existing plates without lens evidence retain unknown historical lens context.
