## Statement

The system shall store lenses as records (focal length, field, beam expansion) that can be replaced on a laser, derive from the installed lens the focused spot diameter and the work field, and record on every plate the lens it was made for, so that a change of lens is visible and results from different lenses are not silently treated as comparable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A lens has a name, a focal length, an optional field size and a beam expansion (1 when there is none); several lenses can be stored and exactly one can be installed on a laser at a time.
- [ ] The spot diameter of the installed lens is calculated as 4 x M2 x wavelength x focal length / (pi x beam diameter at the lens) when the lens, the beam diameter, the wavelength and M2 are known; a spot diameter measured by the operator takes priority.
- [ ] With the lens and the beam known but M2 unknown, only a lower bound (M2 = 1) is shown and it is not used in any calculation.
- [ ] The work field is the smaller of the lens field and the confirmed laser-settings field; a settings field larger than the lens field is flagged.
- [ ] Fluence and pulse overlap in operating points use the effective spot diameter and state whether it is measured or calculated.
- [ ] Every new plate and its plan store a snapshot of the installed lens, so editing or replacing the lens later does not change a stored plate.
- [ ] Checking a photographed pair warns when the lens installed now differs from the lens of the plate.
- [ ] A lens installed on a laser cannot be deleted; the open questions of a laser ask for the lens, or for M2, until the spot diameter is known.
