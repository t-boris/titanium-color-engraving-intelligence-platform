## Statement

The system shall let the operator of a photo work in a dot kind (Jarvis, Atkinson, Stucki and the others) open the colour map from the work, pick any measured colour of the work's laser, lens and titanium that can be a dot, and have every dot fire at that colour's settings: a fill's power, speed, frequency, Q-pulse and passes with its line step as the dots' pitch, or a grey scale's tone on that scale at its power. The work, its look, its close-up, its time, its LightBurn file and the ramp of its kind use those settings, the colour is taken at the work's width, and the dots can go back to the work's scale.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A work in a dot kind shows «Цвет с карты»; it opens the colour map of the work's laser and lens with a strip naming the work, its kind and width, what can be picked and a way back without a change
- [ ] On that map a colour that can be the dots shows «Точки этим цветом» right under the picked colour and in each search result, with the settings the dots will take; a colour that cannot be the dots is paler on the map and says why: another titanium, a check not accepted yet, a line, a picture's own tone, a tone of a dot engraving, a cross-hatched fill, a step finer than 0.0085 mm, a scale not measured on the work's titanium or a power outside it
- [ ] A picked fill makes the dots fire at its settings: the work shows its colour at the work's width (between, at or outside the row lengths it was measured at), the settings and DPI of the fill, and «Другой цвет» and «На шкалу»; the file's Image layer has the kind's ditherMode, Min = Max = the fill's power and the fill's speed, frequency, Q-pulse, step and DPI
- [ ] A picked tone of a grey scale puts the work on that scale with the dots at the tone's power
- [ ] «На шкалу», a power on the scale's strip, another scale or undo puts the dots back on the scale; redo brings the picked colour back
- [ ] A new width takes the fill's colour at that width again; a fill no longer measured keeps the colour it had when picked, marked, and the work still opens and exports
- [ ] «Проба этого вида» of a work with a picked fill is a ramp of the kind at the fill's settings; once photographed and accepted its measured tones replace the estimate
- [ ] Opening the map to pick takes one request; the map's own time is not made longer by more than 0.1 s
