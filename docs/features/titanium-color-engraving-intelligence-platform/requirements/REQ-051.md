## Statement

The system shall let the operator replace the titanium colour of any source colour of a vector work from the colour editor itself, choosing from every measured colour of the laser, lens and titanium; shall show for every titanium colour which engravings it is known from (tests or works, with links) and how stable it is (confirmed by repeats, engraved once, or doubtful); shall let the operator order the choice by closeness, stability or speed and show only the repeated colours; shall offer colours more stable than an unconfirmed one, nearest to it first; and shall call leaving the areas of a colour out of the file by what it does.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every row of the editor's source colours has a Replace button (not on an excluded colour) that opens the window of the colour table, and a hand-chosen colour can be returned to the automatic choice
- [ ] Every engraving behind a colour says whether it was a test (a calibration plate, a laser capability test, a parameter matrix) or a work (a vector work or any other finished work), with a link to its experiment, its analysis and, for a vector work, the work
- [ ] A colour says whether it is confirmed by repeats (two or more engravings agree), engraved once, or doubtful (the engravings disagree); the same words and marks are on the swatches of the palette, the picker, the colour table and the active colour of the brush
- [ ] The picker orders by closeness to the source colour, by stability or by speed, and can show only the colours confirmed by repeats
- [ ] When the current colour is not confirmed by repeats, the picker offers the confirmed colours nearest to it
- [ ] Leaving a colour or an area out of the file is called Not engrave / Engrave, with a hint that the areas stay unengraved and only the cleaning remains
- [ ] The colour map shows the same kind of each source
