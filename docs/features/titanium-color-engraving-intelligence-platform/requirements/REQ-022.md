## Statement

The system shall periodically review accepted experiment and engraving-result records. It shall identify evidence-backed improvements, useful discoveries, unstable results and gaps in task/material coverage. Each finding shall state what data supports it, what it compares, the sample count and uncertainty; hypotheses and AI-generated explanations shall remain visibly distinct from measured conclusions.

When useful evidence is missing, the system shall propose a targeted follow-up experiment. A proposal shall name the question or hypothesis, explain why the result is valuable, list candidate LightBurn settings and state which task, laser/lens and titanium stock it covers. Proposals shall be stored and linked to any generated test plate and its eventual result. Creating a test plate shall place the configured surface-cleaning operation before its measurement/test geometry.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] New accepted measurements can trigger analysis, and the operator can also run or schedule periodic analysis.
- [ ] Findings include links to their source records, task and material context, sample counts and uncertainty/limitations.
- [ ] The system distinguishes a measured improvement from a hypothesis or exploratory anomaly.
- [ ] Proposed experiments target a stated evidence gap or uncertainty and include a human-readable reason for each selected setting.
- [ ] An accepted proposal can generate a LightBurn test plate, with the configured cleaning pass first, and the resulting photo/job pair links back to the proposal.
- [ ] As evidence accumulates, prior findings can be recalculated while retaining their history and provenance.
