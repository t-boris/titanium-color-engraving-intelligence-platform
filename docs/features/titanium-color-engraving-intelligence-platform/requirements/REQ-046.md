## Statement

The system shall let the operator zoom the colour map in and out and move it in every direction, with the mouse wheel, by dragging, with buttons and the keyboard, and with two fingers on a touch screen, without changing the size that tells the confidence of a colour.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The wheel zooms about the cursor between the whole map and 60 times; a sideways wheel or shift with the wheel moves the map
- [x] Dragging moves the map and a drag that starts on a colour does not pick it; a plain click still picks it
- [x] Buttons + and − zoom about the middle, «Весь вид» returns to the whole map, the arrow keys move it, + and − zoom it, 0 resets it
- [ ] A pinch with two fingers zooms and one finger moves the map on a touch screen
- [x] The picture cannot be moved out of its frame; the axis names stay where they are
- [x] A circle keeps its size on the screen when the map is zoomed (its diameter tells the confidence of its settings, DEC-084)
- [x] Picking a colour or searching again keeps the view of the plane; the scale is shown next to the buttons
