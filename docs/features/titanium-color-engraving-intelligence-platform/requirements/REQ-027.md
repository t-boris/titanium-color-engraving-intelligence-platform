## Statement

The system shall align the photograph of a work engraved from a picture to the picture stored in its LightBurn file, find what the frame hides, and report how the grey levels of the picture came out as power-dependent colour.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] All 8 orientations of the picture are tried; the registration is refused unless the best beats the second by 1.4 times
- [x] The correlation of the edge maps over the three Lab channels after refinement must reach 0.25, otherwise the registration is refused with a reason
- [x] The edge of the frame is found per side from the step of the median level; the margins hidden by the frame are reported in millimetres and the visible window limits the measured surface
- [x] When the edge is not found on a side a margin of 6 percent is assumed there and the check says so
- [x] The tonal profile lists per grey level the implied power (linear between Min and Max power for grayscale mode), L*, a*, b* and chroma, with rank correlations of each with the grey level
- [x] On a synthetic photo engraving with a known pose the registration agrees with the truth within 1 mm and the hidden margins within 1.5 mm
- [x] A file without a picture, a photograph of something else and a picture without orientation are refused, never guessed
