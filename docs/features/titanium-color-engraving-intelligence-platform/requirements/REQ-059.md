## Statement

The system shall have "Фотогравировка" as the fourth section of the menu and, for grayscale mode (all settings fixed except Min/Max power), learn from the operator's own grayscale engravings which grey makes which colour on titanium: show each measured scale, the colours the chosen Min/Max give to every grey, how the operator's engravings confirm it, a preview of any picture on the scale with its engraving time, a LightBurn file with the picture on an Image layer, and a grey-ramp plate that measures new scales.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The menu reads: Эксперименты, Карта цветов, Векторная работа, Фотогравировка, then the rest; "Цветная гравировка" is no longer in the menu and its address still works; the banded tonal form and the preview by LightBurn file are reachable from the section.
- [ ] A scale is one grayscale mode (laser, lens, titanium, speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval, passes) with its measured power -> colour points from grayscale Image engravings only (photographs and grey ramps); scales are listed best first (chart-referenced, most engravings).
- [ ] For a chosen Min/Max inside the measured span the screen shows the grey -> colour bar, a table of greys 255...0 with power and colour, and where each point was measured.
- [ ] The confirmation compares every engraving with the curve of the other engravings (leave one out, tolerance 5 dE) and says whether the scale is confirmed and where it is not.
- [ ] Fills with the settings of a scale are shown beside it as a reference with their dE, never as points of the curve (DEC-100).
- [ ] An uploaded picture (PNG/JPG) is shown next to its predicted engraving at the chosen Min/Max and width, with its engraving time; "подобрать серые под её цвета" replaces each pixel by the grey whose colour on the scale is nearest.
- [ ] The downloaded LightBurn file holds the picture on a CutSetting_Img layer with the scale's settings, Min/Max, interval and dpi = 25.4 / interval, stored bottom row first as LightBurn stores it; the platform's reader reads it back upright and equal to the upload.
- [ ] The plate "Шкалы серого" puts one Image layer per tile with a 16-step ramp from white to black; rows and columns are chosen from speed, line interval, frequency, pulse width, passes (default rows: interval 0.0127 / 0.0169 / 0.0212 / 0.0254 mm); its photograph is measured step by step at the power of each grey and every tile becomes a scale.
