## Statement

The system shall align photographs to engraving geometry, measure visible outcomes with explicit calibration and quality checks, and distinguish chart-corrected evidence from approximate camera-relative appearance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The standard capture workflow records a fixed rig, controlled lighting and viewing angle, RAW capture and a reference chart in every frame. Detectable charts are used for color correction before device-independent color measurement.
- [ ] Photographs without a detectable chart are visibly approximate and excluded from model updates and validation. Where clean titanium is visible, camera-relative appearance may still be reported using an exposure-normalized comparison to that reference.
- [ ] Generated calibration plates use their markers and machine-readable identifier for automatic geometry recovery and cell association. Finished-work plans optionally include markers and an identifier outside the image area.
- [ ] Unmarked vector works can be aligned from distinctive element matches. All eight rotation/mirror orientations are evaluated; ambiguous orientation, insufficient or poorly distributed anchors, and excessive residual error refuse automatic registration and leave manual four-corner placement available.
- [ ] Alignment records retain the method, orientation confidence, anchor counts, residual error in millimetres and each element's status: anchor, confirmed, computed, partly hidden or hidden.
- [ ] Photo engravings can be aligned against the picture embedded in the LightBurn file across all eight orientations. Registration must satisfy defined orientation-separation and image-agreement thresholds, otherwise it is refused with a reason.
- [ ] Visible frame windows and hidden margins are recorded and excluded from measurement masks. Assumed margins are explicitly distinguished from detected margins; wholly hidden elements are not measured.
- [ ] Synthetic photo-engraving registration with known pose is accurate within 1 mm and hidden-margin estimates within 1.5 mm.
- [ ] The operator can review alignment overlays and measurement masks before accepting an observation. Region measurements exclude edges, holes, obscured pixels and service geometry.
- [ ] Image layers report engraving mode, depth-versus-tonal classification, resolution, dot settings, cleanup, passes, sub-layers and picture processing.
- [ ] For an aligned embedded image with sufficient visible pixels, the system reports measured L*, a*, b* and chroma by stored grey level, tonal range, rank correlations, selected picture orientation and evidence strength. Missing pictures or insufficient pixels produce no invented profile.
- [ ] For grayscale mode, tonal reports show implied power linearly from minimum power at the lightest level to maximum power at the darkest. Reports distinguish stored grey levels from unmodeled processing and dithering; the layer's median color remains available.
