## Statement

The system shall describe every image mode LightBurn offers (what the grey level controls, which settings matter, whether a pixel is struck), show that description with every image layer, and predict how a picture will look at given settings from tonal profiles and power curves measured in the same mode and settings, naming how far independent engravings disagree and refusing with the reason where nothing was measured.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] One catalogue states, for grayscale, threshold, ordered, atkinson, dither, stucki, jarvis, newsprint, halftone, sketch and 3dslice: the family, what the grey level controls (power, dot density, edges, depth), the settings that matter, and what the platform can predict
- [ ] Every image layer of a file shows its mode in words from that catalogue with its dpi, line interval, dot width, Min and Max power and the other settings the file states; no mode of the operator's files has a missing label
- [ ] The tags of image layers the file may carry (negative, pass-through, cells per inch, halftone angle, overscan) are read and shown, not reported as unknown
- [ ] The prediction of a dithered mode reads the colour of a grey level from tonal profiles measured in that mode at those settings; without such a measurement it is refused with the reason
- [ ] Each point of a prediction curve carries how far the independent engravings that measured it disagree, and the preview names the share of the picture that lies in powers where they disagree by more than the tolerance
- [ ] On the two real grayscale photo engravings that share one mode (Girl on a bike, Motorcycle) the prediction of each from the other is reported with its error by power range
