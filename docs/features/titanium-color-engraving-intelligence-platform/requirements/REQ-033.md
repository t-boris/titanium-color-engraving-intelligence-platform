## Statement

The system shall split a photograph into at most the chosen number of colour regions (up to 29, 28 with registration markers), following the edges of the picture, with every region touching its neighbours without gaps or overlaps, giving the most detail to eyes and lips of faces and to areas the operator marks, and shall show the result before any colour is assigned.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The operator chooses the number of colours from 2 to 29 (28 when registration markers are included); the result never has more colours than chosen
- [ ] The union of the regions covers the whole picture and two regions share only their common border: rasterised, the uncovered and the doubly covered areas are each below 0.5 % of the picture
- [ ] Every border between two regions is one shared curve, smoothed once and used by both regions; holes are kept
- [ ] Faces are found automatically; eyes and lips get finer regions than the rest of the face; a picture without a face is split normally and says that no face was found
- [ ] The operator can mark an important area by hand (and remove a marked or detected area); it is split more finely when the picture is split again
- [ ] Regions smaller than the minimum area are merged into the neighbour of the nearest colour, except inside important areas
- [ ] The split result is shown beside the original, each region filled with its source colour, before titanium colours are assigned
- [ ] The same picture and settings give the same regions
