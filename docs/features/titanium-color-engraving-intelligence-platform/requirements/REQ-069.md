## Statement

The system shall treat every titanium stock record of the same family and grade as one titanium for grey scales, their curves, the LightBurn file preview, photo works, the vector palette and the colour map, whatever the records' names, sizes, thicknesses, batches or surfaces; a record without a grade shall be only itself.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A photo work's file previewed on «Small Plate» (Grade 5) is predicted from a grey scale engraved on «Big Plate 150» (Grade 5)
- [ ] A scale engraved on two plates of one grade is listed once, with the engravings of both, and says «Титан Grade 5 · измерена на <plates>»
- [ ] A photo work moved to another plate of its scale's grade keeps that scale; an engraving of it on that plate counts in the scale and is checked as the work's scale
- [ ] A plate of another grade, and a record without a grade, never take points of this titanium; choosing such a plate for the scale is refused
- [ ] In the colour map one recipe on two plates of one grade is one entry with both engravings; the «Материал» filter offers the grade; each point still names its plate
- [ ] Palette entry ids, and so the colours kept by vector works, do not change
- [ ] When the preview refuses a layer, it lists where its mode is measured (laser, lens, one plate per titanium) with «Показать на нём»
