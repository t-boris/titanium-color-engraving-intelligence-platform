## Statement

The system shall let the operator hide the colours of the colour map whose circle is smaller than that of one engraving never repeated, shall show the confidence of a colour whenever the operator picks it (on the map and in the search results), and shall show where the colour is known from: every independent engraving behind it, whether it agrees with the colour of the settings, and where to look at it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A colour is doubtful when its circle (DEC-084: 4 x 1.5^(agreeing - 1 - differing)) is smaller than that of one engraving never repeated, that is when agreeing - 1 - differing < 0; a colour engraved once is not doubtful
- [ ] The colour map and its search take a filter that leaves out doubtful colours; the remembered filter is carried by the next questions; the number left out is told in the head of the screen and, for the search, in a message with a button that shows them again
- [ ] Without the filter doubtful colours stay on the map, are counted, and are marked 'doubtful' where they are described
- [ ] A picked colour on the map and every found recipe show the confidence (the circle at the size the map draws, the number, how many engravings agree, the warning when the colour changed under the same settings) or say that it is not assessed because the engraving was never repeated
- [ ] A picked colour and every found recipe list the independent engravings it is known from, newest first: the experiment and photographs, date, layers, the colour measured, how far it is from the colour of the settings, whether it agrees, how it was measured, with links to the experiment and the analysis; a recipe engraved once says it is one engraving
- [ ] The colour of the settings is shown with the engravings when there are several, and the limit within which engravings agree is told
- [ ] Counts and the filter work on the real data in under 0.5 s
