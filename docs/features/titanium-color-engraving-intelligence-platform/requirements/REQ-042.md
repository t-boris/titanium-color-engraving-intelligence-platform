## Statement

The system shall turn source images into editable, task-specific engraving plans, with gapless vector artwork, evidence-aware color assignment and operator review before export.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An input image, intended task and physical size produce an editable internal EngravingPlan. Strategies include text, line art, filled/color artwork, tonal photo and color-path photo; source images, geometry, task assignments, settings and exports remain linked.
- [ ] Discrete Color Engraving and Continuous Photo Engraving are independent workflows. Continuous engraving offers Tonal and Color-path modes under one calibrated transfer-function abstraction.
- [ ] Vector preparation follows image edges and produces at most the chosen 2–29 color groups, reduced to at most 28 when registration is enabled and further limited by other required output layers.
- [ ] Vector regions preserve holes and shared borders. Rasterized uncovered and doubly covered areas are each below 0.5% of the picture.
- [ ] Detected faces prioritize eyes and lips; the operator can add or remove important areas and regenerate the split. No-face results say so. Small regions merge into a neighboring region of similar color except where important-area detail must be retained.
- [ ] The split is previewed before titanium colors are assigned. The operator can revise geometry and task assignments before export.
- [ ] Discrete matching uses eligible validated/production colors for the selected hardware and titanium context, honoring time filters. Initial assignment selects the fastest recipe within the visible, configurable ΔE tolerance.
- [ ] When no eligible color meets the tolerance, the nearest color may be shown as a proposed substitute, but the source color remains unreachable until the operator explicitly substitutes or excludes it. A plan with unresolved unreachable colors cannot be finalized.
- [ ] The operator can recolor a whole group or individual region. Automatic variants support closest-to-original, preserved lightness order, restorable random variants and fewer layers; all variants are previewable and reversible.
- [ ] The preview compares the original, split and titanium-colored result, showing recipes, measurement basis, independent engraving counts, color error, confidence, stability and areas without adequate evidence.
- [ ] Every exported region has a recipe and complete applicable settings. The whole work's estimated engraving time is shown before export.
