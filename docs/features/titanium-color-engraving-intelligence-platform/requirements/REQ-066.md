## Statement

The system shall keep the row length of every colour measurement and shall predict the colours of a photo work at its width and of each vector work layer at the row length of its regions, saying whether that size was measured, lies between measured sizes or outside them, and shall offer plates that measure colours at chosen sizes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A measured grey ramp, picture or fill keeps the row length read from its pair's LightBurn file; sizes within 10 % are one size
- [ ] A photo work's preview, colour choice, rules, time and file use the scale's colours at the work's width: measured there, interpolated in log length between two measured widths, or the nearest width outside them, and the work says which
- [ ] Changing a photo work's width redraws its prediction at the new width
- [ ] An engraving is compared only with engravings at or around its own width; at another width it is not compared and is not a failed confirmation; the count of engravings named is the count the prediction used
- [ ] The grey-scales page shows the widths each scale was measured at and the scale at each of them
- [ ] «Проба на ширине работы» creates a one-ramp grey plate as wide as the work at its scale's settings, Min and Max, and opens it; it is refused when the laser carries another lens
- [ ] The grey-ramp plate has a ramp-width axis; a fill-size plate engraves recipes as 4 mm strips of chosen widths; a plate may be up to 300 mm
- [ ] A vector work shows each layer in the colour its recipe gives at the row length of its regions in the editor, the list picture, delta E and the colours its file promises, and marks a layer whose size was not measured
- [ ] The automatic colour choice compares each source colour with each recipe's colour at the row length of that colour's regions
