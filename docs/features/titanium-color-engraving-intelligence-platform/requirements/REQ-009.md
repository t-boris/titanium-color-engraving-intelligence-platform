## Statement

Color measurement in the first version shall be based on photographs taken on a fixed rig (camera on a stand, controlled light and viewing angle, RAW capture) with a color reference chart in every frame. The system shall use the chart to color-correct each photo before measuring cells, and shall reject or flag photos where markers or the chart cannot be detected.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every ingested plate photo is associated with a capture setup record (rig, light, angle) and a detected reference chart.
- [ ] Cell colors are reported in a device-independent color space after chart-based correction.
- [ ] Photos without detectable registration markers or reference chart are flagged and not used for model updates.
- [ ] Measurement repeatability of the rig (re-shooting the same plate) is tracked and shown as part of stability data.
