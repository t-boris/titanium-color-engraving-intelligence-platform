## Statement

The system shall present every experiment as one object that contains its plate, its engraving file, all its photographs with their checks, the measured result and its conclusions, shown from one list, with the photograph and its overlay always visible.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] One list shows experiments, each with a thumbnail of its latest photograph, its state, laser, lens and titanium; there is no separate list of plates, engravings or photographs
- [x] A plate with its checks, or a finished work with its re-photographs, is one experiment; a plate without photographs is an experiment waiting for a photograph
- [ ] Opening an experiment shows the photograph with the overlay first and without any action; the checks can be chosen, brightness raised and contours switched
- [x] Deleted plates and checks are not part of any experiment; the checks of a deleted plate stay as a finished work
- [x] The plate, the checks, the measured layers and the conclusions are on the experiment screen; old screens link back to their experiment
- [ ] The conclusions for the material stay on the list screen
