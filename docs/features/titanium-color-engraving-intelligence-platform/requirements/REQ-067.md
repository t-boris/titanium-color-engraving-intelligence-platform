## Statement

The system shall let a photo work chosen by colour keep the drawing of its picture (light and dark in their order over the lightness the scale reaches, then the nearest hue) or take the nearest colour, and shall draw as a 0.3 mm line each edge of the picture the predicted engraving would lose, the same in the preview and in the LightBurn file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A new photo work keeps the drawing and has contours on; a work saved before opens the same way
- [ ] With the drawing kept, a light skin whose nearest scale colour is a dark tan is engraved light, and lips darker than the skin stay darker
- [ ] In a flat area a grey whose colour changes fast with power gives way to a slower one at most 3 dE farther; in a busy area the nearest is taken
- [ ] An edge the prediction loses is drawn about 0.3 mm wide along it in the darkest grey (the lightest on a dark area or a light ridge) at any resolution; an edge kept, a part with an even rule, a speck under 1 mm are not drawn
- [ ] The colour choice and the contours are saved in the work, undone and redone; the work says how far the drawing is kept and what share is outlined
- [ ] The LightBurn file is the picture the preview shows, at the layer's resolution
