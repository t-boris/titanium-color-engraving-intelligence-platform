## Statement

When the drawing of the file and its largest picture have not found the work in the photograph, the system shall read the scale of a ruler or measuring angle lying beside the plate and look for every picture of the file at that scale, including the pictures of layers whose output is switched off, and shall accept the position at which the pictures agree; which layer is found shall not matter, because all layers lie in one coordinate system.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The scale (pixels per mm) is read from millimetre ticks that also show their 5 mm and 10 mm ticks, and a photograph of an even hatch, of a rough surface or without a ruler gives no scale.
- [ ] Where two rulers meet at a right angle, the inner corner and the side the plate lies on are found, and the pictures are looked for on that side first and anywhere in the photograph if nothing is found there.
- [ ] Every picture of the file is searched in the eight orientations; the orientation in which the pictures agree on where the file's origin lies wins, and a single picture is believed only if it matches well and clearly better than in any other orientation.
- [ ] The pictures of layers whose output is switched off are used to find the work and are still not measured; the operator is told which layers found the work and that those that are off are on the plate.
- [ ] The scale is tried as read, doubled and halved (a ruler may carry half-millimetre ticks); a scale at which a picture would be longer than the photograph is refused.
- [ ] A work found this way is not accepted unless every picture used correlates with the photograph after refinement and their corners agree with one transform to within 0.6 mm.
