## Statement

The system shall link the photograph of an engraved photo work to the work by the identifier its LightBurn file carries, and show on the work, for every accepted photograph, each grey of the picture: the colour the other engravings of those settings predict, the colour engraved and the difference, with a verdict as the scale's confirmation gives it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The LightBurn file of a photo work carries its identifier (TCE1:TCE-P-... in the Notes); a photograph uploaded with that file is linked to the work even when no work was chosen.
- [ ] The «Подтверждение» tab of a work starts with its engravings: «Загрузить фото гравировки» opens the upload with the work's laser, lens and titanium chosen, and every photograph of the work shows its state and the Min and Max in its file.
- [ ] Once accepted, a photograph shows each grey compared (grey, power, predicted colour, engraved colour, dE), the median, the largest difference, the share within 5 dE and «подтверждает прогноз» when the median is at most 5 dE; the prediction comes from the other engravings of the same settings (leave one out), as the scale's confirmation does it.
- [ ] When nothing can be compared the work says why: no clear colours, a chart on one side only, no other engraving of those settings, or powers outside the measured span; a photograph engraved with other settings than the work's scale is compared with engravings of its own settings and says so.
- [ ] An accepted photograph counts in its scale and in the scale's confirmation.
- [ ] The check, analysis and experiment screens of such a photograph name the work and open it; the experiment page offers the work and its own file, not plate actions.
