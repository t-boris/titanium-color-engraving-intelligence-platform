## Statement

The system shall render, for a grayscale-mode image layer of a LightBurn file, how the engraved picture will look on titanium for the layer's settings (Min and Max power, speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval, passes), from the measured relation of power to colour at those settings, and shall state what the prediction rests on and where it has no evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The grey level of the stored picture becomes power by DEC-057 (Min for the lightest, Max for the darkest, linearly between) and the power becomes a colour by linear interpolation in Lab between the neighbouring measured powers of the same mode (speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval, passes, material, setup)
- [ ] A power outside the measured span of the mode is not predicted: those pixels are hatched in the preview and their share is reported
- [ ] Every point of the curve carries its source (pair or plate, layer, referenced or camera-relative); a prediction built on camera-relative colours says so on the preview
- [ ] Picture adjustments of the file (gamma, contrast, brightness) are applied with standard formulas and marked as assumed; enhance and denoise are not modelled and are named
- [ ] Dithered modes (Stucki, Jarvis, threshold) and files without a picture are refused with a reason
- [ ] Changing Min or Max power on the screen re-renders the preview from the same curve without a new upload
- [ ] On a synthetic picture with a synthetic curve the rendered Lab of every pixel equals the curve's value at its power within 0.5 dE; the accepted Inna pair rendered through its own profile reproduces its measured bins
