## Statement

The system shall give every recipe (the same settings on the same laser, lens and titanium) a confidence in its colour that grows with every independent engraving that gives the same colour and falls with every one that gives another, shall take as the recipe's colour the colour its engravings agree on (a later redo replaces an older colour), and shall prefer the more confident colours when it chooses titanium colours for a work.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Engravings of one recipe whose colours differ by at most ΔE 5 agree; the colour of the recipe is the mean of the largest agreeing group and an engraving outside it does not move it
- [x] When two groups are equal in size the group with the newest engraving gives the colour: a recipe first yellow and engraved again dark brown becomes dark brown
- [x] Confidence rises with each further agreeing engraving (0.5, 0.6, 0.67 for two, three, four) and falls with each differing one; it is not assessed for one engraving
- [x] A recipe whose agreeing group is not a strict majority is shown as contradicted and is not chosen by the automatic modes while another colour exists (a work whose titanium has no other colour is still coloured with them, marked); the operator can still choose it by hand
- [x] In the palette of a vector work a colour measured on a worse basis than another colour of the same settings is shown as replaced and is not chosen automatically; saved works keep their ids
- [x] Within the delta E tolerance the automatic choice takes the more confident colour before the faster one
- [x] The colour map search measures a recipe's distance to its agreed colour and shows the confidence and how many engravings agree; the palette and the knowledge pages show the same counts
- [x] Measurements stay unchanged: confidence, agreement and replacement are derived on every read
- [x] On the colour map the diameter of a colour's circle follows the confidence of its recipe: a colour engraved once is middle, one whose repeats agree is larger the more there are, one whose colour changed is smaller; its title names the confidence and how many engravings agree
