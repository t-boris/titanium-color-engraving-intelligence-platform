## Statement

The system shall derive laser characterization from the device's passport, test report, waveforms, reliable controller settings and installed lens, and use it with the selected job settings in operating-point calculations and plate checks. Unverified or conflicting controller values shall remain excluded until resolved against evidence. Unknown inputs shall disable only the dependent estimates or checks and shall be visibly identified.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Characterization is recomputed from stored evidence on every read and labels each item's basis and calculation eligibility.
- [ ] Effective average power uses the unit's measured test-report value when within 0.5–1.5 times rated power, otherwise rated power.
- [ ] Only uncontested printed or explicitly confirmed controller limits narrow the laser's frequency range; contradictory limits are excluded and visibly flagged.
- [ ] Unresolved Max PWM does not become either 4000 kHz or 200 kHz as an asserted controller limit.
- [ ] Known field limits constrain plate size including markers; unknown limits are labelled unavailable rather than treated as a successful fit check.
- [ ] Frequency outside known controller limits produces a warning; frequency outside laser limits produces a problem; modeled pulse energy does not exceed the passport maximum.
- [x] Operating-point calculations use the plate or plan's pulse width, line interval and passes and remain labelled estimates, not measurements.
