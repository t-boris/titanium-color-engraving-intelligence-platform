## Statement

The system shall show the screens 'Эксперименты' and 'Карта цветов' and the pages inside them (one experiment, the colour model of one laser and lens, the knowledge of one colour) on one screen, so that the page does not need to be scrolled.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] At 1421 x 951 the page height equals the window height (document scrollHeight = innerHeight) on #/experiments (also with ?device=, with a filter that empties the list), #/colors in its default state, after a search, after a click on a point and with filters that leave few or no colours, on #/experiment/<key> on every tab for an experiment with a matrix, a finished work and one without a photo, on #/colors?device=&lens= for every set and tab, and on #/knowledge/<id>
- [x] Only a list or table too long for its panel scrolls inside it: the experiments list, the colour search results, the model table and the long tabs of the model and of the experiment's results
- [x] Experiments: the filtered list on the left, the compact working task over the findings for the material on the right; the four actions stay in the header
- [x] Colour map: filters and the folded explanation on the left, the projection switch, the lightness range and a chart that fills the remaining height in the centre, the colour search and its results on the right
- [x] Nothing was removed: every section, number, link and action of the earlier pages is in a tab, a panel or a fold-out (the old-section to new-place tables are in tasks/todo.md); the open tab or set is remembered by the browser; the empty states keep their text and links
- [x] The section menu is the icon rail of DEC-081 on all these pages (it never folds away); below 1100 px the columns stack and the page scrolls as before
- [x] The pages are covered by node tests (tests/test_experiments_screen_ui.py, test_color_map_one_screen.py, test_experiment_screen_ui.py, test_colour_model_ui.py, test_one_screen_frame.py)
