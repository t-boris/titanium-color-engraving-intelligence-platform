## Statement

The system shall represent physical lasers and replaceable lenses from evidence, preserve historical equipment context, and use only eligible equipment facts in calculations and compatibility checks.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] A device record stores identity, optional serial number, rated power, frequency limits, selectable pulse widths, pulse-energy limits, acceptance-test data, controller settings and original source documents. Source documents can be viewed and removed; charts derive from stored data.
- [x] Equipment values distinguish documented, handwritten/unverified, explicitly confirmed, conflicting and unknown states. Illegible or conflicting values remain unresolved and cannot constrain calculations until supported by evidence.
- [x] A laser has at most one installed lens. Lens records preserve available field, focal-length, beam and spot information without fabricated values; measured spot diameter takes precedence over a labelled estimate.
- [x] Every new plate and plan snapshots its device and historical lens configuration, including the available spot value and its basis. Later equipment edits do not rewrite historical records.
- [x] Power, frequency and speed are modeled calibration axes. Pulse width, line interval and passes are editable job settings recorded on each plate, plan and observation without requiring a laser profile.
- [x] Models, repetition counts, stability and production eligibility are separated by device, historical lens, task/operation mode, material and applicable job settings. Unknown conditions are not silently equated to known conditions; migrations retain original values and provenance.
- [x] Operating-point estimates and plate checks derive from current stored evidence and selected settings, with their basis shown. Unknown inputs disable only dependent estimates; unknown spot information does not prevent parameter-based experiments.
- [x] Average-power calculations use the unit's measured test value when it is within 0.5–1.5 times rated power, otherwise rated power. Modeled pulse energy never exceeds the documented maximum.
- [x] Unsupported pulse-width steps are refused with supported choices shown. Frequencies outside laser limits are identified as problems; frequencies outside reliable controller limits produce warnings.
- [x] Known lens and controller field limits constrain the full job footprint, including markers. Conflicts are flagged and unknown field limits are reported as unchecked.
