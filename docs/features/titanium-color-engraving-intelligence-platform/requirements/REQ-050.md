## Statement

The system shall not measure a layer of a LightBurn file that was not engraved (its output is switched off) or that the operator's file left where the exported file of the work had it while the rest of the file was moved or resized (a hidden layer, which LightBurn's tools skip), shall say so in a warning and a note on the layer, and shall not let such a layer take part in the alignment, the coverage of other layers, the knowledge or the review of the work.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A layer with its output switched off in the file is flagged left out with a note, is not measured, writes no observation on acceptance, and the check warns which layers were not engraved
- [ ] When the engraved file carries the identifier of a vector work, its layers are compared with the newest readable export of that work: the move (scale, offset and mirror of each axis) that most layers share is found, and a layer whose shapes sit where the export had them but not where that move puts them is left behind
- [ ] A layer that was edited (its shapes match neither place), a file that moved nothing, a file with fewer than three layers that agree on a move, and a plan without an export say nothing and leave every layer measured
- [ ] A deleted shape or two does not make a layer look left behind
- [ ] The check warns which layers stayed, by how much the rest was resized, and whether they are hidden in LightBurn; the warning and the notes have Russian translations
- [ ] A layer left out is not drawn on the overlay and its shapes do not take part in the alignment of the photograph: on the real work 22 the strays outside the plate are gone and the other layers' measurements move by less than 1 dE
