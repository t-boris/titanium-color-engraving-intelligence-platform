## Statement

The system shall, when a photo work keeps the drawing, lay the picture's tones along one smooth path of its scale so that a gradient of the picture is engraved as a gradient without spots and its features stay visible, and shall draw contours only on request and only on greys picked by colour.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The path runs from the scale's lightest grey to its darkest in power order, its lightness never rising, skipping a swing of hue at one lightness
- [ ] A gradient of the picture gets powers that only rise as it darkens; grain of a flat area does not turn into spots
- [ ] A light skin is engraved light and lips darker than the skin stay darker
- [ ] Contours are off for a new work and for a work saved before; the plain picture is never drawn on and its switch is disabled
- [ ] The preview, the LightBurn file and the work's text say the same: the path's lightness span, its greys and the order kept
