## Statement

The system shall ask «Какая гравировка?» when a photo work is made and show the work's picture as it would come out in every kind that colours titanium (grayscale and the dot kinds), keep the kind in the work with undo, give a dot kind one power picked on the scale strip, write the kind into the LightBurn file after the cleaning, and offer «Проба этого вида» whose photograph replaces the estimate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A new work opens with the dialog «Какая гравировка?»: one tile per kind (Градации серого, Порог, Упорядоченный, Аткинсон, Флойд–Стейнберг, Стаки, Джарвис, Газетный, Полутон, Эскиз), each with the work's picture in that kind; 3D-срез is not offered
- [ ] A dot kind's tile and preview say «оценка»: every dot in the scale's colour at the dot power, the cleaned titanium between the dots; the close-up shows the dots at the layer's DPI
- [ ] The kind and the dot power are kept in the work, undone and redone; the dot power is refused outside the scale's measured powers and returns to the default when it falls outside after a change of scale or width
- [ ] The «Вид» button switches the kind; for a dot kind a click on the scale strip sets the dot power
- [ ] The file of a dot kind has C00 cleaning first and the picture as uploaded on C01 with ditherMode of the kind and Min = Max = the dot power
- [ ] «Проба этого вида» makes a ramp in that kind at that power; once its photograph is measured the preview shows the measured tones at that power
- [ ] A work made before 0.21.0 opens in grayscale
