## Statement

In a photo work the operator can ask the AI, in their own words, to change parts of the picture ("всё как есть, черно-белое ... платье сделать фиолетовым или красным, ярким ... кожу сделать равномерно светлой", 2026-10-22). The picture's parts (background, hair, body skin, face skin, clothes, other) are found by a people segmentation model; the AI (OpenAI, as the vector colouring) proposes a rule per part: as it is, the photograph's own colours, black and white, a target colour keeping the part's light and dark, or one even tone; the greys are always chosen on the work's scale at its Min and Max, never by the model. Each part's rule can also be set by hand. The rules are saved in the work and apply to the prediction, the time and the LightBurn file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] An «AI» tab of a photo work shows the parts found (share, colour) and can show them tinted over the picture.
- [ ] The operator's wish goes to the AI with the parts and the colours the scale reaches; the photograph goes only when the operator allows it; an answer that breaks a rule is sent back once with the reason, a second bad answer is refused and nothing changes.
- [ ] Each part's rule can be changed by hand without the AI.
- [ ] The prediction, the time and the LightBurn file follow the rules; each rule says how near its greys come to what was wanted (dE).
- [ ] The rules are saved in the work and survive a reload and a copy.
