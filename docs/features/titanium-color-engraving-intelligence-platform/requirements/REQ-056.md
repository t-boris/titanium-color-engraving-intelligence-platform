## Statement

When the work was found by the pictures of the file (REQ-055), the overlay shall outline every layer whose output is switched off where the found work puts it, in a colour of its own and without measuring it, and the notes and warnings of such layers shall say what the photograph shows instead of that they were not engraved.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With the work found by pictures, the overlay of the real diploma (pair 105) outlines the eagle, the signatures, the text fills and the plate's cleaning rectangle as well as the seal; the outlines of the two text layers lie on the engraved letters within 0.1 mm (edge strength under the outlines is highest at no shift, measured on 97,649 and 25,975 outline pixels).
- [ ] A layer left behind (REQ-050) is still not drawn, and a layer whose output is off is still not drawn when the alignment was not found by pictures.
- [ ] The outline of a layer that is on the plate but not measured has its own colour, different from the measured layers and from the grey of an unmeasured layer.
- [ ] The note of a layer whose picture was found says that its picture is on the plate although its output is off in the file; the note of another layer that is off says that it is not measured and where its outline is drawn; no warning of the same check says that these layers were not engraved.
- [ ] The warning for a work found by pictures names the layers whose pictures were found and, separately, the layers that are only placed; both warnings and the notes have Russian translations.
