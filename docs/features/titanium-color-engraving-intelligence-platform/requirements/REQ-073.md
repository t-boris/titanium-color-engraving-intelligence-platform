## Statement

The system shall let the operator select regions of a vector work (click, Shift+click or a lasso) and turn them into a photo zone that is engraved as a photo engraving of the work's own picture instead of flat titanium colours: the zone has its kind (grayscale or a dot kind such as Jarvis), its grey scale (the palette), Min/Max or the dots' power and whether its greys follow the picture's colours, can be switched quickly between kinds and scales with the zone's picture shown in each, is drawn in the editor and the list as it will come out, and is written to the LightBurn file as one Image layer cut to the zone (transparent outside it) after the colour layers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] In the selection tool a drag draws a lasso: regions at least half inside are selected (Shift adds, Alt removes); Space+drag or the middle button pans
- [ ] «Фотогравировка» on a selection makes a new zone with the best measured scale of the work's laser, lens and titanium, its span at the zone's width, grayscale and colours by the picture; «В зону N» adds the selection to a zone, «Вернуть в вектор» returns regions to their colours
- [ ] A zone's regions leave the colour layers; the editor and the list picture show the zone's predicted photo engraving in them
- [ ] The tab «Фото» shows every zone with its kind as tiles of the zone's picture in every kind, its scale chosen from the measured ones with their colour bars, Min/Max (grayscale) or the dots' power (dot kinds) and «Цвета по картинке»; every change is undone and redone
- [ ] The file has C00 cleaning, the colour layers, then one Image layer per zone with the kind's ditherMode, the scale's settings and Min/Max (or Min = Max = the dots' power); its picture covers the zone's box at no more than the layer's DPI, transparent outside the zone, and our reader lays it exactly on the zone (also after LightBurn 2.2 saves it again)
- [ ] Zones count towards the 28 layers of a work; the job time includes each zone
- [ ] A new split keeps each zone on the new regions that lie at least half inside it; a merge keeps the region that is left in its zone
