## Statement

The first version shall support titanium with power, frequency and speed as calibration axes. Pulse width, hatch/line interval and passes shall be job settings recorded on every plate, EngravingPlan and measurement, rather than constants of an operator-managed laser profile. Comparable knowledge shall be partitioned by laser device, historical lens configuration and equal job settings; power, frequency and speed remain the modeled axes within each partition. Different partitions shall not silently share observations, maturity or production approval. Existing records shall retain their provenance. Additional axes shall remain possible without losing existing parameter data.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The operator selects a laser device and sets pulse width, line interval and passes on the plate or plan without creating a laser profile.
- [ ] Calibration varies power, frequency and speed; plates support power-by-frequency grids with speed represented by a series.
- [ ] Every measurement preserves its full parameter vector, device identity and historical lens context; later equipment or job edits do not rewrite these records.
- [ ] Models, stability estimates, repetition counts and production eligibility remain separate across different devices, lens configurations, pulse widths, line intervals or pass counts. Unknown conditions are not treated as equal to known conditions.
- [ ] Changing a job setting requires neither a new laser profile nor a profile version.
- [ ] Existing data retain their original values and provenance when profile-based grouping is replaced.
- [ ] New plate forms prefill 200 ns only when supported by the selected device; the value is editable and is not labelled experimentally optimal.
