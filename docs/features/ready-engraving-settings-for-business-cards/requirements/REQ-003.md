## Statement

The system shall let the operator choose a colour for an element or group from the measured colours of the card's device, lens and material, and shall set the element's engraving settings to the recipe that produced that colour.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The colour choice lists the measured colour swatches (the ones the vector colour editor shows) with the evidence of each.
- [ ] Choosing a swatch creates or selects the settings set with that recipe and assigns it to the element or group.
- [x] A colour that is only predicted by the model is offered separately and labelled as not measured, or not offered at all, as decided in the open question on sources.
- [x] A colour for which nothing is measured is reported as unknown; no recipe is guessed for it.
