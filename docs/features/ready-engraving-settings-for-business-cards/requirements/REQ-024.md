## Statement

Each business card shall be tied to one laser, lens and material, saved with it, and each settings set shall show where its values came from; settings measured on another laser, lens or material shall never be given to the card.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A new card saves the laser, lens and material of the workshop's current setup at that moment, and the editor shows them.
- [ ] A set shows its source line (for example the photo kind with its laser, lens and material) or "Custom".
- [ ] Changing the card's laser, lens or material replaces the sets chosen for the old setup by proposals for the new one, or by "unknown"; a set typed by hand must be confirmed again for the new setup before export. Nothing carries over silently.
- [ ] The setup and the origin of every set are saved with the card and shown again after it is reopened.
- [ ] A card saved by 0.41.0 has no setup: it is shown as "setup not set", keeps its numbers, and asks for the setup before settings can be chosen or exported.
