## Statement

Each settings set shall show where its values came from, and the system shall warn when the card's device, lens or material changed after the choice.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A set shows its source line (for example the photo kind with device, lens and material) or "Custom".
- [ ] Changing the card's device, lens or material marks the sets chosen for another setup as "chosen for another setup" and offers to choose again; nothing changes silently.
- [ ] The origin is saved with the card work and shown again after it is reopened.
