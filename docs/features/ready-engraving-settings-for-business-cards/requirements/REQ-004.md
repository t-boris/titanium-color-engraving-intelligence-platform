## Statement

The system shall let the operator take the settings of a photo engraving (the best known settings of a photo kind, or the settings of one of the workshop's photo works) for a settings set of a card.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The photo choices show the kind, its score and status, and where the best result came from.
- [ ] Choosing one fills the numeric engraving parameters and records the source; the fields that only make sense for a raster photo (dither mode, DPI, dot width) are handled as decided in the open question on them.
- [ ] The best result of another titanium is shown only as such, as in photo-engraving-quality DEC-004.
- [ ] A failed (flat) engraving is never offered as a choice, as in photo-engraving-quality DEC-006.
