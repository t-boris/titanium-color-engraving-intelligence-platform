## Statement

The system shall let the operator take the settings of a photo engraving (the best known settings of a photo kind, or the settings of one of the workshop's photo works) for a settings set of a card.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The photo choices show the kind, its score and status, and where the best result came from.
- [x] Choosing one fills the numeric engraving parameters and records the source; the raster fields (dither mode, DPI, dot width, minimum power) are kept in the set and apply to pictures engraved as raster photos (REQ-026); vector elements ignore them and the choice says so.
- [x] Results of another laser, lens or material are never offered, not even as a nearest choice (Q-005).
- [x] A failed (flat) engraving is never offered as a choice, as in photo-engraving-quality DEC-006.
