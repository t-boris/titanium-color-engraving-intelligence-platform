## Question

Which sources of ready settings does the first version offer for a card?

## Why it matters

Decides what the operator can choose from, and whether anything that is not a measurement (the film-model prediction) ever reaches a card file. The workshop's rule so far is that settings are never invented.

## Options

- **A**: Measured and validated sources only: the best photo settings per kind, measured colour recipes, and validated findings of the device. The model prediction is not offered.
- **B**: Everything in A, plus the grey scales (a measured power span for a mode), so the operator can pick the power inside the span.
- **C**: Everything in B, plus the film-model prediction for a colour, in its own group labelled "model prediction, not a measurement".

## Recommended

A: measured and validated sources only. It keeps the rule that nothing is invented; B and C can be added later as separate groups.

## Answer

Measured sources only (option A): colour (measured colour recipes), photo (best settings per kind) and lines and text (validated findings of the device). Types without measurements, for example deep engraving, are shown as "unknown" with the nearest choices; nothing is invented. The model prediction and the grey-scale span are not offered in the first version. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.
