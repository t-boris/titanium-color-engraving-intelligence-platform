## Question

Which device, lens and material do the choices use: the workshop's current setup, or the setup saved with the card?

## Why it matters

Settings are only valid for the laser, lens and titanium they were measured on. Today a card work does not save its own setup; it follows the workshop's current one.

## Options

- **A**: The card saves its own device, lens and material when it is created (taken from the current setup), the operator can change them on the card, and a change warns about the sets chosen for the old setup.
- **B**: The card always follows the workshop's current setup; the sets are re-checked whenever it changes.

## Recommended

A: save the setup with the card, so a reopened card is judged against the setup it was made for.

## Answer

CORRECTION (same day, by the operator in the conversation; it replaces the first answer "always the workshop's current setup"): the card is tied to its own laser, lens and material (option A). Every work, a business card included, belongs to one specific laser and lens and material, and settings are never given to another laser at all. The card saves its device, lens and material when it is created (taken from the workshop's current setup at that moment). Choices are offered only from measurements made on that same laser, lens and material; results of any other laser, lens or material are never offered, not even as a "nearest" choice. If the card's setup is changed, the sets chosen for the old setup stop counting: they are replaced by proposals for the new setup or shown as unknown, and a set typed by hand must be confirmed again for the new setup before export. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.
