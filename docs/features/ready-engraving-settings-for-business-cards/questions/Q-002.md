## Question

What happens to the fields of a photo setting that only make sense for a raster photo (dither mode, DPI, dot width, minimum power)?

## Why it matters

The card engraves vectors (pictures are traced into shapes), so a photo setting is only partly usable. The operator must know what was taken and what was dropped.

## Options

- **A**: Take the numeric parameters (power, speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval, passes) and drop the raster-only fields; the choice lists the dropped fields.
- **B**: Take everything the source has and write the raster-only fields into the layer too, even where the card does not use them.
- **C**: Also let a card picture be engraved as a raster photo (a new capability), so the raster fields apply.

## Recommended

A: take the numeric parameters and list what was dropped. C is a separate, larger feature.

## Answer

Add raster engraving of pictures (option C). A picture on a card can be engraved as a raster photo, so the raster fields of a photo setting (dither mode, DPI, dot width, minimum power) apply to it, in addition to the numeric parameters. The operator chose this although it was recommended as a separate, larger feature; it is part of this feature. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.
