# Discussion — Ready engraving settings for business cards

### Answer to Q-001 · 2026-11-07

Which sources of ready settings does the first version offer for a card?

Measured sources only (option A): colour (measured colour recipes), photo (best settings per kind) and lines and text (validated findings of the device). Types without measurements, for example deep engraving, are shown as "unknown" with the nearest choices; nothing is invented. The model prediction and the grey-scale span are not offered in the first version. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.

### Answer to Q-002 · 2026-11-07

What happens to the fields of a photo setting that only make sense for a raster photo (dither mode, DPI, dot width, minimum power)?

Add raster engraving of pictures (option C). A picture on a card can be engraved as a raster photo, so the raster fields of a photo setting (dither mode, DPI, dot width, minimum power) apply to it, in addition to the numeric parameters. The operator chose this although it was recommended as a separate, larger feature; it is part of this feature. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.

### Answer to Q-004 · 2026-11-07

Should a new card's first settings set start with the best known proposal, or with a prompt to choose?

With a proposal (option A). A new card's first settings set is filled with the best known proposal for the workshop's laser, lens and material, marked "proposed"; the operator must confirm it before export. When nothing is known, the set says "unknown" and what has to be measured. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.

### Answer to Q-005 · 2026-11-07

Which device, lens and material do the choices use: the workshop's current setup, or the setup saved with the card?

Always the workshop's current laser, lens and material (option B). A card does not keep its own setup; the choices and the check of the sets follow the setup the workshop has selected, and the sets are re-checked whenever it changes. The operator chose this although saving the setup with the card was recommended. Answered by the operator on 2026-11-07.

### Boris Tsekinovsky · 2026-11-07

Q-005 corrected by the operator the same day, after first choosing "always the workshop's current setup" (option B): "Every work, a business card included, must be tied to a specific laser and lens and everything. We cannot give the same settings to another laser at all." Binding answer: option A, the card is tied to its own laser, lens and material; nothing measured on another laser, lens or material is ever offered as a choice, not even as a nearest one.
