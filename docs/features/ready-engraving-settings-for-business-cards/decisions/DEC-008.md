## Context

The operator wants the settings to be a choice, for example a colour taken from the measured colours or the settings of a photo engraving, rather than numbers typed into a form. Stated by the operator on 2026-11-07: if nothing suitable is found, Custom exists, and everything else comes before it.

## Alternatives

1. Show the number boxes first with a "fill from..." button next to them.
2. Remove hand entry altogether (rejected by the operator: Custom must exist for what is not found).

## Decision

The editor offers the known choices first and \"Custom\" last. A hand-typed set is marked as the operator's own, and editing a number of a chosen set turns it into Custom while keeping the original source as a note.

## Reason

Values read from measured results are the workshop's reason to exist; typing is where mistakes and invented numbers enter.

## Consequences

The present number form of the editor moves behind \"Custom\"; its validation stays.
