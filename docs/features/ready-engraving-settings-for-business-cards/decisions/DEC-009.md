## Context

Stated by the operator on 2026-11-07 while answering Q-005: "every work, a business card included, must be tied to a specific laser and lens and everything; we cannot give the same settings to another laser at all". Today a card work does not save its setup; it follows the workshop's current one.

## Alternatives

1. The card follows the workshop's current setup (the operator first chose this, then withdrew it).
2. Show the nearest results of another setup, labelled (rejected by the operator: never across lasers).

## Decision

A business card saves its laser, lens and material when it is created. Ready settings are offered only from measurements made on exactly that laser, lens and material; nothing from another laser, lens or material is offered, not even as a nearest choice. A change of the card's setup replaces the sets chosen for the old one by proposals for the new one or by unknown, and hand-typed sets must be confirmed again before export.

## Reason

Laser parameters are only valid for the setup they were measured on; giving them to another laser could damage a titanium card or the laser. This is the strict form of the workshop's rule that parameters are not invented or carried over.

## Consequences

The card document gets a setup (device, lens, material). Cards saved by 0.41.0 have none and ask for it. The photo-engraving rule that shows another titanium's best result \"as such\" does not apply to cards. Whether the same rule must hold for photo and vector works is not decided here.
