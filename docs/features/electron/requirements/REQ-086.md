## Statement

The system shall provide public laboratory discovery and authenticated owner-controlled creation and release publication for both public and invitation-only private laboratories in the first online beta. Readers shall be able to browse, download or connect public releases without registration. Access to private releases shall require sign-in and server-side verification of an invitation authorized by the laboratory owner. Local work and file-based laboratory exchange shall remain independent of cloud accounts. Participant contributions shall be deferred to later stages after contribution access rights are decided, and online publication privacy shall be resolved before the cloud stage.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Local work and file-based laboratory exchange require no cloud account.
- [ ] Public catalogue browsing and public release downloads or connections require no cloud account.
- [ ] An account is required for owner cloud actions and for reader access to invitation-only private releases.
- [ ] Owner cloud actions use managed authentication with server-enforced ownership permissions.
- [ ] System-browser sign-in and a validated secure return to the application work on both supported platforms.
- [ ] Account recovery restores access to the same account and laboratory ownership.
- [ ] Sign-in cancellation and provider failures leave local work available.
- [ ] In the first online beta, an authenticated owner can create both public and invitation-only private laboratories and publish their releases.
- [ ] The public catalogue exposes public laboratories for browsing without registration.
- [ ] Readers can download or connect published public releases without registration.
- [ ] Private release access requires sign-in and server-side verification of an invitation authorized by the laboratory owner.
- [ ] Private release access is denied to unauthenticated readers and readers without an owner-authorized invitation.
- [ ] Accessible release cards display author-selected usage terms.
- [ ] Every laboratory mutation is authorized for its owner on the server.
- [ ] Readers cannot edit, publish or contribute results to another owner's laboratory in the first online beta.
- [ ] Local editing cannot modify another owner's publications.
- [ ] Readers' transfer experiments remain separate local observations.
- [ ] Participant contributions are deferred to later stages after contribution access rights are decided; the first online beta does not offer this capability.
- [ ] Online publication privacy must be resolved before the cloud stage.
