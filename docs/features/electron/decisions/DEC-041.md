## Context

DEC-038 includes public and invitation-only private laboratories in the first online beta, while REQ-084, REQ-086, DEC-027 and DEC-028 retain conflicting public-only scope. The team explicitly chose to preserve DEC-038.

## Alternatives

1. Limit the first online beta to public laboratories and defer invitations and private access to a later release.

## Decision

Include both public and invitation-only private laboratories in the first online beta, reaffirming DEC-038.

## Reason

This preserves the explicitly accepted product scope and establishes a consistent access contract for implementation and acceptance testing.

## Consequences

Align REQ-084, REQ-086, DEC-027, DEC-028 and the cloud API description with DEC-038. Preserve anonymous access to public releases; require sign-in and server-side invitation authorization for private releases. Participant contributions remain outside the first beta. F-001 remains open until the conflicting documents are reconciled.
