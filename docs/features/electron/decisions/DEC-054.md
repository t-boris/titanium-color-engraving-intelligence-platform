## Context

REQ-085 asks that a foreign recipe can start a separate own transfer experiment linked to its source release and observation, that own equipment limits are checked before export, and that the measurements keep the own equipment and are compared with the source by ΔE without touching the author's observation or its repeat count.

## Alternatives

1. Add the own measurement to the release's recipe as another engraving: it would raise the author's repeat count with a measurement from another laser.
2. A free-form plan without the plate machinery: it would lose the markers, the cleaning and the photograph workflow.

## Decision

«Проверить у себя» makes a test plate of one 10 mm cell on the own laser with the lens on it now, for an own titanium record, through the existing plate code: the cell carries the release recipe whole (every setting, as REQ-012 writes recipes), so the file engraves exactly the published recipe. Before the plate is made the own laser's limits are checked (power, repetition rate, pulse width); a setting it cannot take is refused with the reason and never changed to fit. The plate's plan keeps the transfer record: laboratory, release, content hash, palette colour, the release identities of its observations, the colour the release measured and the recipe. Its photographs are accepted as any plate's and become own observations with the own laser, lens and titanium; the transfer screen compares each with the release's colour by ΔE. The release's snapshot is never written, so the author's observation and its independent engraving count stay as they were.

## Reason

Reusing the plate path gives the transfer every existing check and workflow; keeping the own measurement in the workshop and the comparison beside it satisfies REQ-085 without touching the release.
