## Context

Access scope for the first online beta.

## Alternatives

1. A — только публичные публикации и локальный обмен файлами.
2. B — публичные и закрытые лаборатории с приглашениями.
3. C — только закрытые лаборатории с приглашениями.

## Decision

Support both public and private laboratories in the first online beta. Private laboratories require invitations and server-side authorization of readers.

## Reason

The user selected public and private laboratories rather than deferring private online sharing.

## Consequences

The first online beta includes reader authentication, invitations and server-side access checks for private laboratories. This supersedes the exclusion of private laboratories in proposed DEC-027. Previously downloaded releases remain usable offline.
