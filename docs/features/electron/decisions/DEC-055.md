## Context

REQ-083 asks for a benchmark-backed resource policy (sizes, record counts, profile dimensions, nesting, memory and temporary disk) with recorded evidence; the overview proposes 10 000 measurements connected in at most 10 s with progress at least once a second on an agreed machine with an SSD; DEC-036 leaves the reference machine open. DEC-024's starting limits (16 MB, 50 000 measurements) were set before anything was measured.

## Alternatives

1. Keep DEC-024's 16 MB: a release of the allowed 50 000 measurements (29 MB) could not be written.
2. Measure on a laptop as the reference: the owner has none agreed; the limits above leave a fourfold margin instead.
3. No upper limits (the format reads in constant memory): a 500 000-measurement release still takes minutes to connect and hundreds of MB of snapshot, which an operator must not meet by accident.

## Decision

The reference machine is the owner's Mac Studio (Mac14,14, Apple M2 Ultra, 128 GB, internal SSD, macOS 27). tools/bench_tcelab.py measures a workshop of N area-fill measurements (10 per photograph) through export, whole read, snapshot build and connection, timing each step without tracing and tracing Python's peak allocation in a second run; results are kept under docs/features/electron/benchmarks/ with the hardware, dataset sizes, elapsed times, peak memory, output and temporary disk sizes. Measured 2026-10-24 (app 0.28.0): 10 000 measurements → 5.8 MB file, export 2.6 s, connect 2.1 s with progress every 0.06 s, snapshot 8.7 MB; 50 000 → 28.9 MB, export 7.9 s, connect 12 s, snapshot 42.5 MB; 500 000 (timed only, tcelab-2026-10-24-time) → 289.6 MB, export 89 s, read 55 s, snapshot 96 s and 424 MB, connect 577 s with progress still every 0.06 s, 1.8 GB of temporary disk; reading and building stay at 0.0–0.7 MB traced whatever the size, export keeps about 1.8 KB per measurement (18 MB at 10 000, 92 MB at 50 000). The resource policy is therefore: file at most 64 MiB (raised from 16 MB so that the 50 000-measurement limit fits; a 500 000-measurement release stays refused, as a ten-minute connection is not something an operator meets by accident), one line at most 256 KiB, at most 1 000 equipment and 1 000 material records, 50 000 captures, 50 000 measurements, 5 000 tone profiles, 10 000 provenance records, 256 bins per profile, nesting depth 8, strings of 2 048 characters, numbers below 1e15; a release needs 12 times its size plus 64 MiB of free disk before it is connected (measured: 1.5 times for the snapshot, the rest is margin for the staging copy and the journal). The overview's target (10 000 in ≤ 10 s, progress ≥ once a second) is met with a margin of 4 on the reference machine; a slower machine is expected to stay within it up to a factor of four.

## Reason

The limits now follow measurements rather than guesses, the overview's performance target is confirmed on named hardware, and the benchmark tool makes every later change of the limits a measured one.

## Consequences

lab_format.DEFAULT_POLICY carries the limits; tests/test_lab_memory.py keeps reading and building flat and export under 4 KB per measurement; the benchmark is rerun and its file added when the policy or the format changes.
