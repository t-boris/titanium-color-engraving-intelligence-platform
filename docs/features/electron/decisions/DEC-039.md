## Context

Public desktop distribution requires signing resources and hosting for installers and update metadata.

## Alternatives

1. Use project-owner-controlled signing and release hosting.
2. Limit distribution to an unsigned internal pilot.

## Decision

Use signing resources and release hosting registered to and controlled by the project owner. Signing keys shall remain outside the repository.

## Reason

This establishes ownership of the resources required for public distribution.

## Consequences

The specific hosting service and signing arrangements remain to be selected before public distribution.
