## Context

Recipients need explicit usage rights for published laboratory releases.

## Alternatives

1. Uniform terms with attribution for all public laboratories.
2. Author-selected terms from an approved list.
3. A limited pilot pending agreement on usage terms.

## Decision

Authors shall select usage terms from a small, product-approved list. Incompatible terms shall prevent combining or republishing the affected data.

## Reason

Author choice accommodates different sharing preferences while an approved list bounds compatibility handling.

## Consequences

The approved options and their permissions remain to be defined. This decision does not settle whether v1 supports re-exporting or republishing imported data (Q-006).
