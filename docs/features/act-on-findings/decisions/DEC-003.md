## Context

A recipe's colour is the consensus of its independent engravings (agreement.consensus). Engraving time is read from accepted_at, which a re-measure renews, so a date cut-off would let old engravings back after «Перепроверить все работы».

## Alternatives

1. A since-date: a re-measure renews accepted_at and brings old engravings back
2. Choose any engraving to keep: more choice than asked; the operator asked for the latest

## Decision

The decision names the recipe (laser, lens, recipe key), the engraving kept (the newest by model._when at that moment) and the ids of every older engraving of the recipe; those engravings' observations of that recipe stop counting. An engraving made later has a new id and counts. A new decision for the same recipe replaces the earlier one. It is refused when the recipe has one engraving.

## Reason

Engraving ids are stable across re-measures and re-photos; the operator's intent is "the old ones are wrong", not "everything before a moment".
