## Context

«Считать с последней гравировки» keeps the newest engraving of a recipe, and the agreed colour breaks a tie toward the newest engraving. Both dated an engraving by the acceptance of the pair that measured it. A re-measure is a new pair accepted now, so after «Перепроверить все работы» every photograph of the live workshop carried the re-check time (25.10 16:05–16:13), and re-measuring one old photograph from a finding would make its engraving «the newest». The rehearsal on a copy of the live data showed it: the dates of the older engravings all read 25.10.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the re-measured pair's acceptance date (the old behaviour): the order of engravings follows the order of re-measures
2. Copy the original accepted_at onto the re-measured pair: changes what accepted_at means for the pair and the history of acceptances

## Decision

The date of a photograph is the acceptance of its first measurement: the pair at the start of its correction_of chain (ingest.first_accepted). model._when, the colour map's point date and the engravings in a «с последней гравировки» decision all use it. The pair's own accepted_at is unchanged.

## Reason

A re-measure revises how the same photograph is measured; it does not change when the engraving was made or photographed. The operator's «последняя версия» means the engraving made last.

## Consequences

On the live data the older engravings read their real dates again (20.10, 23.10, 24.10); the colour map shows each photograph's first acceptance; a tie in the agreed colour goes to the engraving photographed last, as before the re-check.
