## Context

A recipe's colour is derived in two places (model.summarize for knowledge and findings, colormap.agreed for the colour map, palette and works); the oxide model, the experiment library and the laboratory export read observations too.

## Alternatives



## Decision

exclusions.excluded_ids is applied in model.eligible_observations, colormap._rows (so the colour map, palette, predictions and vector works), oxide.calibrated_observations, the experiment library and a new laboratory release (published releases are unchanged). Every exclusion or «Вернуть» is audited and then recalculates once: knowledge.refresh, designer.reconcile_statuses, findings.run_analysis('operator'), work_errors.after_change; refused while «Перепроверить все работы» runs. photo_knowledge reads tone from checks, not colours, and is not affected.

## Reason

A measurement out of one calculation but in another would give contradicting colours on two screens.
