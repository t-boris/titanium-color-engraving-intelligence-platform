## Statement

The system shall show, before a remedy applies, what leaves the calculation and the recipe's colour before and after, and after it applies recalculate the knowledge, the findings and the works once.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] An opened finding starts with «Что сделать»: one button per remedy that fits its kind, each with a line on what it does
- [x] A remedy first shows a confirmation: the photographs, layers or engravings that leave, and the recipe's colour swatch before and after with ΔE
- [x] The colour shown after equals the recipe's colour once applied
- [x] After it applies the screen says «Готово · расчёт № N» with «Вернуть»
- [x] A remedy is refused while «Перепроверить все работы» runs
