## Statement

The system shall let the operator make a recipe's colour count from its newest engraving: the older engravings stay in the history but stop counting, and engravings made later count as usual.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Anomaly, unstable and questioned findings of a recipe with two or more engravings offer «Считать с последней гравировки»
- [x] After it the recipe's colour is the newest engraving's colour, with one engraving counted
- [x] A new engraving of the recipe made afterwards counts; a re-measure or re-photo of an older engraving does not
- [x] It is refused with a clear message when the recipe has one engraving
- [x] The knowledge entry shows «Считается с гравировки …» with «Отменить», which brings the older engravings back
