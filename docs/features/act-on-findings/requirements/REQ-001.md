## Statement

The system shall let the operator take one measured layer of one photograph out of the recipe, the colour map, the palette and every other calculation from an anomaly finding, keep the photograph and its other layers, and undo it with «Вернуть».

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] An anomaly finding offers «Убрать измерение»; after it the observation is not in the recipe's engravings, the colour-map points or the next findings run, and the anomaly is gone
- [x] The photograph and its other layers still count; the photo page shows the layer as «убрано из расчёта оператором» with «Вернуть»
- [x] A re-measure of the photograph (one or «Перепроверить все работы») keeps the layer out
- [x] «Вернуть» brings the measurement back into every calculation and recalculates the findings
- [x] Taking out and returning are written to the audit
