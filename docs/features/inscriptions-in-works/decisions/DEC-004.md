## Context

A photo work's width can be changed after inscriptions are placed on it (a vector work's size is fixed when it is made). An inscription is kept in the work's millimetres, so without a rule it would keep its size and place in mm while the picture under it grows or shrinks.

## Alternatives

1. Keep every inscription in mm: the letters keep their physical height, but their place on the picture shifts and they may fall off it
2. Scale everything, the gap included

## Decision

A width change scales the inscriptions with the picture, as a group scaled in LightBurn: position, letter height, spacing, radius, path points, backing margin and corner radius are multiplied by the new width over the old one (clamped to their ranges). The gap stays the same in mm. The change is one step of undo with the width.

## Reason

The operator composes the inscription on the picture; enlarging the work should keep that composition. The gap is the laser's clean margin between the letters and the picture, a physical tolerance, not part of the design.

## Consequences

The letter height in mm changes with the width; the screen shows it. lettering.scaled, photo_work.update.
