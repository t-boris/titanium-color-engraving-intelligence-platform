## Context

The operator wants the inscription "inserted into the picture": cut out of a photo engraving, cut into the vectors of a vector work. Later they will still want to change the text, the font, the place or the colour.

## Alternatives

1. Burn the letters into the split (raster the text into the regions and trace again): fast to draw but loses the letters' shape at the split's resolution and changes every region id
2. Always a rectangle under the text

## Decision

A work keeps its inscriptions as a list of objects (text, font, size, stretch, spacing, layout, place, colour, gap, backing). The vector document and the picture are never changed by them. The letters are computed from the object, and so are the cut (the letters widened by the gap, or the backing with its gap) and the backing's ring. They are applied when the work is drawn, timed and written: a region of a vector work loses the part under the cut (it is written as a polygon without its curves where it was cut), a photo zone's picture and a photo work's picture are transparent under the cut, and the letters and the backing are filled with their recipes on the layer of that recipe (one layer per recipe, shared with regions of the same recipe). Letters "cut out of the picture with a gap" by default (0.3 mm of clean titanium); a backing (rectangle, oval or the letters' outline widened) is optional, filled with its own colour or left as clean titanium.

## Reason

Operator's choice «Буквы с зазором» of 2026-10-23; a non-destructive object keeps every change undoable and editable, and the split, its versions and the photo zones stay as they are.

## Consequences

Each inscription takes the layer of its recipe (a new recipe takes a new LightBurn layer, inside the layer cap); its area counts in the time; a colour no longer measured or a font that is gone blocks the file with a message.
