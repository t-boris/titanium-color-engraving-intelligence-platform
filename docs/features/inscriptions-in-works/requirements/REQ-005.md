## Statement

The system shall cut an inscription out of a photo engraving work: the picture is not engraved under the cut, the letters and the backing are engraved as fills of their recipes after the picture, and the preview, the time and the LightBurn file show it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Image layer's picture is transparent under the cut (LightBurn skips it) in grayscale and in every dot kind.
- [ ] The file keeps the cleaning on C00 and the picture on C01; the letters and the backing follow on layers of their recipes with their complete settings, laid where the preview shows them.
- [ ] The predicted look shows the cut as clean titanium and the letters in their recipe's colour at their size; the list's thumbnail too.
- [ ] The time adds the letters' and the backing's fill time to the picture's.
- [ ] A work without inscriptions writes the same file as before.
