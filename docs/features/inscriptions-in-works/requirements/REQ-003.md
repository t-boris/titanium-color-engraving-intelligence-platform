## Statement

The system shall let the operator add, place, change, duplicate and delete inscriptions on the picture of a vector work and of a photo engraving work, choose each inscription's colour (and its backing's) from the measured colours of the work's laser, lens and titanium, and undo and redo every change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A tab «Надписи» in the vector editor and in the photo work lists the work's inscriptions; «Добавить надпись» puts a new one in the middle of the picture in the last font used.
- [ ] An inscription is dragged on the picture; handles turn and size it; its fields (text, font, height, stretch, spacing, rotation, layout and its parameters, colour, gap, backing) change it, and the picture shows the result at once.
- [ ] A path is drawn on the picture by clicks (Enter or a double click ends it) and its points can be moved afterwards.
- [ ] The colour is chosen in the same window as the editor's colours (measured colours with ΔE, time and where they are known from); the backing takes its own colour or stays clean titanium.
- [ ] Every change is one step of the work's undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z); typing in the text field makes one step per pause, not per letter.
- [ ] A work without inscriptions draws, times and writes exactly as before.
