## Statement

The system shall cut an inscription into a vector work: every region under the cut (the letters widened by the gap, or the backing) loses that part, the letters and the backing are engraved as filled shapes of their recipes, and the editor, the layers, the time and the LightBurn file show it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] In the exported file a region crossed by an inscription is a closed path without the cut (holes where letters are inside it), and no region path overlaps a letter or the gap.
- [ ] The letters are Path shapes on the layer of their recipe (shared with regions of that recipe, else a new layer within the layer cap); the backing likewise.
- [x] A photo zone's picture is transparent under the cut.
- [ ] The layers list and the time count the letters' area and leave out the cut part of the regions; the row length of a layer includes its letters.
- [ ] An inscription whose colour is no longer measured or whose font is gone blocks the file with a message naming it; one outside the picture is cut off at its edge and says so.
- [x] The file read back by the platform's own reader gives the letters' area within 1 % of the computed area.
