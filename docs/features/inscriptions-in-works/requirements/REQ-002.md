## Statement

The system shall lay out an inscription of one or more lines in a chosen font and style at a letter height in millimetres, stretched in width and height, with letter and line spacing, alignment and rotation, straight, bent on an arc, around a circle or along any path, the letters upright on the curve or bent with it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The height of capital letters equals the given height in mm (measured on the outline of «H» or the font's cap height).
- [ ] Kerning and ligatures of the font are applied; Hebrew and Arabic are written right to left, a line mixing directions keeps each run in its own direction.
- [ ] Stretch in width and height, letter spacing, line spacing, alignment (left, centre, right) and rotation change the outline as named.
- [ ] Arc: a bend from -100 % to 100 % turns the baseline into an arc (100 % a half circle, negative bends the other way); circle: around a centre at a radius from a start angle, outside or inside; path: along a curve drawn on the picture through the operator's points (smooth), from a start offset.
- [ ] On a curve the letters stand upright on it (each turned to the curve) or are bent with it (every point of the letter follows the curve).
- [ ] The outline is a set of closed polygons in the work's millimetres; letters that overlap are one shape (no double engraving).
