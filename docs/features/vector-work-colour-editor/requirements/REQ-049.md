## Statement

The system shall undo and redo every colouring and merging operation of a photograph vector work, including the document version, the ΔE tolerance and the filters in force.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Undo after paint, merge of colours, merge of areas and paint returns step by step to the first state, version and colouring; redo returns step by step to the last.
- [ ] A new operation after an undo clears the redo steps; a new document version never reuses a version number.
- [ ] Undoing a change of tolerance or filters restores them as well as the colouring.
- [ ] A variant made on another document version is refused with a translated message until undo brings that version back.
- [ ] Undo entries of works made before this release still undo.
