## Statement

The system shall merge a selected connected set of areas into one area with one outline in a new document version, keeping the picture covered without gaps or overlaps.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Areas are selected with the select tool (click, Shift+click); merging is offered only for two or more areas connected through shared borders (the button is disabled otherwise and says why, DEC-080); areas touching only at a corner are refused with a translated message.
- [x] The merged area keeps the id, source colour and choices of its largest member; its pixels are the sum of its members'.
- [x] Its outline is closed, its holes are separate loops, and the picture's coverage stays without gaps or overlaps (measured as before the merge).
- [x] The exported LightBurn file of the new version is read back with every area and every hole in place.
- [x] The merge can be undone, which brings the previous version back.
