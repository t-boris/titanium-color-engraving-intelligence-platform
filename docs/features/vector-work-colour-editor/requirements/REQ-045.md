## Statement

The system shall paint, on a click with the brush, the clicked area, every area of its source colour, or a fill of bordering areas of similar source colour with the active titanium colour.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Scope "area": only the clicked area takes the colour; other areas of its source colour keep theirs.
- [x] Scope "source colour": the source colour takes the colour and every area of it shows it, including areas that had their own colour.
- [x] Scope "fill": the clicked area and every area reached through shared borders whose source colour is within the similarity ΔE of the clicked area's take the colour; the fill never passes through excluded areas or areas of excluded colours.
- [x] The hover preview of a fill names exactly the areas the click then paints.
- [x] Painting an excluded area includes it again.
- [x] Painting with "automatic" returns the scope to the automatic choice.
- [x] Each paint is one operation in the history and can be undone.
