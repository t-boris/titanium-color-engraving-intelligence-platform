## Statement

The system shall turn two or more source colours of the split into one source colour in a new document version.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The merged colour's Lab is the pixel-weighted mean of its members, and it keeps the number of the member with most pixels; other colours keep their numbers.
- [x] Every area of a member belongs to the merged colour afterwards; area ids and geometry are unchanged.
- [x] Choices and exclusions of the merged-away colours are dropped; area choices stay.
- [x] Every brush scope and automatic mode works on the new version; the version can be undone.
- [x] Fewer than two colours, or an unknown colour, is refused with a translated message.
