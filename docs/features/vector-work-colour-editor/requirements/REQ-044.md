## Statement

The system shall present the colouring of a photograph vector work as one large picture in titanium colours beside a panel with the tools, the active titanium colour, the palette, the layers in use, the source colours and the totals.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The picture fills the card beside the panel; it zooms (wheel, + and −, fit) and pans by dragging; a switch shows titanium colours, source colours or the original photograph.
- [ ] The panel offers the tools brush, pipette and select, and the brush scope with its similarity ΔE.
- [ ] The palette lists every measured colour of the work's laser, lens and titanium, nearest to the hovered area's source colour first, each with ΔE and seconds per cm²; colours measured without a reference are drawn dashed.
- [ ] The layers list shows each titanium colour in use with its area, number of areas and time; the source colours list shows each colour with its titanium colour and ΔE.
- [ ] Totals show layers used of the available, the colour layers' time and the mean ΔE, and update after every operation.
- [ ] Hovering an area outlines exactly what a click would change and shows its source colour number, titanium colour, ΔE and recipe.
- [ ] The pipette (or Alt+click) makes the clicked area's titanium colour the active colour.
- [ ] Undo and redo are buttons and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z.
