## Context

REQ-045 says that painting a source colour makes "every area of it show it" (criterion 2), that painting an excluded area includes it again (criterion 5), and the hover must outline exactly what a click changes (REQ-044). In release 0.6.0 a click with the scope "source colour" included again only the clicked area; other areas of that colour that were excluded one by one stayed excluded, although the hover outlined them. Asked on 2026-10-19, the operator chose to include them all.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the individually excluded areas excluded and stop the hover outlining them
2. Leave the behaviour of 0.6.0 and record the divergence

## Decision

A click of the brush with the scope "source colour" and a titanium colour sets the colour's choice, clears the own colours of its areas, includes the colour again and also includes again every area of that colour that was excluded one by one. Painting with "automatic" leaves exclusions as they are, as before. The scopes "area" and "fill" are unchanged: they include again the areas they paint.

## Reason

The operator's answer; every area of the colour then shows the colour, as criterion 2 says, and the hover outline names exactly the areas the click changes.

## Consequences

An area excluded on purpose comes back when its whole source colour is painted; the hover shows it before the click and undo restores the exclusion.
