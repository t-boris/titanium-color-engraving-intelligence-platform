## Context

Release 0.5.1 coloured a source colour automatically only when a measured titanium colour lay within the ΔE tolerance; otherwise the nearest was "proposed" and the file stayed blocked until the operator accepted, replaced or excluded each such colour (REQ-042 criterion "unreachable until the operator explicitly substitutes or excludes it", DEC-074 of titanium-color-engraving-intelligence-platform, after DEC-009). On work 22 the tolerance was 0.05 (none saved for the laser and lens, so the form asked for one), all 28 colours were proposed and had to be accepted one by one. The operator: "все должно быть принято в самом начале".

## Alternatives

1. Keep the acceptance step with one "accept all" button
2. Leave proposed colours uncoloured (hatched) until chosen

## Decision

For a photograph vector work every source colour gets a titanium colour at once: the fastest within the tolerance, else the nearest (as the automatic modes already compute). A colour beyond the tolerance is shown with its ΔE and can be outlined on the picture; it never blocks the file. The file is blocked only when a chosen titanium colour is no longer measured, when the palette is empty, when nothing is left to engrave, or when the recipes exceed the LightBurn layers. The "accept" step and its operations are removed; earlier accepted choices stay as hand choices. This amends REQ-042 (criterion on unreachable colours) and DEC-074 of the closed feature for photograph vector works; discrete artwork conversion (DEC-009) is unchanged.

## Reason

The operator decides by looking at the coloured picture; a per-colour acceptance adds clicks without adding information, since the nearest colour and its ΔE are shown anyway.

## Consequences

A work can be exported with colours far from the original; the ΔE badge and the outline of far areas are the operator's warning.
