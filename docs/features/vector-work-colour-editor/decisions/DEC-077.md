## Context

Until 0.5.1 the undo stack held colourings only; merging source colours or areas changes the document version, and undoing a settings change did not restore the tolerance and filters. Variants of the colouring were restorable at any time (REQ-042 of titanium-color-engraving-intelligence-platform).

## Alternatives

1. Restoring a variant also switches the document version
2. Transform stored variants on every merge

## Decision

Every editor operation can be undone and redone. A snapshot holds the colouring, the document version, the ΔE tolerance and the filters; undoing a merge brings its earlier document version back from the history. A new operation clears the redo stack. Document version numbers never repeat (one more than the highest in the history). Variants remember the version they were made on; a variant of another version is refused until undo brings that version back. Splitting again stays a fresh start (clears undo, redo and variants). Undo entries of older works are read as belonging to their current version.

## Reason

One undo for everything the operator does in the editor; restoring a variant must not silently undo a merge.

## Consequences

This narrows REQ-042's "variants can be restored" to their own split version.
