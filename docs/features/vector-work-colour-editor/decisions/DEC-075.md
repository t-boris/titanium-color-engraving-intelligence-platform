## Context

The operator asked for "нормальный редактор": change any colour by clicking any area of the picture, several modes of choosing, merging. Asked on 2026-10-19, the operator chose: the layout "brush and palette"; click scopes "only this area", "every area of the same source colour", "fill over neighbours"; automatic recolouring "only for the whole work".

## Alternatives

1. A click opens a palette next to the area with the scope to choose each time
2. Automatic modes applied to a selection as well

## Decision

One large picture of the work in titanium colours with zoom and pan, and a side panel: tools (brush, pipette, select), the brush scope, the active titanium colour and the palette of every measured colour, the layers in use, the source colours, the totals, undo and redo. A click with the brush paints with the active colour: one area; or every area of its source colour (the colour's choice, clearing the areas' own choices inside it); or a fill that spreads from the clicked area over bordering areas whose source colour lies within a similarity ΔE of the clicked one's, never through excluded areas. Painting an excluded area includes it again. Hovering outlines exactly what a click will change. The automatic modes (closer to the original, keep tonality, random, fewer layers) recolour the whole work and keep the operator's hand choices, as before.

## Reason

The operator's answers; a brush with a scope reaches one area, a colour or a patch in one click, and the hover outline removes surprises.

## Consequences

Similarity is measured between source colours: an area has no colour of its own besides its source colour in the split (segment.py), so a fill cannot separate two areas of the same source colour; a finer split does.
