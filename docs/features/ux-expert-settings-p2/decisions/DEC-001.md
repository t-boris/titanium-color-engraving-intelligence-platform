## Context

The research did not establish measured contrast or a preference for a light theme.

## Alternatives

1. Limit the first stage to readability and density improvements in the current theme.

## Decision

Compare theme readability and task performance under actual workshop lighting and on a tablet before deciding whether to release a light theme.

## Reason

The product owner selected option A.

## Consequences

A light theme is not committed for release; the comparison must inform a later release decision.
