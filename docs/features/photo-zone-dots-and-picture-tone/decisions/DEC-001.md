## Context

LightBurn has Brightness, Contrast and Gamma in its Image settings. The app predicts the look (and the dots, by dithering the picture itself) from the picture's greys; if LightBurn changed the greys after the export, the forecast would no longer show what is engraved.

## Alternatives

1. Write the values into LightBurn's Bitmap settings and leave the picture as it is: the forecast and the dots would not match the engraving
2. Edit the uploaded picture in place: the change could not be undone or moved back to as-is

## Decision

Brightness (-100..100 %), contrast (-100..100 %) and gamma (0.2..5) are part of the work's and the zone's state and are applied to the picture's greys with the formulas the app already uses for LightBurn's adjustments (prediction.adjust_grey) before anything is drawn, dithered, timed or written. The file's picture carries the toned greys and LightBurn's own adjustments stay at 0, 0, 1.

## Reason

One set of greys for the look, the close-up, the tiles, the time and the file: what the operator sees is what LightBurn engraves, and nothing is applied twice.

## Consequences

A toned picture is memoised (two at a time) and keeps the untoned one, so it is never toned twice. Cached drawings carry the tone in their keys.
