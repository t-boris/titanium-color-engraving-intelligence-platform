## Context

Since 0.25.0 (DEC-118 of the platform feature) a photo work's dots can take any measured fill or grey-scale tone of the colour map; photo zones of vector works were left on their scale (item D6, postponed).

## Alternatives



## Decision

The same rules and code (dot_colours) serve the zone: a fill keeps a recipe whose settings fire every dot and whose step is the zone layer's DPI, its colour taken at the zone's width and again when the width changes; a tone of a grey scale moves the zone to that scale and power; a dot power or a scale puts the dots back on the scale. The map opens from the zone screen or from the zone's card in the editor and returns there; each pick is one step of the editor's undo.

## Reason

One behaviour for every picture the operator engraves in dots, and no second set of rules to keep in step.
