## Context

With «Подобрать серые под цвета» the greys of a grayscale picture are chosen from the picture's colours, not taken from its own greys; a brightness of the greys has nothing to act on there.

## Alternatives

1. Tone the colours before the greys are chosen: a different, colour-space operation the operator did not ask for

## Decision

The tone applies to every dot kind and to grayscale while the picture's own greys are engraved. With greys chosen for the colours the sliders are off with a note; the values are kept and act again when the choice is switched off or a dot kind is chosen.

## Reason

The operator's answer of 2026-10-24: «Точки и градации серого», the latter while the greys are the picture's own.
