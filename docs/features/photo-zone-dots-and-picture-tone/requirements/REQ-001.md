## Statement

The system shall let the operator choose the dots' colour of a photo zone in a dot kind from every measured fill and grey-scale tone of the work's laser, lens and titanium on the colour map, and engrave, time and forecast the zone at that colour's settings.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The zone screen and the zone's card in the editor's «Фото» tab show «Цвет с карты» for a dot kind; it opens the colour map in picking mode for that zone, filtered to the work's laser and lens, with a strip naming the zone and a way back without changes.
- [ ] «Точки этим цветом» on a fill keeps its recipe: the zone's Image layer fires at the fill's power (Min = Max), speed, frequency, pulse and passes, its DPI is 25.4 / the fill's step, and the look, the close-up, the tiles and the time use the same settings and the fill's colour at the zone's width.
- [ ] «Точки этим цветом» on a grey-scale tone moves the zone to that scale at that power.
- [ ] The map returns to where it was opened: the zone screen, or the editor when opened from the card.
- [ ] «На шкалу», a dot power, another scale or undo puts the dots back on the zone's scale; each pick is one step of the editor's undo and redo.
- [ ] When regions are added to or taken from the zone, the fill's colour is taken again at the new width; a colour no longer measured is marked as such.
- [ ] A grayscale zone refuses a colour of the map with a message in Russian.
