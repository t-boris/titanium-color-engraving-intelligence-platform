## Statement

The system shall let the operator change the brightness, contrast and gamma of the picture of a photo work and of a photo zone, and show and engrave the picture with that tone.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The picture panel of a photo work and of a zone screen shows «Яркость», «Контраст» and «Гамма» as sliders with their numbers and «Как есть»; a change is saved after a pause in the slider's movement and the forecast follows.
- [x] Brightness and contrast take -100..100 %, gamma 0.2..5; a value outside is refused with a message in Russian and changes nothing.
- [x] The greys of the picture in the LightBurn file equal the picture's greys after the tone (within one level), and LightBurn's own Brightness, Contrast and Gamma stay 0, 0, 1.
- [ ] In a dot kind a lighter picture has fewer dots and a darker one more; the close-up, the tiles of the kinds and the time follow.
- [x] In grayscale with «Подобрать серые под цвета» the sliders are off with a note, the values are kept and the file does not change; they act again when that choice is off or a dot kind is chosen.
- [ ] Each change of the tone is undone and redone; a new photo zone takes the tone of the last one; the zone's card in the editor names a tone that changes the picture.
- [x] While nothing can be forecast (the work's scale is not measured) the sliders are hidden, as the power sliders are.
