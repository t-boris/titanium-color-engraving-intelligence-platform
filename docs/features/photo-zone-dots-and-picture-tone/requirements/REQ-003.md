## Statement

The system shall answer a change of the tone or of the dots' colour of a photo zone about as fast as any earlier change of that zone, and open the colour map in picking mode about as fast as for a photo work.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] On the operator's 150 mm zone (3000 × 2557 px) a change of the tone takes no longer than a change of the dots' power (about 1.5 s on the copy of 2026-10-24).
- [x] The colour map in picking mode for a zone answers within 0.6 s warm.
- [x] Dragging a slider sends one change after the movement pauses, not one per step.
