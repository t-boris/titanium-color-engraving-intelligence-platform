# Photo engraving quality

## Idea

Photo engraving (LightBurn image layers: grayscale and the dot modes Jarvis, Atkinson, Stucki, …) gets the attention colour gets. After every photograph each photo-engraved layer is measured for tone (darkness, contrast, shadows and highlights kept, dot gain), detail and grain, with conclusions that name the setting to change for darker, lighter, sharper or softer. The laboratory keeps, per laser, lens, titanium and mode, the best measured settings and the measured effect of each setting; a photo zone or photo work fills them in when its mode is chosen. When knowledge is missing it proposes the next experiment: one compact plate with up to 8 copies of one picture at different settings, centred on the best known settings and narrowing with every round, so the defaults converge.
