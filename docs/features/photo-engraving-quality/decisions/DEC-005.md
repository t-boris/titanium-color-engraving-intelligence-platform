## Context

A photo zone writes no dot width correction, so its «Лучшее измеренное» and «Взять лучшие настройки» already skip engravings made with one (best(dot_width=False)). The plate the laboratory proposed on the same panel came from knowledge(), which did not: on the live data (2026-10-25, after the re-check) zone 2 of work 41 «Яна портрет - 1» showed «Лучшее измеренное: 25 % … по фото 149» and, below it, a plate around 30 %, 150 mm/s, 0.0231 mm, dot width 0.02 mm (photo 139) with a copy that removes the dot width: two different «best» in one panel, one the zone cannot engrave.

## Alternatives

1. Leave the general titanium proposal on a zone's panel: two different «best» side by side, one the zone cannot reproduce

## Decision

knowledge() takes dot_width (default True); advice() passes the owner's: for a photo zone only engravings made without a dot width count as the kind's best and as the centre of the «around» proposal. Photo works and the «Выводы» tab keep every engraving.

## Reason

The experiment proposed must start where the owner can engrave, and the panel must name one best.

## Consequences

On the live copy zone 2 now shows one best (photo 149, 25 %, 125 mm/s, 0.01 mm, score 0.264) and the plate around it. Whether a zone's plate should spend a copy on the dot width (0.01 mm) is asked separately.
