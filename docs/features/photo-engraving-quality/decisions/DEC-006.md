## Context

On the live data (2026-10-25) every Atkinson engraving on Big Plate came out a negative: photo 147 at 10.2 % made light gold dots (L* 92), lighter than the titanium, so the picture's range was −0.1 L* and its score 0. best() still returned it, so choosing Atkinson for zone 2 of «Яна портрет - 1» filled in 10.2 %, and the laboratory proposed an Atkinson plate around 10.2 %. Halftone on photo 147 (12.1 %, L* 94) behaved the same. Chosen by the operator in chat on 2026-10-25 («Тёмные точки другого вида»).

## Alternatives

1. Keep the zone's own dots and change only the kind
2. Keep filling in the failed engraving and only warn

## Decision

An engraving with the «flat» finding (its dark end less than FLAT_RANGE_L = 15 L* darker than its light end, or lighter) has failed: it is listed, never a kind's best, never filled in and never the centre of a proposal. A dot kind whose every engraving on a titanium failed (status «failed», «не получился») takes, when chosen, the dots of the best usable engraving of another dot kind on that titanium as an estimate (photo_knowledge.stand_in); the panel says which photographs failed and why, and «Взять тёмные точки «…»» applies those dots to a work or zone already set to the failed settings. The proposed plate is centred on those dots with the failed kind's dither. A kind never photographed keeps what the owner had, as before.

## Reason

Filling in settings that are known to give no picture is the opposite of converging; the closest measured dark dots on the same titanium are the best estimate, and the plate around them measures the kind itself.

## Consequences

On the live copy: Atkinson and halftone on Big Plate take Jarvis photo 149 (25 %, 125 mm/s, 0.01 mm) for a zone, photo 139 for a work; on Small Plate Jarvis and Atkinson both failed and nothing usable stands in, so the owner keeps its dots and the panel says dots darker than the titanium are needed. The «Выводы» tab counts the kinds that failed.
