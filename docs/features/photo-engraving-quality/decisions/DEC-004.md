## Context

REQ-004 and REQ-005 need, per laser, lens, titanium and kind of photo engraving, the best measured settings, the effect of each setting, a fill when the kind is chosen, steps darker / lighter / sharper / softer and a proposed plate. Photographs are judged on read (DEC-001), and grey scales of the workshop often turn back lighter past some power (the oxide's turn), so a Max past it makes the darkest places of a picture light.

## Alternatives

1. A knowledge table updated on every acceptance: a second source that can go stale and needs a migration
2. The step moves the setting measured on the most photographs first: steadier, but not what REQ-004 asks (the largest effect)
3. Steps that change several settings at once: the operator cannot tell what did what

## Decision

1. Knowledge is derived on read from the accepted checks (photo_knowledge.py), not stored: every photo layer is judged (DEC-001), grouped by its settings into engravings; tone comes from a grey ramp when there is one, else from the picture; detail from the picture. A grey ramp that turns back adds its part in order (min_power..max_power before the turn) as an engraving of its own. The best engraving of a kind is the highest score on this titanium; another titanium's is shown only as such.
2. Choosing a kind on a photo work or zone fills in the best measured engraving of it: grayscale takes its measured scale with Min and Max; a dot kind takes a photo recipe — the dots fire at the photographed settings, and their colour is the photographed black. Without a measurement nothing is replaced and the screen says the settings are an estimate. A grayscale default span is the scale's measured span narrowed to the ordered span (Max stays before the turn).
3. Each step changes one setting: the one with the largest measured effect that way (then the one measured on the most photographs), by what the effect says for about 3 L*, at most ×1.5 or ÷1.5; without a measured effect the prior of the kind's family. A step never goes past a limit or a grey scale's turn; with Max already past the turn no step is offered and «Взять диапазон до разворота» brings it back. «Взять лучшие настройки» takes the best engraving in one change. Every step and take is one step of undo of the owner.
4. Proposals: the next plate of a photographed variations plate that has not settled (centred on its best copy, changes halved, DEC-003); a plate around the best of a kind measured only on works; for an unmeasured dot kind a plate around the best dot engraving. Each proposal lists the change each copy makes and the plate's size, made on the picture of the newest photographed variations plate; before the first one the screen says to make it from a work or zone («Подобрать настройки: 8 копий»). Proposals show on the work, the zone, «Эксперименты» and «Выводы».

## Reason

Reading on demand keeps one source (the accepted checks), so every new photograph improves the defaults at once and nothing goes stale; it takes about 0.1 s per laser on the live data. The turn is guarded because past it more power is lighter, which reverses every step and makes the picture wrong.

## Consequences

The best settings and effects change as photographs are accepted or rejected. A kind photographed only on another titanium is not filled in. The first variations plate must come from a work or zone, because its picture is the operator's.
