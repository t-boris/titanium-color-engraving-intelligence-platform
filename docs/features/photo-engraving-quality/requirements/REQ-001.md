## Statement

The system shall make a plate with 2–12 copies of one picture, each engraved at its own photo-engraving settings with a grey ramp at the same settings under it, and measure every copy and every ramp from one photograph of the plate.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each copy is two LightBurn Image layers (the picture, the ramp) with the same kind, power, Min power, speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval, passes and dot width correction
- [ ] The labels above the copies name the first copy's settings and, for every other copy, what differs from it
- [ ] The picture is stored once; every copy in the file engraves the same picture
- [ ] Fewer than 2 or more than 12 copies, two identical copies, unknown settings, a kind other than grayscale or a dot kind, settings the laser cannot take and a picture that is not PNG or JPEG are refused with the reason (and the copy's number)
- [ ] After the photograph each ramp has a tonal profile measured step by step and each copy a tonal profile against its picture
- [ ] The plate can also be made from the plate dialog with an uploaded picture, its first copy's kind and the copy size; without copies given, the copies change one setting at a time
