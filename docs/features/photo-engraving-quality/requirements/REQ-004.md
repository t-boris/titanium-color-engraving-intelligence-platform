## Statement

The system shall keep, per laser, lens, titanium and photo mode, the best measured settings and the measured effect of each setting; when the operator chooses a mode for a photo zone or a photo work, it shall fill in the best known settings, and four buttons shall move the settings by the measured effect for slightly darker, lighter, sharper or softer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Choosing Jarvis (or another kind) on a zone or a work fills in the best measured settings of that kind on this laser, lens and titanium, and says which photograph they come from
- [x] Without a measurement the settings come from the operator's archive or the scale, and the screen says they are not measured yet
- [x] Each of «Темнее», «Светлее», «Чётче», «Мягче» changes the setting with the largest measured effect in that direction by one small step and is one step of undo
- [x] Every new accepted photograph with a better score replaces the best settings
