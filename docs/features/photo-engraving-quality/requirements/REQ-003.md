## Statement

The system shall, after every accepted photograph with a photo-engraved layer, show for each such layer its tone, detail and grain (DEC-001) and say in words what came out too dark, too light, flat, blocked, reversed, blurred or grainy and which setting to change for darker, lighter, sharper or softer; on a photo-variations plate it shall rank the copies and say what each changed setting did.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Each photo-engraved layer of a checked photograph has a quality block: levels told apart, range, blocked shadows, lost highlights, reversals, mid-tone darkness, detail kept at 0.2/0.4/0.8 mm, grain
- [ ] On a variations plate the copies are ranked by the score and the best one is named
- [ ] For every setting changed on the plate the conclusion states its measured effect on darkness, range and detail (e.g. «мощность +20 %: темнее на 6 L*, тени забиты до 0,4»)
- [ ] The conclusions appear on the experiment page and in «Выводы» in Russian
- [ ] Earlier photographs get the block too (backfilled), without measuring the photograph again where the profile is enough
