## Statement

The system shall offer, on the picture of a photo work and of a photo zone of a vector work, a button that makes a photo-variations plate of that picture as its file engraves it, at its own settings and at one change each, and opens the new experiment.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The first copy has the work's (or zone's) kind, power (Min and Max in grayscale), speed, frequency, pulse width, line interval and dot width correction
- [ ] The copies show the part of the picture with the most tone at the size the work engraves it, not the whole picture shrunk
- [ ] The plate records which work or zone it was made from
- [ ] A work pinned to a laboratory release, or a laser with another lens than the work's, is refused with the reason
- [ ] The button says what the eight copies change before it is pressed
