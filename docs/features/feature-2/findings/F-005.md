## Finding

Representing the entered content does not establish whether the physical QR code is usable after engraving. The specification leaves open whether the product must assess the code's suitability at the chosen physical size and warn the operator, or merely generate its geometry.

> The generated code represents the content entered for its selected type.

## Possible interpretations

- Check the code at its placed physical size and warn when engraving or scanning may be unreliable.
- Generate the code without a suitability check and leave physical validation entirely to the operator.

## Resolution

Проверять QR-код при выбранном физическом размере и предупреждать оператора, если его размер или свободное поле не соответствуют установленным порогам. Разрешить продолжить работу. — see DEC-038.
