## Finding

Separate preparation is required, but its deliverable and the preservation of each side's geometry and assigned settings are not specified. The existing project requires LightBurn-loadable plans, so the card workflow needs an explicit outcome that connects preparation to engraving.

> The operator can prepare each side of a card separately for non-colour engraving.

## Possible interpretations

- Each side is exported separately as a LightBurn project with its geometry and assigned settings.
- Preparation only produces an internal plan, leaving transfer to the engraving software unspecified.

## Resolution

Экспортировать каждую сторону в отдельный проект LightBurn, готовый к открытию и проверке оператором. — see DEC-035.
