## Finding

The specification does not state whether an editable card work must survive closing or restarting the application. Without that outcome, a two-sided composition may be implemented as a temporary session even though the operator needs to return to the work.

> The operator can switch between the two sides and continue editing each side.

## Possible interpretations

- Save the complete editable work, including both sides and their settings, for later reopening.
- Keep the design only for the current session and retain exported engraving files.

## Resolution

Сохранять обе стороны и их настройки, чтобы после закрытия или перезапуска приложения продолжить редактирование. — see DEC-036.
