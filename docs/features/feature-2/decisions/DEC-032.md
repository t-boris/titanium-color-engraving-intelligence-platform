## Context

A titanium card can have a front and a back.

## Alternatives

1. Design one side per work.

## Decision

Design both sides in one card work and prepare each side separately for engraving.

## Reason

The user selected a two-sided workflow.

## Consequences

Each side needs its own design and engraving preparation within the same card work.
