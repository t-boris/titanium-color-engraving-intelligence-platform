## Context

The existing requirement allows the operator to switch between and edit both sides, but does not specify whether the editable work survives closing or restarting the application.

## Alternatives

1. Persist both editable designs and their dimensions without requiring engraving settings to be saved.
2. Keep the design only for the current session and retain exported engraving files.

## Decision

Persist the complete editable card work, including both sides, their editable elements, dimensions, and engraving settings, so the operator can reopen it and continue editing after closing or restarting the application.

## Reason

The team chose full persistence to let the operator return to the complete card work without recreating either design or re-entering its engraving settings.

## Consequences

REQ-001 must require saving and reopening the complete editable card work, including both sides, their elements, dimensions, and engraving settings. Acceptance criteria must verify that editing can continue after closing or restarting the application.
