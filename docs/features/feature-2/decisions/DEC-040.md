## Context

DEC-038 requires a module-size and a quiet-zone check at the placed physical size, with separately defined thresholds, and leaves the values open. The operator picked the recommended values when asked (2026-11-06). The workshop's own registration QR uses 0.25 mm modules for camera reading, which a phone may not manage.

## Alternatives

1. 4 modules and 0.30 mm (fewer warnings, harder for a phone to read).
2. 4 modules and 0.50 mm (more conservative, more cards get a warning).

## Decision

Warn when a QR code's quiet zone is smaller than 4 modules or its module size is smaller than 0.40 mm at the placed size. Both thresholds are kept in one place in the code and checked independently; either warning is non-blocking.

## Reason

4 modules is the quiet zone of ISO/IEC 18004; 0.40 mm is the operator-approved module limit for phone scanning of engraved metal.

## Consequences

The module size is the placed symbol width divided by its module count. The quiet zone is measured as the clear distance from the symbol to the nearest other engraved element on the same side or to the card edge, in modules. Passing both checks does not guarantee that the engraved code scans.
