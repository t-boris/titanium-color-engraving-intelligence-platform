## Context

REQ-005 requires QR codes to represent the entered content, but does not address whether a code placed on a physical card can be engraved and scanned reliably.

## Alternatives

1. Block engraving preparation until the QR code's module size and quiet zone meet established thresholds.
2. Do not assess physical suitability automatically; leave validation to the operator.

## Decision

Check each QR code's module size and quiet zone at its placed physical size. Warn the operator when either does not meet the established threshold, while allowing work and engraving preparation to continue.

## Reason

The selected approach makes physical risks visible while preserving the operator's ability to proceed.

## Consequences

Extend REQ-005 with a check of module size and quiet zone at the placed physical size, a warning when either falls below its threshold, and acceptance criteria confirming that the operator can continue. Define the thresholds separately. Passing the check does not guarantee that the engraved code will scan.
