## Context

The available blanks are estimated to be 85 × 53 or 85 × 54 mm.

## Alternatives

1. Fix one size after measuring the available blanks.

## Decision

Offer 85 × 53 mm and 85 × 54 mm presets and allow a custom size.

## Reason

The user accepted support for both estimated sizes and custom blanks.

## Consequences

The actual blank dimensions remain to be measured by the operator.
