## Statement

The system shall smooth every shared border of a photo vector work by a strength the operator sets in millimetres, store each border once as cubic Béziers and show and export exactly those curves.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The field "Сглаживание, мм" is the strength of the smoothing: at 0 the borders are as traced, at 0.8 mm the portrait's borders are visibly smooth; a larger value never makes a border more angular
- [ ] Both regions next to a border use the same smoothed curve: uncovered and doubly covered area stay below 0.5 % each on the portrait at 0.8 mm, and holes survive
- [ ] Inside a border the tangent is continuous; corners and points where three or more regions meet stay corners (tested on fixtures with a curve, a corner and a junction)
- [ ] The preview draws the curves and the LightBurn file carries them as B primitives; the file read back by the application's own reader lies within 0.01 mm of the previewed curve
- [ ] A work made before keeps its straight segments and opens, edits and exports as before until it is rebuilt
