## Statement

The system shall smooth every shared border of a photo vector work by a strength the operator sets with a slider in millimetres, redraw the whole picture at every position without touching regions, colours or variants, and show and export exactly those cubic Bézier curves.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The slider "Гладкость линий, мм" (0 to 2) is the strength of the smoothing: at 0 the borders are as traced, at 0.8 mm the portrait's borders are visibly smooth; a larger value never makes a border more angular
- [x] Both regions next to a border use the same smoothed curve: uncovered and doubly covered area stay below 0.5 % each on the portrait at 0.8 mm, and holes survive
- [x] Inside a border the tangent is continuous; corners and points where three or more regions meet stay corners (tested on fixtures with a curve, a corner and a junction)
- [x] The preview draws the curves and the LightBurn file carries them as B primitives; the file read back by the application's own reader lies within 0.01 mm of the previewed curve
- [x] A work made before keeps its straight segments and opens, edits and exports as before until the slider is moved
- [x] Moving the slider redraws the borders of the whole picture and nothing else: regions, colours, variants and the undo steps stay as they were, and a burst of movements sends only the last position
