## Statement

The system shall build the palette of a photo work from the measured fill colours of its laser and lens on every titanium record of the same family and grade as the work's record, whatever the plate's name, size, thickness or surface, so that the number of colours it can offer does not depend on which plate the picture is made on.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A colour measured on another plate of the same family and grade is in the palette of the work, in the colour table and in the editor
- [ ] A recipe measured on several plates is one entry with the mean colour of all its engravings and the id it has on the work's own plate, so saved colourings and variants still restore
- [ ] A plate of another grade or family, and a colour measured without a titanium record, are not in the palette; a record with no grade is only itself
- [ ] On the operator's data the palette of the work has more than 29 colours and the mode 'Как можно больше разных цветов' fills 29 of 29 layers
- [ ] The form, the colour tab and the editor say that the colours are of this grade of titanium from any plate
