## Statement

The system shall offer an automatic colouring mode that gives different source colours different titanium colours as far as the measured palette of the work's laser, lens and titanium reaches, keeping the order of light and dark of the source colours and the operator's own choices.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] With no more source colours than palette entries every source colour gets an entry of its own, and an entry never repeats; with more source colours every palette entry is used
- [ ] The lighter of two source colours never gets a darker entry than the other
- [ ] Among all assignments that keep the order and the difference the one with the least pixel-weighted delta E is chosen (checked against brute force), and among equal ones the faster
- [ ] Colours the operator kept stay as chosen and their entries are not given to other colours while other entries remain
- [ ] The mode is a button 'Как можно больше разных цветов' beside the other four in the colouring tab, the work list names it, and a variant saved with it is restored as it was
- [ ] The other four modes and the first colouring of a new split are unchanged
