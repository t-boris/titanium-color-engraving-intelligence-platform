## Statement

The system shall show every saved vector work on the screen "Векторная работа" with a thumbnail of its coloured result and the choices made in it, and shall let the operator open it, make an independent copy to redo it from, or delete it after a question.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] GET /api/vector lists the vector works, newest first, without deleted ones and without other plan kinds, with name, dates, size, algorithm, colours, regions, layers, colouring mode, AI style and own text, number of variants and smoothing
- [x] GET /api/vector/{id}/thumb.png is a PNG of at most 360 px drawn in the titanium colours of the current colouring; it changes after a recolouring and is cached with an ETag
- [x] The screen opens with a card per work: thumbnail, facts and the buttons Открыть, Сделать копию and Удалить; with no works it offers the first one; the creation form is at 'Новая работа'
- [x] POST /api/vector/{id}/copy makes a full independent duplicate (own image and document files, colouring, variants and settings kept, undo, redo and exports reset, history says it is a copy), named '<name> (копия)' unless given a name; deleting the original leaves the copy openable
- [x] Deleting from the list asks first, names the work, says it cannot be brought back and points to the copy; nothing is deleted before the answer
- [x] The list and the copy are covered by tests (tests/test_vector_list.py, tests/test_vector_workspace_ui.py)
