## Context

I-7 asks that after colouring every connected group of included regions with the same recipe becomes one region without inner seams, and that the recolouring and its merges are one undoable step. Merging in the stored split would change the region ids the colouring, the variants, the undo history, the inscriptions and the photo zones refer to, and painting one of the merged regions again would have no border to give back.

## Alternatives

1. Merge the regions in the stored split after every recolouring (a new split version per colouring): every variant and undo step would carry its own split, and repainting a part of a merged area would need the old borders back
2. Leave every region separate in the file: LightBurn fills each one with its own scan pieces and the borders between same-recipe neighbours stay as seams of the scan

## Decision

The stored split, the editor, the undo steps and the variants keep every region. Where the work is written (the LightBurn file of a photograph work), every set of regions on one layer connected through shared borders is merged into one closed shape by segment.merge_groups in one pass: the borders between them are dropped, holes and pinch points are kept, a touch at a corner only joins nothing, regions cut by an inscription and photo-zone regions are not joined, and other layers are untouched. The preview already draws no seam (each region's hairline is in its own colour).

## Reason

The file gets the one shape per group the requirement asks for, while the colouring stays a pure function of the split: undoing or repainting gives the borders back by construction, and the variants, inscriptions and zones keep the region ids they refer to.

## Consequences

The joined geometry is built at export from the memoised geometry of the work; a test reads the file back: one shape per group with its holes, other layers as they were, gaps and overlaps below 0.5 %, undo restores the separate shapes. The time estimate keeps the layer's area (unchanged by the join).
