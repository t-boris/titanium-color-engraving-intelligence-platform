## Context

closest takes for each source colour the nearest entry, so neighbouring source colours share a layer (7 layers, mean delta E 18.47 on the operator's work); tonality gave 6 (19.04), fewer_layers 7, random 13 (58.57). None aims at many different layers. Layers cost engraving time but give the picture more tones.

## Alternatives

1. Make the new behaviour the default of every new split (not chosen: it lowers colour accuracy and lengthens every work)
2. Only tell the operator that the palette has 18 colours (not chosen)
3. Widen the palette with colours predicted by the laser model (not chosen: unmeasured colours; a separate decision)

## Decision

Mode `most_colours` is added beside closest, tonality, random and fewer_layers; the others are unchanged and 'Ближе к оригиналу' stays the first colouring of a new split. The free source colours sorted by L* take entries sorted by L*: with no more source colours than palette entries each gets an entry of its own, rising in L*; with more, every entry takes a run of neighbouring source colours. Dynamic programming gives the least pixel-weighted delta E, then the least pixel-weighted time. The entries of colours the operator kept are not offered to the other colours while others remain. The palette itself is not widened: only measured colours are used.

## Reason

Operator, 2026-10-19, about a work of 29 colours that used 7 layers of 29: 'Но если я прошу 29 цветов - выбирай 29 цветов'. The palette of the work's laser, lens and titanium has 18 measured colours (all measured against the clean titanium of their photographs, one engraving each; 14 more recipes are grey steps of image layers), so 29 different titanium colours cannot exist; 'closest' merged 29 source colours into 7. Offered a new mode, a new default, or only a message, the operator chose the new mode.

## Consequences

One more button and label in the colouring tab and in the work list; one more mode in the API (POST /api/vector/{id}/colouring {op: assign, mode: most_colours}) and in /api/vector-palette/assign; the error text that lists the modes now lists five. On the operator's work the mode uses every one of the 18 colours instead of 7; the mean delta E rises. A variant saved with this mode is restored as it was. Tests in tests/test_palette.py (5, one against brute force) and tests/test_vector_work.py.
