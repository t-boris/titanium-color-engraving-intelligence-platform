## Context

I-6 and DEC-079: Artistic must give distinct seeded hue arrangements within a chosen measured palette subset while keeping the source lightness order for automatic colours, and must handle a small palette and kept choices as Tonality does. The AI styles already have one named «Артистично», so the non-AI mode needs its own name.

## Alternatives

1. A random hue rotation of the whole picture (titanium has gaps in its hues, so most rotations land on the same few entries)
2. Groups chosen colour by colour by the operator (slow, and the editor already paints by hand)

## Decision

«Художественно» takes one of four groups: all colours, warm (hue 15..110°), cool (160..330°) — the ranges and the chroma of 8 from which a colour has a hue are those of the photo work's colouring — or bright (the more chromatic half of the coloured entries). The seed gives every source colour a target hue drawn from the hues of the group's entries; the entries are laid in lightness order by the Tonality dynamic programme with a cost of |ΔL*| plus 0.2 L* per degree between the entry's hue and the target (a grey entry counts 90°). Each press takes the next seed, as Random does; the variant records the seed and the group. An empty group is refused with its name; a group smaller than the colours shares its entries in lightness order; kept choices stay as in Tonality.

## Reason

Targets drawn from the group's own hues always land on colours that exist, so every seed arranges the hues anew, and the lightness order is kept by the same code that keeps it in Tonality.

## Consequences

The colour step gets «Контраст соседей» beside the other modes and «Художественно» with a group selector. Tests: lightness order kept, at least four of five seeds differ and a seed repeats itself, every chosen entry is in the group, a small group shares, an empty group is refused.
