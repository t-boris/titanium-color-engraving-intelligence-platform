## Context

The vector work screen had three cards: "1. Разбиение" (settings, a picture for marking important areas), "2. Цвета" (palette, automatic modes, the editor, variants) and "3. Файл LightBurn". The operator said the split is not a section of its own: "это часть, там где цвета". Keep the variants of the colouring and the LightBurn file; merge the first and the second into one. This is a step towards DEC-078's single workspace, not the whole of it.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the split as its own card and only collapse it
2. Put the split settings in the editor's side panel

## Decision

One card, "1. Цвета и разбиение": the split block on top (algorithm, number of colours, detail, smoothing, face search and "Разбить заново" always in view, then the counts), with the picture for marking important areas folded into a block of its own under it; below it the colours (palette, tolerance, the automatic modes, the editor, the table by colour) and the variants of the colouring; then "2. Файл LightBurn" as its own card. The addresses of the screen do not change.

## Reason

The operator's instruction. The controls stay visible so that the choice of algorithm is not hidden, while the second picture of the same photograph, used only to mark areas, no longer lengthens the page.

## Consequences

The headings are renumbered (the file is now "2."). The split block is rebuilt with each split version, as the split card was; an edit of the form not yet applied is lost when a merge makes a new version. The rest of DEC-078 (tools around a central picture, import and export in a toolbar) is not built.
