## Context

0.7.0 offered SLIC and SEEDS. The operator, looking at the screen: "Алгоритмы не делают разницу". Measured on the portrait of work 22 (28 colours, coarse detail): SLIC 290 regions and SEEDS 267 regions give the same patches (DEC-080's own table: mean ΔE 7.35 vs 7.47). Candidates that differ in kind were put through the same seam (segment._ALGORITHM): a fine grid of 2 px cells (275 regions, follows the shading: brows, lips, the highlight on the nose; 8 s) and Felzenszwalb graph segmentation (173 regions at scale 150: flat planes and ribbons along features, 1.6 s; above scale 500 a face collapses into one region).

## Alternatives

1. Keep only SLIC and SEEDS and describe them better
2. Replace SEEDS by the two new methods
3. Offer only Mosaic and By tones

## Decision

Four methods in the list: "Мозаика" (SLIC, the default), "По тонам" (the fine grid: the colour classes alone decide the shapes), "Крупные плоскости" (graph segmentation, scale bounded so that a face never collapses) and SEEDS, kept at the operator's choice and described as close to the Mosaic. Each description says what the picture looks like and what the method costs in time. The stored name of a method stays its key; works made before keep the key they were made with, and a work without one is SLIC.

## Reason

The operator chose "the same three and SEEDS" among the options. The new methods are different in kind on real data, not in a metric: that is what the choice is for. SEEDS stays but is no longer sold as an alternative to the Mosaic.

## Consequences

DEC-080 is amended, not replaced: its measurements stand. "By tones" must be sped up before it is offered on a 1200 px picture (8 s now). Tests assert that the maps of the four methods differ on a fixture, so that a choice that changes nothing cannot be shipped again.

## Result (2026-10-19)

Built under the keys `slic` ("Мозаика", default), `tonal` ("По тонам"), `planes` ("Крупные плоскости") and `seeds`. On the portrait of work 22 (1200 px, 28 colours, coarse detail, faces off / on): SLIC 290 / 366 regions in 1.5 / 2.7 s, tonal 275 / 335 in 3.3 / 3.9 s (8.6 s before: the clustering now merges superpixel colours on a grid of at most 20 000 points, a coarser step up to 2 Lab units only for a fine grid; the existing methods are unchanged), planes 173 / 226 in 1.4 / 2.2 s, SEEDS 267 / 348 in 1.1 / 1.9 s. The graph scale is fixed at 150: a smooth shaded disc keeps all 8 colours asked for, at 2000 it collapses to 3 (tested). With faces on, planes shows the outline of an eye or lip area where its finer pieces meet a large plane; this is the method's cost and the description says that faces, eyes and lips stay finer.
