## Context

I-3 (overview criterion 3): each detected or marked eye has its own detail and an editable boundary, a missing eye can be marked, and changing one eye keeps the geometry outside it. Before, all fine areas shared one level (0.35 of the coarse superpixel) and were sub-segmented as one run over a box spanning them all, and the k-means of every split started anew, so a change in one eye moved colour borders across the whole picture (on a test picture a fifth of the pixels outside the eye changed colour).

## Alternatives

1. Keep one shared fine level and let the operator only move boundaries (no per-eye control)
2. Re-split only the changed eye's box and paste it into the old document (two topologies to stitch: arcs, junctions and region ids across the seam)
3. Fix the k-means seed only (the clustering still moves with the changed superpixels' weights)

## Decision

An eye, the lips or a marked area may carry a detail: «как у лица» 0.6, «мелко» 0.35 (default), «очень мелко» 0.2 or «максимально» 0.12 of the coarse superpixel (faces.DETAILS); a face has none. Every such area is sub-segmented in its own box at its own size, coarser first so that the finer wins where two overlap, and specks inside it merge below its own superpixel's area. The split keeps the operator's changes to detected areas as `adjusted` ({number, detail, ellipse or polygon}), applied to the areas as found; marked areas carry a kind (eye, lips, other) and a detail. The document stores its colour centres and every superpixel takes the nearest centre; a new split that changes only the detail, boundary or kind of areas (same colours, algorithm, density, faces, removed and number of marked areas) takes the old centres instead of clustering anew, so the borders and colours outside the changed area stay. Any other change clusters anew, as before. On the split screen each area row has a detail select, «Граница» (drag inside to move it, beside it to draw it again) and «Как найдено»; a marked area has a kind select.

## Reason

The operator controls eyes one by one without the rest of the picture changing under them, with one split algorithm and no stitching of two documents.

## Consequences

Splits made from 0.28.0 on differ slightly from earlier ones of the same settings: fine areas are split one by one, and the superpixels' colours are assigned by the nearest centre to their exact mean. Works split before have no centres, so their first area change clusters anew. Colouring, variants and undo still reset on «Разбить заново» (I-8). Tests: on a smooth picture an eye set to «максимально» gets more regions while at least 99.5 % of the pixels outside it keep their colour and borders (100 % measured; 83–87 % without the kept centres); the API keeps `adjusted`, keeps the centres for area-only changes and clusters anew otherwise; the screen sends detail, moved boundary and a marked eye.
