## Context

I-2 asks for a global density that can be lowered or raised apart from the number of colours, with a preview of the region count. The split had three levels of detail (coarse, medium, fine), each a pair of numbers: superpixels along the longer side (70, 100, 150) and the least region as a share of the picture (1/1500, 1/3000, 1/7500).

## Alternatives

1. More named levels (still steps, and no count before the split)
2. A target number of regions that the split searches for (a split per try, several seconds each)
3. Density moving the superpixels only, with the least region fixed (low densities kept tiny regions in flat areas)

## Decision

The split keeps a density: a whole number of coarse superpixels along the picture's longer side, from 30 to 250. The least region follows it along straight lines through the three levels on logarithmic scales (log share against log density), and beyond the ends along the nearest segment, so each level is exactly the density it was (70, 100, 150) and works split before keep their regions. A request may still name a level, which becomes its density. POST /api/vector/{id}/split/preview runs the split with the settings «Разбить заново» would send, the work unchanged, and answers the number of regions and colours and the least region; each answer is memoised for its picture and settings. The split screen has a slider in steps of 5 that asks for the count when it stops (one request at a time, the last position), and again when the colours, algorithm, faces or areas change.

## Reason

One number the operator can move freely in both directions, a count shown before committing to a rebuild that resets the colouring, and nothing changes for existing works.

## Consequences

A preview costs one split (about a second on a 1200 px portrait, longer for «По тонам»); moving back to a position seen before is answered from memory. The creation form keeps the three levels. Tests: the levels are exact, the least region falls steadily with density, a detailed picture gives fewer regions at 40 than at 200 with the same 5 colours, the preview equals the split that follows, bad densities are refused.
