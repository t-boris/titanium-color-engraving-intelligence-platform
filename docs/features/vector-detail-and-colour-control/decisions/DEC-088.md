## Context

Until now (DEC-073) the palette of a photo work held the measured colours of its laser, lens and the work's own titanium record only. The operator's three titanium records are two plates of Grade 5 (3 mm and 2 mm) and one 1 mm plate (typed 'Garde 2'); the work's own record had 18 colours, the other two 472 and 17. Same laser and lens throughout. The size and thickness of a plate do not change how titanium colours under the laser as far as the operator is concerned.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the work's own record only (DEC-073; not chosen: 18 colours cannot give 29 layers)
2. Every titanium record whatever its grade (not chosen: the operator names grade as the material)
3. Merge near-duplicate recipes into new ids (not chosen: saved colourings reference the old ids)
4. Widen the palette with colours the laser model predicts (not chosen here; a separate decision)

## Decision

palette.vector_palette takes the points of every titanium record with the same family and the same grade as the work's record (case and spacing ignored; a record with no grade is only itself; a colour measured without a titanium record is in no palette). Names, thickness, surface and batch are not compared. A recipe (same settings, setup, basis, task) measured on several plates is one entry whose colour is the mean of all its independent engravings; its group, hence its id, is the one it has on the work's own record when it was measured there, else the first by name, so every colouring and variant already saved keeps its entry ids. Laser and lens are still matched exactly. The material of the work is still chosen in the form; the palette preview and the API keep the material_id parameter.

## Reason

Operator, 2026-10-20: 'Какая разница на какой плашке титана сделана картина - выбирай из всех титановых пластинах'; 'Не обращай внимание на размеры - главное материал (titan + grade)'; 'Пусть все плашки будут Grade 5'. On the operator's data the palette of work 22 grows from 18 to 505 colours, so a request for 29 colours can be met (mode most_colours reaches 29 of 29 layers).

## Consequences

The operator's record 1 was changed from 'Garde 2' to 'Grade 5' (backup titanium-before-grade5-20261020T001540Z.sqlite), so all three records are one material. Works made on a plate of one grade see the colours of every plate of that grade; a grade with no other plate behaves as before. Texts of the form, the colour tab and the editor say 'титана этой марки (с любой плиты)'. Older fill/text/line works (vector_job.eligible) are unchanged. Tests in tests/test_palette.py (merged recipes, id stability, grade/family/no-grade cases) and tests/test_vector_work.py.
