## Context

The operator wants Vector Work to resemble Photoshop or Adobe Illustrator, with the image central and tools arranged around it, reducing scrolling.

## Alternatives

1. Retain the scrolling workflow with separate segmentation and colouring screens.
2. Use a unified canvas workspace with surrounding tools and contextual panels.

## Decision

Use a unified, canvas-centred Vector Work workspace. Keep the image as the primary surface, with surrounding tools, contextual settings, and accessible import/export actions. Primary editing actions must remain accessible without scrolling the entire page; panels may scroll internally when needed.

## Reason

Keep the image visible while configuring, colouring, and reviewing vector work.
