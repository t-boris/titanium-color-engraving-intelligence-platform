## Context

DEC-087 added a fifth button, 'Как можно больше разных цветов', beside the four modes of DEC-074; 'closest' merged 29 source colours into 7 layers even when the palette (DEC-088: 505 colours) had room for 29. The operator, 2026-10-20: 'Какая кнопка как можно больше цветов? Не нужна такая кнопка. цветов всегда как можно больше и ограничено количеством в установках'; on the question what to do with the other modes they chose 'Разные цвета везде, «Меньше слоёв» убрать'.

## Alternatives

1. Keep the fifth button (not chosen: the operator does not want it)
2. Distinct colours in the first colouring and 'closest' only, other modes as before (not chosen)
3. Keep 'Меньше слоёв' as the explicit opposite (not chosen: the operator asked to remove it)

## Decision

The number of layers of a photo work is the number of colours set in the split settings (at most 29, and at most the entries of the palette). closest: the one-to-one assignment of least pixel-weighted cost (scipy linear_sum_assignment, a declared dependency), where a delta E within the tolerance costs the tolerance and the faster entry is cheaper, so a colour no other wants still takes the fastest entry within the tolerance, else the nearest; with more colours than entries every entry is used once and the rest take their nearest. tonality: the lightness order with strictly rising entries (dynamic programming, least delta E, then time); with more colours than entries, runs of neighbours share an entry and every entry is used; with kept choices the order is kept around them. random: as before (own entry while the palette has enough), and every entry used when it has not. The entries the operator kept are not offered to other colours while others remain. fewer_layers and most_colours are removed from the API (error text lists closest, tonality or random) and the buttons; a colouring saved with fewer_layers keeps its name in the variant list and, if recomputed, is made by closest. The AI colouring is a separate way and is unchanged.

## Reason

The operator wants as many colours as they set, always, and no extra button. Measured on a copy of their data: closest 29 of 29 layers (mean delta E 6.97, against 20 layers and 6.16 before), tonality 29 (7.84), random 29.

## Consequences

Supersedes DEC-087 and amends DEC-074 and DEC-075 (the tolerance rule now yields to distinctness). A colour that nobody else wants is unchanged; colours that used to share a layer now take neighbouring entries, so the engraving time and the mean delta E rise a little and the layers grow. Tests in tests/test_palette.py (brute-force optimum for closest and tonality, own entry in every mode, every entry used, kept entries) and tests/test_vector_work.py.
