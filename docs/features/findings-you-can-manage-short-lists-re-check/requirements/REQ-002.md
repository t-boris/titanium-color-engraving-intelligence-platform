## Statement

The system shall show the findings of a run as a compact list that opens on the findings needing attention, with state and kind filters with counts, sorting and paging, the photo-engraving conclusions on their own tab, and each finding's full details under its row.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] The screen opens on «Требуют внимания»; chips «Требуют внимания · Учтённые · Скрытые · Все» and one chip per kind show their counts
- [x] Sorting: important first (pinned, then new or changed, then largest deviation), newest, largest ΔE, by laser and titanium
- [x] At most 20 rows at a time, «Показать ещё» for the rest; the server returns one page and the counts
- [x] A row shows pin, kind, a one-line statement, laser and titanium, ΔE, «новое» or «изменился»; clicking it opens the full finding (basis, samples, uncertainty, what it compares, evidence links, history, «Предложить эксперимент»)
- [x] The run, its inputs, rule warnings and the schedule take one line with a fold instead of two cards
- [x] Photo-engraving conclusions are on the tab «Фотогравировка», one row per laser and titanium, details on click
- [x] `#/findings?finding=<id>` opens that finding; the designer's «вывод №» links use it
