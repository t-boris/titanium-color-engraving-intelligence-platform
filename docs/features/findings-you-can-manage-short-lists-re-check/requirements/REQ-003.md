## Statement

The system shall let the operator acknowledge, hide, reopen, annotate and pin a finding, keep these marks across analysis runs, and bring a hidden or acknowledged finding back to attention when it changes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Each row has «Учтено», «Скрыть» («Вернуть» when hidden or acknowledged), «Заметка» and «Закрепить»
- [x] A mark is kept by the finding's key and holds in the next runs while the finding is the same
- [x] A hidden or acknowledged finding returns to «Требуют внимания», labelled «изменился», when it rests on a new engraving or photograph, its kind or sub-kind changes, or its ΔE moves by 1.0 or more
- [x] Pinned findings come first in every sort; a note shows on the row and in the details
- [x] Re-measuring a photograph keeps the marks of its anomalies (the key follows the photograph and layer)
- [x] Every mark change is written to the audit; earlier runs are not changed
