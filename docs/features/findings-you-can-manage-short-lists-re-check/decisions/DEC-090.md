## Context

Screens render long lists in their own way: full cards, tile grids, plain tables; only the font window has paging, only the palette picker sorting. The operator: avoid long lists everywhere.

## Alternatives

1. Fix each screen separately (the same problem returns with the next screen)
2. Inner scroll panels only (the list stays endless, nothing becomes manageable)

## Decision

Every list that can grow past one screen uses one helper, `App.list` (`ui/list.js`): one line per item, details opened under the row on click, optional search, filter chips with counts, a sort choice, «показано N из M» and «Показать ещё» in pages (20 by default, 10 for backups, 30 in editor panels), and per-row actions. Lists of up to about ten items (lasers, lenses, materials, rigs, documents) stay as they are.

## Reason

One behaviour to learn, one place to test, and new screens get it for free.

## Consequences

Every screen that could grow past a page was moved to `App.list` in 0.34.0 (colour model, experiments and the laser page, experiment result and work composition, analysis, ingest review, workshop backups and recovery copies, laboratories and the cloud, designer sets, plan, vector work lists, grey scales). A field, box or link inside a line works by itself and does not open the line; `rowClass` marks a line (an excluded colour dimmed); `open(key)` lets a row action open its line (the note of a finding). The vector editor's palette of squares is not a list of lines but follows the same rule: the 60 nearest colours, then «Показать ещё».
